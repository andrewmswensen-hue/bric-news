title: "HUD Final Rule Requires Citizenship Proof From Every Subsidized Housing Member"
summary: "The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development issued a final rule on Nov. 10 requiring every member of a household receiving federal rental assistance, including Section 8 vouchers and Section 9 public housing, to verify citizenship or an immigration status eligible under Section 214 of federal law. The rule takes effect Dec. 14. Previously, a household could include an ineligible member as part of a mixed-status household by paying a higher, prorated rent share."
detail: "HUD's rule, issued Nov. 10 and effective Dec. 14, tightens eligibility for federal rental assistance programs including Section 8 housing choice vouchers and Section 9 public housing. Under current practice, a household can include a member without eligible immigration status as part of a mixed-status household, paying a higher, prorated share of rent to cover that member. The new rule requires every household member to document citizenship or an immigration status eligible under Section 214 of federal law, a standard advocates say some lawfully present immigrants may not meet. Public housing authorities nationwide administer Section 8 vouchers and public housing, so the verification requirement applies wherever those programs operate, not only in the New York City cases detailed in current coverage. Advocacy groups told City Limits they intend to challenge the rule's implementation. The rule does not change private-market landlord obligations directly, but it changes how local public housing authorities process voucher eligibility and renewals for mixed-status households."
why_it_matters: "Landlords in Franklin County who accept Section 8 vouchers should expect the local public housing authority to begin applying this verification standard around Dec. 14, which could mean some mixed-status tenant households losing all or part of their rent subsidy and falling behind on the portion they pay directly."
