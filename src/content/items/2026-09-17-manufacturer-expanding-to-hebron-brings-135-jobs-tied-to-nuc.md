title: "Manufacturer expanding to Hebron brings 135 jobs tied to nuclear supply chain"
summary: "Westerman Inc., an Ohio manufacturer serving the energy and nuclear sectors, is opening a second facility in Hebron, bringing 135 jobs and about $8.42 million in new annual payroll to Licking County, GROW Licking County said. The company will invest roughly $39.4 million to redevelop a 100,000-square-foot building on John Alford Parkway, vacant about two years since previously housing Ohio Metal Technologies. The Hebron site will support expansion tied to the Centrus Energy American Centrifuge Plant in Piketon, part of a planned large-scale data center project."
detail: "Westerman Inc., currently based in Bremen in Fairfield County, manufactures equipment for the oil, natural gas and nuclear manufacturing sectors. The company is expanding both its existing Bremen location and opening a new Hebron facility to meet growing energy-sector demand, according to GROW Licking County executive director Cameron Garczyk. The Hebron site will occupy a 100,000-square-foot building that has been vacant roughly two years since previously housing Ohio Metal Technologies.\n\nThe expansion is tied in part to growth in nuclear-adjacent manufacturing: Garczyk said the Hebron site will support work connected to Centrus Energy's American Centrifuge Plant in Piketon, which is planned to help power what has been described as the largest data center project in the world. Available positions include operations, project management and quality supervisor roles alongside specialized labor such as machinists, welders and quality inspectors.\n\nThe announcement follows the closures of two other major Hebron employers, Freepoint Eco-Systems and Schwebel Baking Company, within the past several months. Hebron Mayor Valerie Mockus said the manufacturing base helps fund the village's tax revenue beyond its resident population. Licking County ranked first nationally for job growth between March 2025 and March 2026, according to GROW Licking County, citing a 3.6% employment increase compared with a 0.2% decline statewide over the same period."
