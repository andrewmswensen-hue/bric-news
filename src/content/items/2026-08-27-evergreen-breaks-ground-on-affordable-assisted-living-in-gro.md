title: "Evergreen breaks ground on affordable assisted living in Grove City and on Westerville Road"
summary: "Chicago-based Evergreen Real Estate Group has broken ground on three affordable assisted living communities in Ohio, two of them in the Columbus metro: Green Oaks of Grove City at 3000 Holt Road and Green Oaks of Walnut Creek at 4000 Westerville Road. A third is in Fairborn near Dayton. Each is a 120-unit building with 67 studio and 53 one-bedroom apartments for seniors. Completion is expected in fall and winter of 2027. Evergreen is working with the Ohio Housing Finance Agency and the state Medicaid, Aging and Health departments."
detail: "Each Green Oaks community is licensed as a residential care facility by the Ohio Department of Health and approved as a Medicaid assisted living service provider through the Ohio Department of Aging, which is what lets the buildings serve residents at roughly half the cost of nursing home care. Gardant Management Solutions will operate them.\n\nUnits include a kitchenette with sink, refrigerator and microwave, with an option to upgrade to a full kitchen, a private bathroom with shower and grab bars, individual heating and air conditioning, and an emergency alert system. Each building has a commercial kitchen and dining room serving three meals daily, plus a fitness room, salon, game and activity rooms, computer stations and a library, with certified staff on site around the clock and transportation to local businesses.\n\nThe two Columbus-area sites sit in Grove City and along Westerville Road. Evergreen said the developments target seniors who need daily support but want a residential setting rather than institutional care."
