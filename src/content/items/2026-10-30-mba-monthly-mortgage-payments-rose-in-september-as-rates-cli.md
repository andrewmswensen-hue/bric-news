The affordability index rose 3.1% from September 2025, with higher readings indicating worse affordability, and conditions worsened in 39 states. FHA borrowers' median payment rose to $1,906 from $1,856 in August, and conventional borrowers' median payment climbed to $2,244 from $2,188. Payments on new home purchase applications rose even faster, up $93 to $2,307, a steeper increase than the broader purchase market.\n\nIdaho, Nevada, Rhode Island, Arizona and Tennessee recorded the worst affordability readings, while Louisiana, Connecticut, West Virginia, Alaska and Iowa recorded the best. The data reflects the financing conditions facing buyers nationally, including investors financing new acquisitions."
