title: "Downtown Commission approves 242-unit One Twenty Vine apartments in Arena District"
summary: "The Columbus Downtown Commission approved One Twenty Vine on Sept. 22, a seven-story, 242-unit apartment building planned for a gravel parking lot near Nationwide Arena and North Market. The project is by Hamilton Parker's land affiliate in partnership with Nationwide Realty Investors. The building would be residential only, with resident parking on the first floor. No construction timeline has been announced."
detail: "According to Columbus Navigator, the Downtown Commission approved One Twenty Vine at its Sept. 22 meeting. The project would replace a paid public parking lot on the block bounded by Vine, Spruce, Armstrong and Kilbourne streets, one block west of Park Street.\n\nThe building has a two-story base with five stories above it and a parapet just over 83 feet. It is residential only, with no retail. The ground floor holds a leasing office, fitness center and resident lounge, and parking is fully enclosed and reserved for residents. A skywalk would link the third floor to the Kilbourne Garage next door. The interior courtyard would include a pool, grills and a fireplace. The application combines nine parcels and portions of right-of-way, with 120 Vine St. as the future address.\n\nThe owner on the application is HP Land Development, an affiliate of the local building-supply company Hamilton Parker, which has owned the block for years. It is working with Nationwide Realty Investors, the Arena District developer. The same partnership built Two Twenty Vine, which opened with 124 apartments next to the Kilbourne Garage. The staff report notes that the lot is currently used for public paid parking and that the plans include no public parking. The city's project file shows no construction timeline yet."
