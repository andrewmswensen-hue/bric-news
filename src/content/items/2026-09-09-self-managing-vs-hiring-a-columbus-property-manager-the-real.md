title: "Self-Managing vs. Hiring a Columbus Property Manager: The Real Cost Math"
summary: "Flat-rate property management in Columbus runs $117 to $184 per unit per month, or roughly $1,529 to $2,333 a year on a $1,400 rental, according to RL Property Management. Self-managing costs less in fees but more in time, vacancy days and retail-priced repairs: a well-priced single-family rental typically leases in 14 to 21 days once rent-ready, and each vacant day on a $1,400 unit costs about $46. Columbus's new rental registry opens October 1 and costs $15 per unit annually with no exemption for single-family homes, adding one more filing to a self-managing owner's list."
detail: "RL Property Management prices flat-rate management in three tiers: Passive at $117 a month, Standard at $137, and Premium at $184, each with a different maintenance approval limit before the owner has to sign off ($1,500 on Passive, $750 on Standard, $350 on Premium). Standard adds HOA and condo association support; Premium adds further services. On a $1,400 monthly rental, that works out to roughly $1,529 to $2,333 a year, including a $125 accounting fee and no separate leasing fee.\n\nSelf-managing avoids that fee but shifts the cost into time and risk that rarely show up on a spreadsheet: vacancy days, retail (non-contractor) repair pricing, and compliance exposure. A well-priced Columbus single-family rental typically leases in 14 to 21 days once it is rent-ready, and each day a $1,400 unit sits vacant costs about $46, so a saved week of vacancy is worth roughly $322. Filing a possession action in Franklin County costs $149 as of August 1, 2026, and runs about $448 all in when handled through RL Property Management, though the larger cost in an eviction is typically the weeks of lost rent rather than the filing fee itself.\n\nColumbus's new rental registry, open for filing October 1 through December 31, 2026, adds a $15-per-unit annual fee with no exemption for single-family homes and no phase-in based on portfolio size, which self-managing owners will need to track themselves."
why_it_matters: "For a Columbus owner deciding whether to self-manage, the new October 1 rental registry filing is one more task professional management already absorbs, and the sharper comparison point is the $322 a saved week of vacancy is worth, not just the monthly management fee."
