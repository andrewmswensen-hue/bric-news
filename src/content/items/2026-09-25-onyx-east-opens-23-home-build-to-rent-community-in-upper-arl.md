summary: "Onyx+East opened Haven, a 23-home single-family build-to-rent community at 3720 Henderson Road in Upper Arlington, with five homes available for immediate move-in and a sixth expected within the month. The four-bedroom homes exceed 2,000 square feet and include an attached two-car garage, private driveway and fenced backyard, with professional lawn care and snow removal included."
detail: "Haven's 23 detached single-family homes sit on Henderson Road in Upper Arlington, near Riverside Methodist Hospital, Ohio State University and nearby corporate offices, and within Upper Arlington and Dublin City school districts. Each four-bedroom home tops 2,000 square feet and comes with luxury vinyl plank flooring, stainless steel GE appliances, quartz countertops and a subway-tile backsplash, alongside a covered front porch and a two-car garage. Onyx+East is offering the first four renters a month of free rent as an opening promotion. The project adds to a growing national build-to-rent sector: about 63,000 single-family homes built specifically for rent began construction nationwide in the 12 months ending June 2026, 6.9 percent of all single-family starts, and national build-to-rent occupancy reached 92.6 percent in the second quarter of 2026. Onyx+East and industry data cited in the announcement point to Central Ohio's population and employment growth as a driver of demand, noting that Columbus estimates the region will need roughly 200,000 new housing units over the next decade."
