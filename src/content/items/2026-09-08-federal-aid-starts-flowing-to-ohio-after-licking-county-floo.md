title: "Federal aid starts flowing to Ohio after Licking County flooding"
summary: "FEMA approved $36 million in disaster recovery funding for Ohio, Michigan and Wisconsin two days after Gov. Mike DeWine requested a presidential major disaster declaration for severe storms and flooding that hit 23 Ohio counties, including Licking County, between August 10 and 23. Initial damage assessments found major damage to 806 properties and minor damage to 475 more statewide, with at least 50 properties damaged beyond repair. Trump has not yet issued the full major disaster declaration DeWine requested, so individual assistance programs remain unfulfilled."
detail: "DeWine's request, co-signed by Rep. Troy Balderson and Sens. Jon Husted and Bernie Moreno, told the White House the flooding \"exceeds the capabilities of the State of Ohio, local governments, and disaster relief organizations to provide comprehensive support.\" The money approved so far, $36 million across Ohio, Michigan and Wisconsin, comes through FEMA's Public Assistance and Hazard Mitigation Grant Program and the Building Resilient Infrastructure and Communities program, which fund local governments and infrastructure repair rather than individual households. DeWine's letter also asked Trump to activate FEMA's Individual Assistance Grant Program, which could provide direct grants to residents for temporary housing, home repairs and rental assistance, along with the SBA's Physical Damage Loan program; neither has been approved yet because Trump has not issued the full major disaster declaration. John Wieber, interim director of the Licking County Emergency Management agency, said a full declaration would open FEMA application programs for residents, though it would not make anyone whole. In Pataskala, flooding hit the Greenbriar Village mobile home park hard enough to cut power to residents in addition to the flood damage."
why_it_matters: "Landlords with flood-damaged rental property in the 23 affected counties, including Licking County, should track whether Trump issues the full major disaster declaration DeWine requested, since that step, not the infrastructure funding already approved, is what would open FEMA's individual assistance and SBA physical damage loan programs to property owners."
