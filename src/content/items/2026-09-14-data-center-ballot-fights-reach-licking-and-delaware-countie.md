detail: "The deadline to file charter amendment petitions for the November 3 ballot passed September 4, and voters in 18 Ohio municipalities and townships will now decide the future of data centers in their communities, according to the Ohio Capital Journal. Several of those communities, including Granville, Pataskala and Sunbury, sit within Licking and Delaware counties. Nine petitions statewide mirror a broader effort to ban data centers that draw more than 25 megawatts of power a month; other communities are pursuing narrower measures, such as zoning changes, recall petitions targeting officials who approved specific projects, or referendums striking down existing city agreements. Fourteen of the 18 communities with petitions filed have already received formal proposals for new data center projects. A Bowling Green State University poll found a majority of Ohioans support some form of data center moratorium, while opponents statewide cite rising utility bills, environmental concerns, and the pace of approvals with limited public input as their main grievances. Ohio has the fourth most operational data centers of any state, according to a Pew Research Center analysis, and developers are pursuing dozens more statewide."
