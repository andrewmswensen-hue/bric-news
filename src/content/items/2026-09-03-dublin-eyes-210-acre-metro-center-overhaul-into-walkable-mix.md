title: "Dublin eyes 210-acre Metro Center overhaul into walkable mixed-use district"
summary: "Dublin officials are pursuing a redevelopment of the roughly 210-acre Metro Center area, currently a suburban office park with about 90 acres of parking lots (7,700 spaces), into a walkable, mixed-use district with housing, retail, restaurants and expanded trail connections, according to WSYX (myfox28columbus.com). The plan follows the city's Metro Center Revitalization Plan, adopted in December 2024, which calls for proposed zoning changes and design guidelines to add green space, pedestrian connections and a stronger sense of place while keeping the area an employment center."
detail: "The Metro Center Revitalization Plan, adopted by Dublin City Council in December 2024, targets an area dominated by surface parking, more than 90 of the roughly 210 acres, for conversion into a mixed-use district combining commercial, residential and civic space. The plan includes proposed zoning changes and design guidelines meant to create a stronger sense of place, improve pedestrian connections and add green space, while preserving Metro Center's role as a major employment hub.\n\nOne identified focus area is the Frantz Road Gateway, where the plan calls for a connected trail corridor, linear park space and better transitions between Metro Center and the neighborhoods around it, tying the district into Dublin's existing trail network. A worker at a Metro Center business told WSYX the area is well suited to redevelopment given its scale and existing infrastructure.\n\nThe plan remains in the vision and zoning-framework stage; specific redevelopment projects would still need to move through Dublin's standard rezoning and development review process before construction. No timeline or cost estimate for the broader transformation was reported."
why_it_matters: "Roughly 210 acres, nearly half of it currently parking, is a large amount of underused land for a built-out suburb to rezone at once, and the density and use limits Dublin sets when it rewrites Metro Center's zoning code will apply before any individual redevelopment project reaches the city's approval process."
