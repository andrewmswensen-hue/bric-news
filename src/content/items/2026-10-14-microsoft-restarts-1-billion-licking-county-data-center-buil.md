title: "Microsoft restarts $1 billion Licking County data center buildout"
summary: "Microsoft has resumed construction on its $1 billion data center expansion across Licking County after pausing the project in April 2025 over rising material and tariff costs. The company held groundbreaking ceremonies at its Heath and Hebron sites in late February 2026, with Hebron construction expected to finish in November, according to Construction Review. The New Albany site, which received a 15 year property tax abatement from the city in October 2024, continued site preparation through the pause and is nearing completion of its site work, the outlet reported."
detail: "Microsoft's data center buildout across Licking County, announced in October 2024 at an initial $1 billion, includes sites in New Albany, Heath and Hebron. The company paused construction in April 2025, citing higher costs tied to tariffs on construction materials and imported technology, and said land at the paused sites would remain available for farming in the meantime. According to Construction Review, Microsoft held groundbreaking ceremonies at the Heath and Hebron sites in late February 2026, and the Hebron data center is expected to be finished by November. The New Albany site, a 245,000 square foot data center on nearly 200 acres, continued through the pause and is nearing completion of its site preparation under contractor Ames Construction. New Albany approved a 15 year property tax abatement for the project in October 2024. No updated completion date has been published for the Heath buildings. The project is expected to create at least 30 full-time jobs at the New Albany site, with One Columbus listed as the regional economic development partner."
why_it_matters: "The restart puts construction activity and a 15 year property tax abatement back on New Albany's books, and the hiring and utility demand across three Licking County sites is the kind of employer-driven growth that has been pushing up rents and land values in that corridor."
