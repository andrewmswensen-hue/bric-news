title: "Franklin County tentative values rise about 10 percent in triennial update"
summary: "The Franklin County Auditor's Office released tentative property values on June 9 as part of its triennial update, with most county homeowners seeing an average increase of about 10 percent. Grandview Heights and Bexley are up about 14 percent, Upper Arlington and Westerville about 12 percent. Appraisers reviewed roughly 16,000 sales from 2025 and compared similar properties within each neighborhood rather than entering homes. Auditor Michael Stinziano said a higher value does not translate one to one into a higher tax bill."
detail: "Ohio law requires county auditors to review home sales every three years so values stay current, and Franklin County is in the middle of that triennial update. Appraisal Director DeMarcus Parker said the office reviewed about 16,000 sales from 2025. Appraisers do not go inside homes; they analyze sales data and compare similar properties within a neighborhood, which is why the auditor's office is asking owners to supply information the data cannot show. Auditor Michael Stinziano said flooding from recent storms, other damage, or an outdated kitchen are the kinds of conditions that can move a value in either direction, and that the office will adjust values based on documentation owners provide. According to NBC4, the county says the relationship between value and tax is not one to one: the bill depends on how a property compares with others in the area and on the levies that apply to it. Owners can look up tentative values online now. Informal value reviews run from July through September and can be done in person or virtually with an appraiser, or by submitting documentation. Final valuations are released in December, and a formal appeals process follows."
why_it_matters: "A 10 percent county average with Bexley and Grandview Heights near 14 percent sets the base for the next three years of tax bills on Franklin County rentals, and because appraisers never see the interior, condition items that depress a rental's actual value are invisible unless the owner documents them. The informal review window ran July through September, ahead of final values in December."
