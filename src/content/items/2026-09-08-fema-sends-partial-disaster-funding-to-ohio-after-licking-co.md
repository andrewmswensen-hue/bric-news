title: "FEMA sends partial disaster funding to Ohio after Licking County flooding"
summary: "FEMA approved $36 million in disaster recovery funding for Michigan, Wisconsin and Ohio two days after Gov. Mike DeWine asked President Trump to issue a Presidential Major Disaster Declaration for flooding that hit 23 Ohio counties, including Licking County, between Aug. 10 and Aug. 23. Initial FEMA and SBA damage assessments found major damage to 806 properties statewide and minor damage to 475 more, with at least 50 damaged beyond repair. Trump has not yet issued the full disaster declaration, so individual assistance grants for residents remain unapproved."
detail: "The flooding struck 23 Ohio counties from Aug. 10 to Aug. 23, with the South Fork of the Licking River overtopping and damaging properties including a mobile home park in Pataskala. Gov. DeWine's request to President Trump, co-signed by Rep. Troy Balderson and Sens. Jon Husted and Bernie Moreno, sought a Presidential Major Disaster Declaration that would open up FEMA's Individual Assistance Grant Program for temporary housing, home repairs, and rental assistance, along with SBA physical damage loans. Two days after DeWine's letter, the Trump administration approved $36 million from three FEMA programs, Public Assistance, the Hazard Mitigation Grant Program, and Building Resilient Infrastructure and Communities, split between Michigan, Wisconsin and Ohio. That funding generally supports public infrastructure and mitigation projects rather than direct payments to homeowners. The Individual Assistance Grant Program, Transitional Sheltering Assistance, and SBA physical damage loans that would help individual residents remain unapproved because Trump has not yet declared a full major disaster for Ohio. Licking County's emergency management agency says it is continuing to document damage to support the state's case for the fuller declaration."
why_it_matters: "A Licking County landlord with flood damage from the August storms cannot yet apply for FEMA individual assistance or an SBA physical damage loan, since only the infrastructure and mitigation funding has been approved so far, so that repair money is still contingent on Trump issuing the fuller disaster declaration DeWine requested."
