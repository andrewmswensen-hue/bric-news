detail: "Southwest Licking Local School District and West Licking Joint Fire District sent a joint letter in early October to the boards of trustees of Etna and Harrison townships and to Pataskala City Council, saying tax incentives granted to developers are costing both agencies millions of dollars a year. The letter says the two agencies together lost about $12.9 million last year to tax abatements, tax increment financing districts and Community Reinvestment Areas, with the fire district's share alone totaling $7.1 million. School district treasurer Richard Jones said the net loss to the school district is about $4 million a year.\n\nThe letter says the districts will continue honoring incentive agreements already in place but do not support approving new ones, and they are asking to be included in discussions before future deals are made. Grow Licking County economic development director Cameron Garczyk said his organization already tries to bring affected taxing bodies into talks with developers early. Etna Township Trustee Rachel Zelazny said she has invited both agencies to take part going forward. Voters in the area are also deciding a school bond issue and an income tax increase from 0.75% to 2% this week."
why_it_matters: "A single abatement package can run millions of dollars a year once CRAs, TIFs and other incentives stack up, and these districts are now pushing to review deals before they are signed, a shift that could slow how fast Licking County townships approve the next incentive package."
