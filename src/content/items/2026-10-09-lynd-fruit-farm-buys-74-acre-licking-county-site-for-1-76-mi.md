detail: "Lynd Fruit Farm, a family owned apple orchard operating since 1919, purchased a 74-acre property at 10503 North St. in Utica, in Licking County, for $1.76 million, according to Licking County Auditor records. The sale closed August 26 after a July auction run by Bauer Realty & Auctions, where at least 13 entities placed bids. Lynd Fruit Farm won with a bid of $1.6 million that was later finalized at $1.76 million; the sale went back and forth with another anonymous bidder who wanted only two of the property's three parcels.\n\nThe site splits into a 63.9-acre stretch of farmland and two 5-acre parcels, one holding the property's only structure, a small ranch house built in 1960, and another that Bauer Realty & Auctions had marketed as suited for residential use. In a Facebook post, Lynd Fruit Farm said it has no specific development plans and intends to pursue wetland and prairie restoration instead, citing the land's potential for habitat and education use.\n\nLicking County has become a target for large scale development pressure in recent years, with dozens of data centers built in nearby New Albany and Intel's $28 billion chip plant announced for the county in 2022, a trend residents have nicknamed the area's turn into a Silicon Heartland."
