title: "Columbus rental registry opens Oct. 1, no exemption for small owners"
summary: "Columbus' rental property registry opened for initial registration October 1 and closes December 31, 2026, at $15 per unit annually, with no exemption for single-family homes or small owners. City council passed the registry 8-1 on April 20, 2026, under ordinance 0923-2026 (City Code Chapter 4515). Before passage, the city dropped proactive interior inspections and cut the per-unit fee from $20 to $15, with the fee cap reduced from $2,500 to $1,500. Routine inspections will cover exteriors, common areas, and shared mechanical systems, not individual unit interiors."
detail: "Columbus City Council passed the city's rental property registry on April 20, 2026, by an 8-1 vote, with Councilmember Emmanuel Remy casting the lone dissent and Councilmember Nicholas Bankston sponsoring the legislation at the request of Building and Zoning Services. Ordinance 0923-2026 enacted Chapter 4515 of the Columbus City Code, and a second ordinance, 1713-2026, set the fee schedule on July 2, 2026. Initial registration runs October 1 through December 31, 2026, with the registry itself taking effect for the 2027 calendar year. There is no phase-in by portfolio size, zip code, or property type, and no exemption for single-family rentals. Registration is annual, and property owners must update their information within 30 days of any change. The program changed in two ways between its original proposal and passage: the city removed a provision for proactive inspections of unit interiors, limiting routine inspections to exteriors, common areas, and shared mechanical systems, and it lowered the per-unit fee from an initial $20 to $15, with the per-property fee cap cut from $2,500 to $1,500. Ownership transparency and the annual filing requirement were not softened, since identifying the responsible party for each property is the program's core purpose. At $15 per unit, Columbus' fee is about a fifth of the $70 per unit that Cleveland charges under its own rental registry. As of September 1, 2026, the city had not yet published a registration portal or administrative rules under Columbus City Code 4515.01, so property owners should watch for an announcement from Building and Zoning Services on how to file."
