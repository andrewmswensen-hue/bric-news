summary: "Licking County voters will decide a Licking Park District levy renewal and roughly a dozen municipal, township, and school funding requests on the Nov. 3 ballot, the Columbus Dispatch reports. The park district seeks a five-year renewal of its 0.375-mill levy, generating about $2.27 million a year for parks and trails. Granville and Pataskala voters will separately decide charter amendments on whether to prohibit data center construction, while Perry Township voters will vote on a new zoning plan for unincorporated areas. Early voting is underway and runs through Nov. 1."
detail: "Licking County voters face a countywide property tax renewal and roughly a dozen municipal, township, and school funding requests on the Nov. 3 ballot, according to the Columbus Dispatch's roundup of certified county issues. The Licking Park District is asking voters to renew its 0.375-mill levy for five years, which would generate an estimated $2.27 million annually and cost property owners $10 per $100,000 of a property's auditor-assessed market value. The district maintains more than 1,600 acres of parks and dozens of miles of trails and is preparing a new park along the Licking River.\n\nSeveral townships and villages have fire, EMS, road, and park-related levy renewals or new levies ranging from roughly 1 to 4 mills, each carrying its own per-$100,000 cost to property owners. Granville and Pataskala voters will separately decide charter amendments that would prohibit data center construction in their communities, and Perry Township voters will vote on a proposed zoning plan covering the township's unincorporated territory. Southwest Licking schools are asking voters to replace an existing 0.75% earned income tax with a 2% tax and to approve a 2.87-mill levy to repay an $80.7 million school construction bond."
