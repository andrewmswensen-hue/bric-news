detail: "Two downtown Newark preservation projects, the restoration of the Licking County Courthouse's ornate West Courtroom and of the Louis Sullivan Building across 3rd Street, will receive 2026 State Historic Preservation Office Awards from the Ohio History Connection on Friday, Nov. 6, at the Ohio Supreme Court building in Columbus, the Reporting Project reported. Newark is the only city honored twice this year among a dozen award recipients chosen from 37 applicants statewide; the office has given the awards since 1983.\n\nThe Sullivan Building restoration, which included repairing or replacing hundreds of terra-cotta exterior tiles, took 10 years. Both projects sit on Courthouse Square, which also includes the Newark Arcade, a $19 to $20 million restoration completed in February 2025 that added housing units along with retail space and was honored with the same award a year earlier. State Historic Preservation Officer Diana Welling said her office was impressed enough with Newark's preservation record that it held its annual staff retreat there, touring the courthouse, the Sullivan Building, and the Newark Earthworks, a UNESCO World Heritage Site."
