detail: "Ohio voters weighed in on 133 local school tax and bond levies on Tuesday's ballot, according to The Statehouse News Bureau, with 69 levies seeking new revenue and 60 up for renewal. Ohio School Boards Association CEO Tom Hosler said school districts faced added uncertainty this year because of statewide debate over property tax changes, on top of rising costs and state funding that has lagged inflation.\n\nHosler said districts had to decide earlier in the year how much revenue to seek in November, before knowing whether the Ohio legislature would pass property tax legislation that could change how much they are able to raise. That's really a scary proposition for school boards to be in, he said.\n\nSeparately, Ax the Tax, a group pushing to eliminate Ohio property taxes outright, said it fell short of the signatures needed to make this November's ballot but is continuing to collect signatures for a possible 2027 vote. Ohio's gubernatorial candidates have also proposed various forms of property tax relief, and Hosler said the next legislature is likely to take up new tax changes."
