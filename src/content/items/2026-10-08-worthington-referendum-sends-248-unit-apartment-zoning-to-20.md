detail: "A referendum petition on a Worthington zoning change has enough valid signatures to go before voters in November 2027, the city announced Oct. 7, according to the Columbus Dispatch. City Council voted July 20 to rezone 445 East Dublin Granville Road to allow a 248-unit apartment complex, a $52.4 million project from builder Elford Development. Under Ohio law, residents can force a referendum on most council zoning votes by gathering signatures equal to at least 35% of the turnout in the last gubernatorial election. The group Democracy Before Developers spent two months collecting signatures and submitted 3,476 valid names, clearing the 3,017 threshold. The city says the rezoning stays in effect, and the project remains possible, until the November 2027 vote. Nearly 40% of Worthington's voters signed the petition. The land is currently owned by disability nonprofit I Am Boundless, which had agreed to sell to Elford Development and, as part of the deal, to retain its existing jobs and add 65 to 80 more. Neither Democracy Before Developers nor I Am Boundless responded to the Dispatch's requests for comment. The outcome will decide whether a mid-size multifamily project proceeds in a built-out, high-demand Columbus suburb, or whether the zoning reverts and the site stays as is."
