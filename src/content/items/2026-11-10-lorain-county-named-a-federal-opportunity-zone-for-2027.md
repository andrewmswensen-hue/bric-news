summary: "Gov. Mike DeWine and the Ohio Department of Development have named Lorain County a federal Opportunity Zone under the Opportunity Zones 2.0 program, state Rep. Gayle Manning (R-Avon) announced. The designation takes effect January 1, 2027, and makes investment in the county eligible for federal tax incentives tied to housing development, business expansion, job creation and community revitalization."
detail: "State Rep. Gayle Manning, R-Avon, announced that Gov. Mike DeWine and the Ohio Department of Development have recognized Lorain County as a federal Opportunity Zone under the Opportunity Zones 2.0 program. The designation takes effect January 1, 2027. Opportunity Zones give investors a federal tax incentive, typically a deferral or reduction of capital gains tax, for putting money into designated census tracts, with the stated aim of attracting outside investment for housing development, business expansion, job creation and community revitalization, according to Manning's announcement. The release did not specify which census tracts within Lorain County carry the new designation or how many were included. Lorain County sits outside BRIC's core Franklin, Delaware and Licking county coverage area, but the designation is part of the same nationwide OZ 2.0 map rollout that replaced thousands of zones designated under the original 2018 Opportunity Zones program, trading many urban-core tracts for additional suburban and rural ones. Manning's office framed the designation as a tool to help attract investment in the county."
