title: "Grove City council votes 6 to 1 for a 12-month data center moratorium"
summary: "Grove City Council voted 6 to 1 in June to impose a 12-month moratorium on data centers, with a six-month review added by amendment. The vote followed an hours-long meeting in which residents raised concerns about water, air, noise and environmental effects, while supporters pointed to economic benefits and a project team cited more than 200 jobs. Some council members said they needed more time before approving any data center project. The moratorium follows similar measures already adopted in two neighboring townships."
detail: "Grove City Council members voted after a packed meeting at which residents on both sides spoke at length. Ron Campbell of the group Protect Grove City argued a data center would degrade property values, the surrounding environment and the community. Supporters argued the city would be sitting on the sidelines while other communities benefit, and one said a moratorium would end the conversation rather than pause it. Representatives for the data center project told council their information showed water usage and noise levels would remain low, and cited more than 200 jobs. According to Spectrum News, council member Mohamed Omar said the responsible step was to take a step back because the city was not ready. The final vote was 6 to 1 for a 12-month moratorium on any data centers in the city while council gathers more information, with an amendment adding a six-month review. Spectrum News reported the Grove City moratorium follows similar moratoriums already put in place by two neighboring townships."
why_it_matters: "A 12-month pause with a six-month review takes Grove City parcels off the table for data center buyers through that window, and the two neighboring townships that moved first mean the pause covers a contiguous area rather than one jurisdiction. Land assembled on the expectation of a data center exit in southwest Franklin County now has a dated timeline attached to it."
