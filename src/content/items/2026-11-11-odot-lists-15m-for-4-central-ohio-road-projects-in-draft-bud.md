detail: "The Ohio Department of Transportation's Transportation Review Advisory Council released a draft funding list Nov. 10 covering 13 major construction projects statewide totaling $46.7 million, set to begin in fiscal year 2027, the Columbus Dispatch reported. Four of the listed projects are in central Ohio. The State Route 161 widening project in Franklin and Licking counties would receive $4 million in fiscal year 2029 for right-of-way work tied to the Mink Street Interchange. Delaware County's Sunbury Parkway project, a nearly $32 million proposal to build a new Interstate 71 interchange at U.S. 36/State Route 37 and connect it with a new Sunbury Parkway road, would receive $3 million in fiscal year 2028 for right-of-way work and project development. The State Route 315 project at Lane Avenue in Columbus would receive $4.1 million in fiscal year 2029 for detailed design work. The U.S. 33 widening project in Franklin and Fairfield counties would receive $4 million in fiscal year 2028 for preliminary engineering. TRAC funds projects under ODOT's Major New Capacity Program, which covers projects costing more than $12 million that increase roadway capacity or reduce congestion."
