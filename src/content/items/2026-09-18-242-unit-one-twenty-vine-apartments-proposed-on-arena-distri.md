title: "242-unit One Twenty Vine apartments proposed on Arena District parking lot"
summary: "A seven-story, 242-unit apartment building called One Twenty Vine is proposed on a gravel lot bounded by Vine, Spruce, Armstrong, and Kilbourne streets in the Arena District. The Downtown Commission is scheduled to review it Tuesday, September 22. The project is residential only, with a resident garage on the first floor and a skywalk to the Kilbourne Garage. The site is now paid public parking."
detail: "Columbus Navigator reports that plans for One Twenty Vine go before the Downtown Commission on Tuesday, September 22, at 8:30 a.m. The drawings are the first public look at the project. The application combines nine parcels and portions of right-of-way into one site, with 120 Vine St. as the future address.\n\nThe building is seven stories, with a two-story base and five stories above it, and a parapet just over 83 feet. It has no retail. The first floor holds enclosed resident parking entered from the rear, plus a leasing office, fitness center, and lounge. A skywalk would link the third floor to the Kilbourne Garage. The courtyard shows a pool, grills, and a fireplace.\n\nThe owner on the application is HP Land Development, an affiliate of Hamilton Parker, in partnership with Nationwide Realty Investors, which developed the Arena District. The same group built the neighboring Two Twenty Vine, which has 124 apartments. The plans include no public parking, so the surface lot near the arena and North Market would be lost. If the commission approves the design, the project still needs building permits."
