title: "OHFA approves financing for four housing developments, one in Columbus"
summary: "The Ohio Housing Finance Agency's board approved financing September 17 for four affordable housing developments across the state, including Seton Square South and West in Columbus, a 108-unit project for older Ohioans co-developed by Seton Development and Borror Development that received up to $10 million in Multifamily Housing Revenue Bonds, a $2 million Housing Development Loan and a 4% Low-Income Housing Tax Credit reservation. The other approved projects are two 120-unit senior developments in Sylvania Township and Lima, and a 122-unit development in Harrison Township near Dayton."
detail: "OHFA's board approved four separate financing packages at its September meeting, all combining tax-exempt Multifamily Housing Revenue Bonds with a 4% Low-Income Housing Tax Credit reservation, the agency's standard structure for preserving or creating income-restricted units. In Franklin County, Seton Development Inc. and Borror Development Co. will build Seton Square South and West, a 108-unit development for older Ohioans in Columbus, backed by up to $10 million in bonds plus a separate $2 million Housing Development Loan.\n\nThe other three approvals went outside central Ohio: EREG Senior Living received up to $35 million in bonds for a 120-unit assisted living project in Sylvania Township, near Toledo, and a second, identically sized project in Lima. Marian Development Group received up to $37 million in bonds for a 122-unit development in Harrison Township, near Dayton.\n\nAll four developments serve older Ohioans, continuing a pattern of state financing weighted toward age-restricted affordable housing this cycle. Because these units carry income and rent restrictions tied to the LIHTC program, they compete for the same renter pool as market-rate senior housing in Franklin County but at below-market rents, which can affect lease-up pace and achievable rents for nearby unrestricted senior properties once construction finishes."
