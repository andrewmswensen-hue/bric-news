summary: "Dublin City Council approved April 13 a final development plan for a 340-unit mixed-use project on a 12-acre parcel near the Bridge Street district. The project includes 28,000 square feet of ground-floor retail, a 430-space parking structure, and a public plaza. The developer, a Cincinnati-based firm, targets a 2028 delivery. Dublin staff cited the project as consistent with the Bridge Street Corridor Vision Plan adopted in 2021."
