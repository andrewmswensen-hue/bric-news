title: "Columbus HOA denies flood claim tied to its own downspout"
summary: "A Columbus homeowners association declined to cover a basement flooding repair bill of more than $6,000 for resident Ebone Ford, even though its maintenance chart assigns gutters and downspouts to the HOA, according to WCMH. Documents reviewed by the station identified a disconnected downspout, which the HOA is responsible for maintaining, as a contributor to August flooding. The association told WCMH its governing documents, not board or management decisions, determine maintenance duties, and said the documents are public and can be reviewed by an independent attorney."
detail: "An Ohio homeowners association refused to help pay for basement flood damage despite acknowledging the disconnected downspout that contributed to it falls under its own maintenance responsibility, WCMH reported. Columbus resident Ebone Ford said August storms flooded her basement for the first time in 18 years in the home after she had already flagged the loose downspout to her HOA. The association's maintenance chart assigns gutters and downspouts to the HOA while leaving interiors to homeowners, and documents reviewed by the station identified the downspout as a contributing factor. The HOA told the station its governing documents, not management or board choices, control who pays for what, and declined to discuss Ford's case directly. Attorney Oney Snyder of Carlile Patchen & Murphy told WCMH that disputes like this one turn on exactly what the governing documents say, and suggested owners request a board hearing or special meeting, bringing neighbors with similar complaints, before pursuing legal action, since litigation costs can exceed the repair bill itself."
why_it_matters: "Owners and investors in HOA communities should check their association's maintenance chart for which exterior components (gutters, downspouts, siding) the HOA is contractually responsible for, since an HOA can maintain that interior water damage is the homeowner's problem even when the triggering defect was the association's to fix."
