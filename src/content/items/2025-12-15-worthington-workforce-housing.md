title: "Worthington completes workforce housing program framework"
summary: "Worthington finalized in December 2025 the framework for a city workforce housing program, following extended community input and council deliberation. Council described the final framework as balancing payroll growth, affordable housing, and workforce housing priorities. The program is intended to support housing accessible to median-wage workers serving the Worthington economy. Implementation details and any associated zoning or incentive mechanisms are expected to roll out through subsequent ordinances."
why_it_matters: "Worthington is one of the tightest housing markets in Franklin County and historically resistant to higher-density residential; even an incremental workforce-housing framework is notable here, and the next round of ordinances implementing it will decide whether the framework actually moves inventory or stays aspirational."
