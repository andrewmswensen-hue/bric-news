title: "Granville developer pauses 174-unit apartment plan amid pushback"
summary: "The Stream Group is pausing the apartment portion of its proposed Munson Trace mixed-use project in Granville after community and council pushback, while still considering the retail and restaurant component, managing partner Seth Teagle told The Reporting Project. The project had proposed 174 apartments, 102 two-bedroom and 72 one-bedroom units, plus shops and restaurants on 17 acres along Galway Drive owned by Licking Memorial Hospital."
detail: "The Stream Group, a Columbus-based developer, proposed Munson Trace for 17 acres along both sides of Galway Drive in Granville Township, land owned by Licking Memorial Hospital under contract to sell to the company. The plan paired 174 apartments, 102 two-bedroom and 72 one-bedroom units, on the south side near the Route 16 freeway with shops and restaurants on the north side. After the proposal drew criticism on social media and at a village council meeting before Stream Group had even filed a formal application, managing partner Seth Teagle said the company decided it lacked the political support to pursue the zoning approvals and variances the apartments would need. Teagle said retail and restaurant operators the company had approached cited insufficient nearby households and daily traffic as concerns, with about 90% saying they needed the residential density to seriously consider the site, partly because roughly 60% of restaurant profits typically come from the lunch rush. Without the apartment component, Teagle said the company must reevaluate what uses the site can support and has not set a new timeline. He said Stream Group still intends to pursue some commercial development on the property."
