detail: "Central Ohio closed on more than 3,000 home sales in July, a 6 percent increase from a year earlier, compared with a 0.7 percent year over year increase in home sales nationwide, according to a report from Columbus Realtors cited by NBC4. The median sale price for a home in the region was around $350,000. More than 6,000 homes remained on the market, the highest inventory level the region has seen in a decade. Carlie Boos, executive director of the Affordable Housing Alliance of Central Ohio, called the report a sign of progress but said the region still has ground to make up after a decade of pent-up housing demand from renters and families who delayed buying. Boos pointed to local efforts such as the city's zone-in initiative, which allows more duplexes, triplexes and senior condos within existing neighborhoods, as one way the region has begun adding housing variety at different price points. The report did not break down sales or inventory by property type, so it does not say how much of the increase involves investor activity or rental conversions specifically."
