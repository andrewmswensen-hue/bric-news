detail: "Hamilton County's latest property value reassessment found that residential properties increased an average of 27% to 28%, the county auditor's office reported, according to the Cincinnati Enquirer. The reassessment is part of Ohio's periodic countywide revaluation cycle, which resets property values for tax purposes. Hamilton County property owners should expect to receive notification letters about their updated valuations in the coming weeks. The increase is smaller than the jump recorded during the county's last revaluation in 2023, when the average increase was 34%, according to the auditor's office. Ohio's 88 counties each run a full reappraisal every six years with a lighter update at the three-year mark, the same general cycle other Ohio counties, including Franklin County, use to reset values between full reappraisals. A higher assessed value can raise a property's tax bill, though Ohio's tax-reduction factors can offset part of that increase depending on local levies passed in prior years."
