summary: "Citizen group Democracy Before Developers submitted 3,976 petition signatures, well above the 3,017 required, seeking a November 2027 referendum on Worthington City Council's July vote to rezone about 20 acres for a $52.4 million, 248-unit housing development on land owned by nonprofit I Am Boundless. The Franklin County Board of Elections must still validate the signatures. I Am Boundless says a successful referendum would delay the project and raise its costs."
detail: "Worthington City Council voted July 20 to rezone roughly 20 acres at 445 E. Dublin Granville Road, land owned by disability nonprofit I Am Boundless, to allow developer Elford Development to build a $52.4 million, 248-unit housing project. Citizen group Democracy Before Developers launched a petition drive on July 23 seeking to put that rezoning decision to a citywide referendum vote in November 2027. Ohio law requires signatures from 35 percent of the number of voters who cast ballots in the city's last gubernatorial race, which for Worthington is 3,017 signatures; the group submitted 3,976 by the Sept. 21 deadline, and its campaign coordinator estimated 85 to 90 percent will be validated. The city will hold the signatures for 10 days before sending them to the Franklin County Board of Elections for validation and certification. The Worthington Chamber of Commerce, a group called Building Worthington's Future, several council members and I Am Boundless have defended the July rezoning. I Am Boundless president Patrick Maynard said a certified referendum would delay the housing project and raise the developer's costs, and would also delay planned walking trails tied to the project."
why_it_matters: "If the Board of Elections certifies the referendum, the rezoning and the 248-unit project stay on hold until a November 2027 vote, which is a concrete timeline any investor tracking Worthington land values or competing rental supply near this site needs to watch."
