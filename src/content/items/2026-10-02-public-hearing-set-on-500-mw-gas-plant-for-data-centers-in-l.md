title: "Public hearing set on 500 MW gas plant for data centers in Licking Co."
summary: "The Ohio Power Siting Board holds a public hearing Tuesday, Oct. 6, on a Will-Power proposal to build a 500-megawatt natural gas power plant and 260-megawatt battery storage system in western Licking County to supply electricity for data centers. It is one of seven plants already approved and four more under review statewide, representing about 5,300 megawatts combined, more than a quarter of Ohio's roughly 20,000-megawatt peak demand, according to the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio. A related evidentiary hearing is set for Oct. 21 in Columbus."
detail: "The Ohio Power Siting Board will hold a public comment hearing at 5 p.m. Tuesday, Oct. 6, at the New Albany-Plain Local Schools Annex Building on a proposal by Will-Power to build a 500-megawatt natural gas-fired power plant with a 260-megawatt battery storage system in western Licking County. A separate evidentiary hearing on the proposal is scheduled for 10 a.m. Oct. 21 at the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio's offices in Columbus. According to PUCO public affairs director Matt Schilling, the plant is one of a growing number proposed to serve electricity directly to data centers rather than the broader grid, a category known as behind-the-meter generation. Seven similar plants have already been approved statewide and four more are under review, totaling about 5,300 megawatts, equal to roughly a quarter of Ohio's peak summer demand of about 20,000 megawatts. One such plant, the 200-megawatt Socrates South facility near Beech and Morse roads in Licking County, began operating in August. An interactive map of the proposed and operating plants is available on the Ohio Power Siting Board's website."
