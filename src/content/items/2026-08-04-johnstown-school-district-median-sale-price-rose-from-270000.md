summary: "Columbus Realtors data shows the median sale price in the Johnstown-Monroe Local School District rose from $270,000 in 2020 to $425,000 in 2025, a period in which data center construction and the Intel project reshaped the area. Spectrum News reported that home prices also rose across Central Ohio over the same span, so the increase cannot be attributed to data center development alone. Johnstown Realtor Tammy Hendren said limited new construction, residents who took developer buyouts and want to stay in the area, and investor demand have combined to tighten supply."
detail: "Developers have bought and cleared homes near Johnstown to make way for data centers and supporting infrastructure, and cranes for the Cologix campus and the Intel project are visible from residential streets. According to Spectrum News, Columbus Realtors figures put the median sale price in the Johnstown-Monroe Local School District at $270,000 in 2020 and $425,000 in 2025. The report is explicit that prices rose across Central Ohio in that period, so the local increase reflects both regional conditions and local ones. Johnstown Realtor Tammy Hendren said many owners who accepted buyout offers still want to live in the community, which adds competition for the homes that remain, and that investor interest during the buyout period also lifted values. She cited limited supply of newly built homes as a further constraint. The report notes the effect on properties sitting directly beside active construction sites is less clear than the district-wide trend, and that owners in that position weighing a move face replacement costs well above the district median for comparable acreage and setting."

That is a source of bidding pressure that ends when the buyouts do, unlike the underlying regional trend."
