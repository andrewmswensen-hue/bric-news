detail: "American Electric Power, the Columbus-based utility that serves AEP Ohio customers including much of the Columbus metro, reported third-quarter 2026 operating earnings of $2.23 per share, compared with $1.80 per share a year earlier. The company said it is tracking at or above the high end of its 2026 full-year guidance range of $6.25 to $6.55 per share and issued new guidance of $6.75 to $7.05 for 2027 and $7.40 to $7.70 for 2028. AEP also announced an expanded five-year capital plan of $100 billion through 2031, which it said should support rate-base growth of more than 13% a year, driven largely by rising electricity demand from manufacturers, data centers, and other large customers. AEP said fully executed take-or-pay service agreements with those large-load customers are now expected to produce up to $21 billion in fixed-cost offsets for existing residential customers over the life of the contracts, an increase from a previously announced $16 billion, because large customers are contracted to absorb the incremental system costs their demand creates. CEO Bill Fehrman pointed to a proposed second rate reduction in Indiana as an example of how that cost-sharing structure can lower bills for existing customers while the company invests in generation, transmission, and distribution to keep up with demand growth."
