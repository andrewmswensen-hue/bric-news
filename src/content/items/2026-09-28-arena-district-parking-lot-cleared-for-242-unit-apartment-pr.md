title: "Arena District parking lot cleared for 242-unit apartment project"
summary: "The Columbus Downtown Commission approved a two-building, 242-unit apartment development, One Twenty Vine, on a gravel parking lot in the Arena District on September 22. Nationwide Realty Investors, the development affiliate of Nationwide Insurance, plans the project at what would become 120 Vine St., combining nine parcels between Kilbourne and Armstrong streets. The buildings would rise six to seven stories with no retail space, 56 enclosed parking spaces, and a shared courtyard with the neighboring 124-unit Two Twenty Vine building."
detail: "The Columbus Downtown Commission approved design plans for One Twenty Vine, a two-building, 242-unit apartment development, on September 22, clearing the way for Nationwide Realty Investors to move toward construction permits on a surface parking lot in the Arena District. The project would combine nine parcels and portions of surrounding right-of-way into a single address at 120 Vine St., on the block between Kilbourne and Armstrong streets. The buildings would rise six stories at one corner and seven at another, using a split-level entrance along Armstrong Street to handle a roughly one-story grade change across the site, with five floors of wood construction over a two-story podium. Plans include 56 enclosed parking spaces, a leasing office, fitness center and resident lounge, but no retail space, and 10 bicycle parking spots. NRI president Brian Ellis said the design works around a high-voltage utility line running through the site while preserving a shared courtyard and pool deck with the neighboring Two Twenty Vine building, a 124-unit project NRI opened on the adjacent parcel last year. The project continues NRI's redevelopment of a site formerly used by building supplier Hamilton Parker, which relocated in 2000. The approval advances a Columbus downtown plan goal of reaching 40,000 downtown residents by 2040 by replacing surface parking with denser housing. The commission's vote clears design review only; construction permits are still required, and no construction timeline was included in the submitted materials."
why_it_matters: "Downtown Columbus landlords with units near the Arena District will be competing against 242 new units once One Twenty Vine delivers, though the project still needs construction permits and has no announced completion date."
