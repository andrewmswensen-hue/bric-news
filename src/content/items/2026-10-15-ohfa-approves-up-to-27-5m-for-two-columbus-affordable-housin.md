summary: "The Ohio Housing Finance Agency board approved financing for three affordable housing developments statewide, two of them in Columbus. Columbus Housing Partnership (Homeport) received a $2.5 million loan, $3 million in assistance funding, and authorization for up to $12 million in revenue bonds to rehabilitate 120 units at George's Creek in Franklin County. The Finch Group and Lutheran Social Services of Central Ohio received authorization for up to $10 million in revenue bonds to rehabilitate 90 units for older Ohioans at two Franklin County sites."
detail: "The Ohio Housing Finance Agency's board approved financing for three affordable housing developments at its October meeting, two of them in Columbus. Columbus Housing Partnership, doing business as Homeport, received approval for a $2.5 million Housing Development Loan, $3 million in Housing Development Assistance Program funding, and authorization to issue up to $12 million in Multifamily Housing Revenue Bonds to rehabilitate 120 affordable units at George's Creek in Franklin County. The project also received a 4% Low-Income Housing Tax Credit reservation.\n\nA second Columbus project, a rehabilitation of Lutheran Social Services' North Community and Village Place developments across two Franklin County sites, received authorization for up to $10 million in Multifamily Housing Revenue Bonds. The Finch Group, doing business as TFG Housing Resources, and Lutheran Social Services of Central Ohio are co-developing the 90-unit rehabilitation, which also received a 4% LIHTC reservation and will serve older Ohioans.\n\nThe third approval, outside the Columbus area, authorized a $2 million Housing Development Loan and a 4% LIHTC reservation for Essence LLC to rehabilitate 189 units for older Ohioans at Ashland Manor in Toledo. OHFA did not set a timeline for construction or completion on any of the three projects."
