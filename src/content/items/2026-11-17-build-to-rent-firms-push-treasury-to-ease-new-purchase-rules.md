title: "Build-to-Rent Firms Push Treasury to Ease New Purchase Rules"
summary: "Build-to-rent operators are urging the U.S. Treasury Department to revise proposed rules implementing restrictions from the 21st Century Road to Housing Act. The draft rules would require large institutional investors, defined as firms with at least 350 single-family rental units, to adopt written build-to-rent programs before buying a community, and would bar operators from purchasing a home more than 18 months after it receives a certificate of occupancy. Quinn Residences CEO Richard Ross told Bisnow the 18-month limit could disrupt how operators buy excess inventory from homebuilders."
detail: "The build-to-rent sector is pushing back on draft Treasury Department rules that would implement restrictions on institutional single-family rental buyers under the 21st Century Road to Housing Act, following a separate fight earlier this year to keep the sector out of stricter regulation in a housing bill moving through Congress.\n\nThe proposed rules define large institutional investors as firms owning at least 350 single-family rental units and would require them to adopt a written build-to-rent program before purchasing a community. A second provision would bar those operators from buying a home more than 18 months after it receives its certificate of occupancy, limiting how long builders can hold inventory before selling it to a BTR buyer.\n\nQuinn Residences CEO Richard Ross, whose firm has developed or purchased 5,200 homes across 34 communities since 2020, told Bisnow it can take two years or more to stabilize a new community, and said the 18-month limit would make it harder for homebuilders to sell excess inventory to BTR operators. More than 100 public comments have been filed with Treasury so far, and the National Rental Home Council said other operators are still preparing their own submissions. Operators expect consolidation across the sector regardless of how the rules are finalized."
why_it_matters: "If Treasury loosens the 18-month resale window in the final rule, Columbus-area build-to-rent buyers could move faster to absorb newly built homes that would otherwise reach owner-occupant or small-investor buyers competing for the same starter homes."
