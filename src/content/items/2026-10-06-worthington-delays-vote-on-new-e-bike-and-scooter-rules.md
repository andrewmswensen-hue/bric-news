detail: "Worthington City Council was set to vote on an ordinance adding new restrictions to the city code for electric bikes, e-scooters, hoverboards and other personal vehicles, but postponed action after more than an hour of debate at its Oct. 5 meeting, according to the Columbus Dispatch. The draft would have required riders under 18 to wear helmets, banned minors under 14 from operating the devices, and added penalties for violations.\n\nCouncil voted 7-0 to send the ordinance back to city staff for amendments, with a new vote planned for Nov. 2. Members want staff to research a five-foot passing distance requirement for cars overtaking riders, an education program for riders ages 12 to 16, and a possible requirement that riders disengage their motors when passing pedestrians on sidewalks. Four residents spoke during the public comment period, with most supporting new regulation. The Dispatch noted the postponement comes as a wave of central Ohio suburbs adopt new e-bike and e-scooter rules amid rising use of the vehicles, particularly among children."
