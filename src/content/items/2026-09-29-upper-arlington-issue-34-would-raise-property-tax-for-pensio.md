title: "Upper Arlington Issue 34 would raise property tax for pension fund"
summary: "Upper Arlington's Issue 34, on the November ballot, would renew an expiring 0.89-mill police and fire pension levy and add 0.24 mills, for a total of 1.13 mills over five years starting tax year 2027. The city says the levy would cost the owner of a $100,000 home about $26 a year, $8.50 more than current, and the owner of a $500,000 home about $130 a year, $42 more. The levy funds Ohio's state-mandated police and fire pension contribution and is expected to raise about $2.2 million annually; failure would create a $2.2 million general fund gap."
detail: "Issue 34 asks Upper Arlington voters to renew an existing 0.89-mill levy set to expire at the end of tax year 2026 and add 0.24 mills, for a combined 1.13-mill, five-year levy effective for tax year 2027 and payable starting in 2028. The levy funds the city's state-mandated contribution to the Ohio Police and Fire Pension Fund, a required 19.5 percent of gross wages for police and 24 percent for fire, which the city has covered through voted property tax levies for 50 years across 10 prior renewals.\n\nThe city estimates the levy would cost $26 per year for every $100,000 of home value before Ohio's rollback provision, or about $23.71 after it, meaning a $500,000 home would pay roughly $118.55 to $130 per year, an increase of $42 or less over current levels depending on rollback eligibility. The levy is projected to generate about $2.2 million annually, and the city says failure would create an equivalent gap in general fund operations, since the pension contribution is a legal obligation regardless of whether the levy passes. The measure appears on the November 2 ballot."
why_it_matters: "If you hold rental property in Upper Arlington, this levy adds a small but permanent line to your property tax bill either way, since the city's pension contribution is mandatory and a failed levy would likely just get made up through the general fund or a repeat ballot attempt rather than disappearing."
