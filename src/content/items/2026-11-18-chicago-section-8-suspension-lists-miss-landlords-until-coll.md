summary: "Chicago Housing Authority has issued 433 suspensions against 402 landlords or companies since 2018 for failed inspections, skipped training, or collecting rent on vacant voucher units, with 246 suspensions still active. An analysis found CHA suspends individual LLCs rather than the people who control them, letting the same investors keep drawing voucher payments at other buildings even after a suspension."
detail: "Since 2018, the Chicago Housing Authority has suspended landlords from new Housing Choice Voucher contracts 433 times for violations including failed inspections, collecting payments on vacant units, and skipped required training; 246 of those suspensions remain active as of October. Because CHA suspends individual LLCs rather than the people who control them, the same investor can continue collecting voucher subsidies at one building while barred at another using a different entity. Four out-of-state investors, Yissocher Rotenberg, Chaim Bialostozky, Mark Nussbaum, and Eliazer Tauber, accounted for roughly 30 percent of large multifamily foreclosure filings in Chicago in 2025, with lenders seeking at least $87 million, and three of the four appear on CHA's suspension list, typically added only after their buildings had already deteriorated or faced foreclosure suits. An entity tied to Nussbaum and the Tauber family, Straightline Management, was suspended in 2024 for contract violations and remained suspended more than two years later. CHA cross-references the city's problem landlord and building code lists quarterly and can extend a suspension across a landlord's full portfolio if it finds multiple vendor IDs used to dodge sanctions, though enforcement varies case by case."
