title: "Whitehall amends zoning code to standardize ADU development rules"
summary: "Whitehall City Council advanced Ordinance 004-2026 through its second reading on February 17, amending Section 1116.03 and enacting new Section 1116.04 to standardize the development of accessory dwelling units citywide. The ordinance creates uniform rules for detached and attached ADUs in residential districts. Specific size limits, setback requirements, and owner-occupancy provisions are set out in the ordinance text. The measure moved toward third reading and final adoption as part of Whitehall's broader housing strategy."
