detail: "The Delaware County Land Bank, legally the Delaware County Land Reutilization Corporation, is starting its own demolition grant program to clear blighted, vacant or abandoned structures across the county. The program runs alongside, but separately from, the Ohio Department of Development's statewide demolition grants, which the Land Bank has previously helped administer. Applications will be accepted and reviewed on a rolling quarterly basis, with the Land Bank board issuing decisions within 30 days after each quarter closes. The first application window opened Oct. 1 and closes Dec. 31. The program currently sets no maximum grant amount, but applicants must provide at least a 25% local funding match. Eligible applicants include municipalities, townships, other local jurisdictions, nonprofit organizations, and private property owners or developers. Delaware County Commissioner Jeff Benton, who sits on the Land Bank board, said the local program gives the county more control over blight removal and a faster response than relying solely on the state process. The Land Bank was established in December 2017 and is governed by representatives from the County Commissioners' Office, the Treasurer's Office, the city of Delaware, and Orange Township."
