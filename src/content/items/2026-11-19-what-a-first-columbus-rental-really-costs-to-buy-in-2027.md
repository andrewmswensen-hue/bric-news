summary: "A $250,000 Columbus rental bought with 15% down requires roughly $56,000 to $71,000 in cash once closing costs, rent-ready repairs, lender reserves and pricing adjustments are added to the down payment, according to RL Property Management. Fannie Mae allows 15% down on a one-unit investment purchase but requires six months of PITI in reserves, and loan-level price adjustments run as high as 4.125% at that down payment level versus 2.125% at 25% down."
detail: "RL Property Management breaks the cash needed for a first Columbus rental into five layers: the down payment, closing costs, rent-ready repairs, lender-required reserves and the owner's own operating cushion, and says all five need to be in hand at closing, not just the down payment.\n\nFannie Mae's eligibility matrix allows 15% down on a one-unit investment purchase and 25% on two to four units through Desktop Underwriter, as of August 2026. But loan-level price adjustments (LLPAs) push many investors toward larger down payments: Fannie's September 2026 matrix prices a loan at 80.01% to 85% loan-to-value with a 4.125% adjustment, versus 2.125% at 70.01% to 75%, a difference of about $4,800 on a $212,500 loan if paid as points. Ohio buyers paid an average of $3,394 in closing costs in 2025, per Bankrate's Lodestar-sourced report, and Franklin County can add a real property transfer tax of up to 30 cents per $100 of value. Fannie Mae's Selling Guide requires six months of PITI reserves for an investment property, which RLPM pegs at $11,388 on a $1,898-a-month payment in its worked example.\n\nUsing Freddie Mac's October 29, 2026 average 30-year rate of 7.24%, Zillow's $243,122 typical Columbus home value and its $1,471 citywide rent index, both from September 2026, RLPM's example shows a 15%-down purchase needing $56,000 to $71,000 in cash before escrows, and a 25%-down purchase needing $80,000 to $86,000. At example rents, both scenarios run short of covering the mortgage, management fee and a maintenance set-aside by $200 to $370 a month, before counting vacancy."
