title: "Franklin City Council raises building permit fees 29% effective July 1"
summary: "Franklin City Council voted unanimously April 14 to raise building permit and inspection fees under Ordinance 2026-031. Single-family new construction permits increase from $485 to $625, a 29% jump, effective July 1, 2026. The ordinance passed on emergency basis, meaning it takes effect without the normal waiting period. Multi-family and commercial rates also increase; see ordinance text for full schedule. City staff cited cost-of-service recovery as the basis for the adjustment."
