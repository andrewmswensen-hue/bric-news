detail: "Delaware County is now the 22nd fastest-growing county in the country, and the city of Delaware alone has more than 4,000 homes under construction, according to WSYX. County Commissioner Jeff Benton said keeping pace with that growth requires expensive infrastructure work, including road widening and new sewer plants and lines. One example is a 141-acre purchase along Sawmill Parkway that commissioners approved in June for $17 million, a site Benton described as ranked in the top five statewide by JobsOhio for its access to the airport and major roads. Traffic capacity on US 23 is a separate concern; Benton said the road cannot currently handle an additional five to ten thousand vehicles a day, and the Ohio Department of Transportation is studying a controversial US 23/71 connector project that some rural landowners in Delaware, Marion and Morrow counties worry would consume farmland. Separately, Delaware County Transit is asking voters in November to approve a 0.2 percent sales tax to fund seven planned service expansions over five years, including new routes in Powell and Orange Township. Executive Director Andy Volenik said ridership has grown 174 percent in recent years, with 25 to 30 percent of trips tied to employment. Without the levy, the transit system says it will likely cut service."
