title: "The real Columbus cost of self-managing versus hiring a property manager"
summary: "A partner cost comparison from RL Property Management lays out the Columbus math on self-managing a rental versus paying for professional management. Flat-rate management on a $1,400 rental runs roughly $1,529 to $2,333 a year depending on service tier, while self-managing saves the fee but adds time, vacancy risk and retail repair pricing that rarely shows up on a spreadsheet. The piece also flags a new requirement: every Columbus rental owner must register with the city's rental registry between October 1 and December 31, 2026, regardless of how the property is managed."
detail: "On a $1,400-a-month Columbus rental, flat-rate management runs about $1,529 a year on a Passive plan, $1,769 on Standard, or $2,333 on Premium, according to RL Property Management's own pricing, which includes a $125 annual accounting fee and no separate leasing fee. Percentage-based competitors price differently: Evernest Columbus, cited as an example, charges 8% of collected rent plus a tenant placement fee equal to half a month's rent, a $250 renewal fee, and a $149 annual property condition report. Which pricing model costs less depends on the year: flat pricing tends to win when a unit turns over, and percentage pricing tends to win during a long, quiet tenancy. Self-managing has its own costs that a spreadsheet often misses. A well-priced Columbus single-family rental leases in 14 to 21 days once it is rent-ready, and each vacant day on a $1,400 unit costs about $46, so a week saved by faster leasing is worth roughly $322. Filing a possession action in Franklin County now costs $149, effective August 1, 2026, and runs about $448 all in when handled through RL Property Management, though the comparison notes that six weeks of lost rent during a turnover or eviction is usually the larger number regardless of who manages the filing. Two costs sit outside both the management and self-management comparisons: a one-time $300 onboarding fee when a unit comes on vacant, and repair costs as they happen, quoted at $84 an hour plus a $15 trip charge and materials for in-house maintenance, or vendor cost plus a 15% project-management fee capped at $450 per project. Owners with five to nine units can qualify for a 10% per-unit discount under RL Property Management's pricing."
why_it_matters: "Every Columbus rental owner has to register with the city's new rental registry between October 1 and December 31, 2026 at $15 a unit, whether they self-manage or hire a property manager, so it is a new fixed cost worth budgeting for now regardless of which side of the cost comparison a portfolio falls on."
