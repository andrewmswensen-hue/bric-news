title: "New Regionomics study projects Central Ohio housing and human needs through 2050"
summary: "A long-range study commissioned by Columbus City Council and the Human Service Chamber of Franklin County examines how population growth is likely to reshape housing, poverty, homelessness, health, disability and workforce participation across the 10-county Central Ohio region through 2050. The report, produced by Regionomics and titled Columbus in 2025, 2035, and 2050: A Survey of Human Needs Now and in the Future, was discussed at a Columbus Metropolitan Club forum on August 27. The full report is posted by the Human Service Chamber of Franklin County."
detail: "The study was commissioned by Columbus City Council and the Human Service Chamber of Franklin County and produced by Regionomics, whose owner Dr. Bill LaFayette presented findings at an August 27 Columbus Metropolitan Club forum alongside Michael Corey of the Human Service Chamber, Rhea Cunningham of the Boys and Girls Club of Central Ohio, and Columbus City Councilmember Melissa Green.\n\nThe report covers the 10-county region rather than Franklin County alone, and projects how a growing, aging and diversifying population will affect housing, poverty, homelessness, health, disability and workforce participation at three horizons: 2025, 2035 and 2050. The forum framed the central questions as where strain will show up first and who is most at risk as the region adds population.\n\nThe document is public and posted on the Human Service Chamber of Franklin County's website. Columbus City Council's role as a commissioning party makes it a likely reference point in future council discussions of housing and human services funding."
