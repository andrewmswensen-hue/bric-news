title: "Federal judge blocks HUD plan to cut funding for fair housing enforcement groups"
summary: "A federal judge blocked the Department of Housing and Urban Development from overhauling how it funds the Fair Housing Initiatives Program, ordering the agency to distribute money using the previous year's framework. The order followed a July lawsuit by the Massachusetts Fair Housing Center and the National Fair Housing Alliance. HUD's plan would have made five awards and excluded organizations with annual budgets below $5 million, a threshold the court said excludes nearly all existing nonprofit fair housing groups."
detail: "Judge Myong Joun of the U.S. District Court for the District of Massachusetts ruled that HUD's stated reasons for the new Notice of Funding Opportunity were pretextual, writing that the agency's explanation that the plan would modernize and strengthen the 39-year-old Fair Housing Initiatives Program belies credulity given that the proposal contained only five awards and set a $5 million minimum budget threshold.\n\nThe suit was filed in July by the Massachusetts Fair Housing Center and the National Fair Housing Alliance, which sought a temporary restraining order along with declaratory and injunctive relief. Housing advocates said the plan would have defunded more than 100 community-based nonprofit fair housing enforcement organizations.\n\nThose groups collectively handle about 75 percent of housing discrimination complaints filed in the United States, and they run much of the paired testing that generates those complaints. HUD said it stands behind the lawfulness of its funding notices and will avail itself of all legal options.\n\nThe order restores the earlier funding framework rather than resolving the underlying dispute."
why_it_matters: "The nonprofit groups this money funds are the ones that run paired testing on rental listings and file the complaints that follow, so the ruling keeps in place the outside enforcement capacity that reviews how Columbus landlords advertise, screen and answer inquiries."
