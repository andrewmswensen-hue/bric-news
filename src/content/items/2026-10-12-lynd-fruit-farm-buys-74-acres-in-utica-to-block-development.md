detail: "Lynd Fruit Farm, a family-owned Pataskala orchard operating since 1919, has purchased 74 acres at 10503 North St. in Utica for $1.76 million, according to Columbus Navigator and Licking County Auditor records. The farm won the parcel at a July auction run by Bauer Realty & Auctions and intends to protect the land from development rather than farm it, with plans to explore restoring wetland and prairie habitat. Co-owner Alex spotted the property in spring and pitched it to the farm's other owners once it went up for auction; the farm says the decision to bid was unanimous. The 74 acres include nearly 64 acres of farmland and two separate five-acre parcels, one of which has a ranch-style home built in 1960. The farm says more detail on its restoration plans, including possible educational use and habitat seed sourcing, is expected this winter. The purchase comes as Licking County sees rising development pressure, with data centers expanding around New Albany and Intel's planned semiconductor campus drawing attention and debate over how to balance growth with the county's rural land."
