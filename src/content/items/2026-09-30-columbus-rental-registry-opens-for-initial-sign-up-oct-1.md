title: "Columbus Rental Registry Opens for Initial Sign-Up Oct. 1"
summary: "Columbus's rental property registry opens for initial registration Oct. 1 and closes Dec. 31, under ordinance 0923-2026, which City Council passed 8-1 in April. The fee is $15 per unit annually, down from an earlier $20 proposal, with the cap reduced from $2,500 to $1,500. Registration applies to every rental unit regardless of owner size, and routine inspections under the program cover exteriors and common areas, not individual unit interiors."
detail: "Columbus City Council passed the rental property registry on April 20, 2026, by an 8-1 vote, enacting Chapter 4515 of the Columbus City Code through ordinance 0923-2026. Councilmember Nicholas Bankston sponsored the legislation at the request of Building and Zoning Services; Councilmember Emmanuel Remy cast the lone dissenting vote. A second ordinance set the fee schedule on July 2, 2026. Initial registration runs October 1 through December 31, 2026, with the registry taking effect for the 2027 calendar year. Every rental unit must register, regardless of the owner's portfolio size, property type, or zip code, and registrants must update their information within 30 days of a change. The fee is $15 per unit per year, below the $20 figure in the program's original draft, with the per-property cap cut from $2,500 to $1,500. The program is a registry, not a license: the city does not approve a property before it can be rented. Chapter 4515 pairs annual registration with a Preventative Education Inspection on a three-year cycle, covering exteriors, common areas, and shared mechanical systems, but not individual unit interiors, which were part of an earlier draft and were removed before passage. As of early September, the city had not yet published a registration portal or procedural rules, and owners should watch for an announcement from Building and Zoning Services. The Columbus Apartment Association, through attorney Dimitri Hatzifotinos, has said it intends to challenge the ordinance on the grounds that it duplicates county filings already required under Ohio Revised Code Chapter 5323, but no lawsuit had been filed as of September 1."
