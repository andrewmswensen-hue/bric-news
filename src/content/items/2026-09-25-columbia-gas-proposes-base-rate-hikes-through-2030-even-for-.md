title: "Columbia Gas Proposes Base Rate Hikes Through 2030, Even for Low Usage"
summary: "Columbia Gas of Ohio filed a preliminary proposal with the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio on Sept. 18 that would raise its base residential rate by $14.79 to $68.05 a month in 2028, then by $5.11 to $72.92 in 2029 and by $4.41 to $77.12 in 2030, charges customers would owe regardless of how much gas they use. The base rate excludes the separate cost of the gas itself and delivery charges."
detail: "Columbia Gas of Ohio filed the preliminary rate proposal with the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio on Sept. 18, the utility's second rate increase request in five years. Its 2021 request originally sought more than $220 million before PUCO approved a $68 million increase; that approval was challenged in court by the Environmental Law and Policy Center and the Citizens' Utility Board of Ohio, and the Ohio Supreme Court dismissed the challenge in June. Columbia Gas said the new case will recover costs of operating its natural gas delivery system, including continued replacement of aging infrastructure, system monitoring technology and emergency response capability, and cited rising costs for contractors, pipe and materials since 2021. Including estimated gas costs, Columbia Gas projected a household using 10,000 cubic feet a month would pay about $137.56 in 2026 under the new base rate, up from an estimated $122.77 under current rates, though it cautioned that global events and weather can move gas prices independent of the base rate change. Columbia Gas must still file a full application explaining the increase, which starts a 180-day PUCO review that includes public hearings; a PUCO spokesperson said a decision is unlikely before summer 2027. The Ohio Consumers Counsel, the state's residential utility watchdog, said it will intervene in the case and is assembling a legal team, and encouraged customers to participate in next year's public hearings."
why_it_matters: "A landlord who covers gas heat in a Columbus rental would see the minimum monthly charge climb by nearly $25 total across 2028 through 2030 even in a unit sitting vacant with the pilot light off, which is worth budgeting into a multi-year pro forma now since the case will not be decided until around summer 2027."
