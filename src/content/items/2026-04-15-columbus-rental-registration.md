summary: "Columbus City Council voted 6-1 on April 15 to advance ordinance 0721-2026, a citywide rental registration and inspection program, to second reading. Under the current draft, owners of one- to four-unit rental properties within city limits would register each unit annually at $85 per unit and submit to exterior inspections every three years. Second reading is scheduled for April 29. Council staff estimated roughly 47,000 rental units would be covered if the ordinance passes as drafted."
