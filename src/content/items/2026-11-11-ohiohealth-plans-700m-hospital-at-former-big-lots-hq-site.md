title: "OhioHealth Plans $700M Hospital at Former Big Lots HQ Site"
summary: "OhioHealth plans to invest $700 million in a new hospital, New Albany Methodist Hospital, at the former Big Lots headquarters site in northeast Columbus, with an opening expected in late 2030, according to NBC4. The 600,000-square-foot project combines 325,000 square feet of renovated space with 275,000 square feet of new construction and is expected to employ about 1,700 people. Columbus City Council approved a zoning change in May allowing hospital use on the nearly 25-acre site."
detail: "OhioHealth CEO Stephen Markovich announced plans Tuesday night to invest $700 million in New Albany Methodist Hospital at the former Big Lots headquarters site in northeast Columbus, NBC4 reported. The announcement came at the New Albany Community Foundation's annual Remarkable Evening benefit. The hospital is expected to open in late 2030 with 160 inpatient beds and provide cardiac, neuroscience, stroke and trauma care, serving northeast Columbus and communities north and east of New Albany, according to Markovich. Construction on the 600,000-square-foot facility, combining 325,000 square feet of renovated space with 275,000 square feet of new construction, is expected to begin early next year. OhioHealth expects the completed campus to employ roughly 1,700 people. The company bought the former Big Lots headquarters at 4900 E. Dublin-Granville Road for $36 million last year, then paid another $22.5 million in August for the adjoining office property at 4960 E. Dublin-Granville Road. Columbus City Council approved a zoning change in May allowing hospital use on the nearly 25-acre site, after OhioHealth appeared before the Columbus Development Commission in February while still studying how to use the property."
