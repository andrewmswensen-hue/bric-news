detail: "Mifflin Township's board of trustees voted 2-0 on Nov. 11 to abolish the township's police division effective immediately, Trustee Kevin Cavener told the Columbus Dispatch. The vote followed the defeat of a 6.9-mill continuing property tax levy on the Nov. 3 ballot, which would have cost property owners $244 for each $100,000 of county assessed value and generated $500,322 a year, according to the Franklin County Board of Elections. Voters rejected the levy 56% to 44%. Cavener said trustees will not pursue another levy to reopen the department, and the division's remaining officers, two full-time and three part-time, were asked to resign. The Franklin County Sheriff's Office will respond to emergency calls in the township but will not provide routine patrols, Cavener said. The division had struggled with staffing since two of its officers were shot during a traffic stop in May 2025. The Fraternal Order of Police's Capital City Lodge No. 9 called the vote a disservice to officers who served the township. Trustees said in a statement that their priority is maintaining effective public safety service while being a responsible steward of public funds, and that more information on future police coverage would come later."
