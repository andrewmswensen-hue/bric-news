title: "HUD rule ends voucher aid for mixed-status households starting Dec. 14"
summary: "HUD finalized a rule ending long-term rental assistance, including Housing Choice Vouchers, for mixed-status households whose members include both eligible and ineligible immigrants. Starting December 14, 2026, all household members must verify citizenship or eligible immigration status regardless of age. The rule ends the prior framework that prorated assistance to cover only eligible members; prorated aid will now be available only temporarily while verification is pending."
detail: "HUD published a final rule that bars mixed-status households, those that include both members with eligible immigration status and members without it, from long-term federal rental assistance. The rule covers Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8) and project-based rental assistance. Effective December 14, 2026, every member of an assisted household must verify U.S. citizenship or eligible immigration status, regardless of age, including children. The change ends a long-standing policy that let mixed-status households receive assistance prorated to the number of eligible members, with no subsidy paid toward ineligible household members. Under the new rule, that prorated arrangement becomes temporary, available only while a household's verification is pending, not as an ongoing accommodation. According to guidance from the National Association of Realtors, public housing agencies (PHAs), the local bodies that administer vouchers, remain responsible for collecting and verifying immigration documentation, not the private landlords who accept the vouchers. Voucher landlords are advised to continue working with their PHA as they have, and should not request citizenship or immigration paperwork from tenants directly. Tenant questions about the new verification requirements should be directed to the PHA. NAR says it will continue monitoring how HUD implements the rule."
why_it_matters: "For Columbus landlords who accept Housing Choice Vouchers, the paperwork here falls on the Columbus Metropolitan Housing Authority, not on the landlord: expect no new tenant-screening steps on your end, but be ready for occasional subsidy recalculations as CMHA re-verifies mixed-status households before the December 14 deadline."
