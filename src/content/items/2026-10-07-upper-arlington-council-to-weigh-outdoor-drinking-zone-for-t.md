title: "Upper Arlington Council to Weigh Outdoor Drinking Zone for Tremont Center"
summary: "Upper Arlington City Council will consider creating a Designated Outdoor Refreshment Area (DORA) covering commercial districts including Tremont Center, under Ohio Revised Code 4301.82. Within a DORA, patrons can buy an alcoholic drink from a participating bar or restaurant and carry it outside within the zone's boundaries. The city's Economic Development team is holding a community meeting on October 14 at 6 pm at St. Mark's Episcopal Church to gather resident feedback before council takes up the proposal."
detail: "Upper Arlington's Economic Development department is proposing a Designated Outdoor Refreshment Area (DORA) for certain commercial districts in the city, including Tremont Center. A DORA is a defined outdoor zone where Ohio's open container law is relaxed under Ohio Revised Code 4301.82: within the boundary, a patron who buys an alcoholic drink from a participating bar or restaurant with an on premises liquor permit can carry it outside and keep drinking it while moving between businesses in the zone.\n\nCity Council will take up the proposal after a community meeting scheduled for 6 pm on Wednesday, October 14, at St. Mark's Episcopal Church on Dorset Road. The city's Economic Development team will explain how the DORA would work and collect resident input before any council vote. Residents with questions can contact Economic Development Manager James Russell.\n\nDORAs have become a common tool Ohio cities use to support foot traffic and dwell time for bars and restaurants in a defined commercial district, and Upper Arlington's proposal would add Tremont Center, one of the city's main retail and dining strips, to the list of Ohio communities with an active zone."
why_it_matters: "A DORA designation tends to extend how long shoppers linger in a commercial district, which can support rents and occupancy for ground floor retail and nearby rental housing around Tremont Center if council approves it."
