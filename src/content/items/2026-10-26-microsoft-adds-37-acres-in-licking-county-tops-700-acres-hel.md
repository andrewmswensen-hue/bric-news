summary: "Microsoft bought another 37 acres in Pataskala for about $6.65 million, bringing its Licking County land holdings to more than 700 acres, according to Data Center Dynamics. detail: "Microsoft's latest purchase covers 37 acres on Refugee Road in Pataskala, acquired for roughly $6.65 million and reported first by Columbus Business First, Data Center Dynamics said. It follows Microsoft's November 2023 purchase of 289 acres on the same road and a further 40 acres added in December 2024, bringing its total Licking County holdings past 700 acres. Microsoft has not confirmed the new Pataskala parcels are intended for a data center, and no project for the site appears on the company's public project pages. The company paused its Ohio data center plans in April 2025, saying it would no longer move forward with $1 billion earmarked for three campuses in New Albany, Heath and Hebron while it reevaluated its investment strategy. Construction resumed on the Heath and Hebron sites in March, and as of July both campuses were in early construction, focused on site preparation, water management and substation work; the New Albany campus was nearing completion of its current construction phase. Microsoft said it would keep serving as the community's point of contact as the projects continue, with all three sites targeting completion by 2029."
