detail: "Canal Winchester is a 10,058-resident suburb about 10 miles southeast of downtown Columbus, with population up 10.3% since 2020 and median household income of $131,209 over 2020-2024, according to Census Bureau figures. Average rents in August 2026 ran about $1,463 for a two-bedroom and $1,840 for a three-bedroom, against a $369,799 median sale price recorded in June 2026, putting cap rates in the 5 to 7% range, toward the stability end of the Columbus-area market rather than the yield end. The city straddles Franklin and Fairfield counties and three townships, and contains at least four school districts: Canal Winchester Local Schools does not serve the entire city, with parts instead assigned to Groveport Madison, Pickerington, or Bloom-Carroll school districts. Because the taxing district on the county auditor's record, not the property's mailing address, determines the actual tax bill, two houses with Canal Winchester addresses can carry different effective tax rates. In the Fairfield County portion of the city, tax year 2025 effective residential rates ranged from 44.60 to 56.02 mills depending on which school district applies, a difference that can run roughly $1,200 a year in taxes on a $300,000 house. Cap rates in the 5 to 7% range put Canal Winchester toward the stability half of the Columbus-area rental map rather than the yield half, meaning values have tended to hold up more than they have produced outsized cash flow. Canal Winchester falls outside the boundary of the Columbus rental property registry under City Code Chapter 4515, whose initial registration window runs October 1 through December 31, 2026, so owners of Canal Winchester rentals are not subject to that city of Columbus filing requirement, even though many of their tenants and comparable properties sit just across the line from registry-covered addresses."
