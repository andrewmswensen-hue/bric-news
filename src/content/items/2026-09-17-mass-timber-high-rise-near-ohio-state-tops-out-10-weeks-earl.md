detail: "A consortium led by Columbus-based Elford Construction topped out Intro Columbus, a 13-story, 158-foot mass timber student housing building near Ohio State University, according to an announcement shared with Construction Dive. The team reached the milestone about 10 weeks ahead of its original November target, a year to the day after the project broke ground on September 8, 2025.\n\nThe 242,000-square-foot building will include 186 units when finished, with completion targeted for 2027. Design firm DLR Group says it is the tallest mass timber student housing project in the country. The team used glulam columns and beams along with cross-laminated timber panels, sourced domestically from Alabama, to move at roughly two and a half floors every two to three weeks, installing 44 CLT panels a day. Elford Construction superintendent Shane Warner credited a tight sequencing plan for the pace.\n\nTimber construction also carries an environmental angle: DLR estimates the project will sequester 1,128 metric tons of carbon compared with a steel and concrete design of the same size. The project reflects a broader shift toward timber in commercial construction as steel prices climb, a trend construction firms have also begun applying to data center buildings.\n\nTopping out ahead of schedule lets crews start interior framing, mechanical rough-in and exterior wall and window installation on the lower floors while upper floors are still being built."
