detail: "The Joint Committee on Agency Rule Review (JCARR) advanced rules proposed by the Ohio Department of Behavioral Health (ODBH) that make permanent an executive order Gov. Mike DeWine issued to address recovery housing concerns. The rules give ODBH direct authority to certify recovery housing in Ohio, an industry Rep. Justin Pizzulli (R-Scioto County) said currently lacks consistent regulation and oversight.\n\nUnder the rules, recovery housing operators must submit a residence registration form to ODBH, which sets procedures for applications, renewals, correcting deficiencies and addressing noncompliance, along with standards for obtaining, maintaining and revoking certification. ODBH is authorized to investigate and penalize unlicensed recovery housing operators, with exceptions clarified for operators with legitimate needs.\n\nPizzulli, who sponsored House Bill 58, which the House passed unanimously to close loopholes in the recovery housing industry, said the rules incorporate several of that bill's provisions. He pointed to a sharp rise in unlicensed operators, particularly in southern Ohio: the state counted 356 recovery houses in 2022, compared with more than 1,700 today."
