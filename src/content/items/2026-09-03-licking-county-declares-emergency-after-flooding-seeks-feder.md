title: "Licking County declares emergency after flooding, seeks federal aid"
summary: "Licking County commissioners declared a state of emergency Aug. 27 after August flooding damaged homes, roads, and property in Pataskala, Heath, Hebron, Buckeye Lake, and parts of Newark, positioning the county to receive disaster aid. Gov. Mike DeWine requested federal disaster relief Sept. 2 for 23 counties including Licking and Franklin, seeking individual assistance covering temporary housing, home repairs, rental assistance, and property replacement, plus Small Business Administration loans for uninsured losses."
detail: "Pataskala was the hardest-hit community, with flooding that damaged homes, trailers, trees, and roads and caused some sewer backups. The South Fork of the Licking River and the Licking River reached major and moderate flood stages, respectively, after heavy August rainfall, and crews conducted several water rescues after drivers entered flooded roads. Emergency Management Agency staff have been conducting damage assessments for weeks and hosted a multi-agency resource event in Pataskala on Sept. 3, with additional events planned in Hebron, Buckeye Lake, and Newark. County Commissioner Tim Bubb said the declaration is meant to help residents access any benefit available for recovery and repair. DeWine's request follows a similar disaster declaration for Licking County after March storms earlier this year. No cost estimate for the August flood damage or final count of affected residents was available as of early September."
