title: "Meta files plans for new 620,000 sq. ft. Licking County data center"
summary: "Meta has proposed a new 620,000-square-foot data center on a 350-acre site in Jersey Township, Licking County, east of its existing New Albany International Business Park campus. The preliminary site plan, filed by Meta subsidiary Sidecat LLC, goes before the Licking County Planning Commission on November 23. The project would sit next to a proposed 500-megawatt gas-fired power plant, and the site includes wetlands, two ponds and a stream Meta proposes to remove."
detail: "The proposed data center would sit on a 350-acre site owned by a Meta subsidiary, east of Mink Street, south of Jug Street and north of State Route 161 and Beaver Road NW in Jersey Township, in unincorporated western Licking County. It would be the first Meta data center building east of the New Albany International Business Park, where the company already has at least 20 buildings on two sites.\n\nThe preliminary site plan, filed by Sidecat LLC, Meta's land acquisition and development arm, calls for a 620,000-square-foot facility and describes a dozen wetlands, two ponds and a stream on the site that the company proposes to remove. If approved, the data center would be powered by the 500-megawatt, gas-fired Neo Power plant that Will-Power Ohio has proposed next door, which remains under review by the Ohio Power Siting Board.\n\nThe Licking County Planning Commission is scheduled to consider the site plan at its November 23 meeting. Data Center Map counts 259 data centers proposed, under construction or operating in Ohio, with 135 in Central Ohio and 52 in Licking County, most clustered around the New Albany business park."
