detail: "The Columbus Dispatch reports that traditional starter homes are getting harder to find in central Ohio. The median Columbus sales price was $350,000 as of last month, compared with $213,500 in August 2019. A Zillow search of Franklin County listings at $150,000 or less on Sept. 18 returned 224 results, mostly in four areas: Linden, Hilltop, the South Side and parts of the Southeast Side. None of the sub-$150,000 listings were in Clintonville, Upper Arlington, Hilliard, Dublin or Worthington.\n\nThe article describes typical listings in each area. In North Linden, many are one-story homes of about 1,000 square feet built in the mid-1900s, some with unfinished basements, and a handful are under $100,000. Hilltop has updated houses near $145,000 and a few under $100,000 that appear move-in ready. On the South Side, listings cluster in Deshler Park and Driving Park, and several are foreclosures. Across all areas, many of the lowest-priced homes are advertised as investment opportunities, sold at auction, or in need of substantial rehabilitation."
why_it_matters: "These are the neighborhoods where entry-level purchase prices overlap with rental demand, so the listing mix shows where acquisition costs and repair scopes sit for a small investor. The note that cheaper homes are often marketed to investors is a reminder that owner-occupant buyers are competing in the same pockets."
