title: "Dublin eyes rezoning 210-acre Metro Center into walkable mixed-use district"
summary: "Dublin officials are advancing a plan to redevelop the roughly 210-acre Metro Center office park, more than 90 acres of which is parking lots, into a walkable district with housing, retail, and expanded trail connections. The city's Metro Center Revitalization Plan, adopted in December 2024, calls for new zoning and design standards for the area rather than a wholesale rebuild like Dublin's Bridge Park district. A Planning and Zoning Commission work session is scheduled for Sept."
detail: "The proposed district would mix commercial, residential, and civic uses, with one focus area, the Frantz Road Gateway, calling for a connected trail corridor and linear park space linking Metro Center to surrounding neighborhoods. City senior planner Chris Will said the goal is to reinvest in an already-developed area rather than continue outward growth, describing the project as reimagining rather than duplicating Bridge Park's mixed-use model. Metro Center currently functions as a suburban office park with roughly 7,700 parking spaces; the plan envisions housing and street-level retail replacing some of that surface parking while preserving the area as an employment center. Community input is expected to continue through the zoning and design-standard process, with no construction timeline set yet since the rezoning itself has not been approved."
why_it_matters: "For an investor with property near Metro Center or along the Frantz Road corridor, a formal rezoning process that adds housing and retail to a 210-acre office park signals where Dublin expects new residential demand to concentrate over the next several years, well before any construction begins."
