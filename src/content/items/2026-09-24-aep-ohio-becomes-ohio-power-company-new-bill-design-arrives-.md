title: "AEP Ohio becomes Ohio Power Company, new bill design arrives Oct. 1"
summary: "AEP Ohio is changing its legal operating name to Ohio Power Company and will send a redesigned monthly bill starting October 1, following a format the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio approved earlier this year. The new bill format is meant to separate distribution, transmission, and generation charges more clearly and add monthly energy-use detail. The company's website will move from AEPOhio.com to OhioPowerCo.com, with automatic redirects. Ohio Power says the change does not affect customer rates, account numbers, service, or how customers pay their bills."
detail: "AEP Ohio, which delivers electricity to about 1.5 million customers across the state, is adopting the legal name Ohio Power Company, the name the company has held for years, and will begin sending a redesigned bill October 1. The Public Utilities Commission of Ohio approved the new bill format earlier this year; the goal is to make clear which charges cover distribution, transmission, and generation, since Ohio Power delivers electricity but does not generate it or profit from the energy it buys on customers' behalf. The bill will also include monthly energy-use detail intended to help customers track consumption.\n\nCompany president Marc Reitter said the rebrand does not change customer service, rates, account access, payment options, or the mobile app; customers keep their existing account numbers and service contacts. The company's website will move from AEPOhio.com to OhioPowerCo.com in October, with the old address redirecting automatically. More detail on the bill redesign is posted at OhioPowerCo.com/Bill."
why_it_matters: "If you manage multiple rental units on AEP Ohio service, update the name on autopay, bookkeeping software, and any tenant-facing utility documentation to Ohio Power Company before October so a payment or record does not bounce on a name mismatch, since the account numbers and rates themselves are not changing."
