title: "Columbus Downtown Commission Approves 242-Unit Arena District Apartments"
summary: "The Columbus Downtown Commission approved a plan Sept. 22 to redevelop a gravel parking lot in the Arena District into a two-building, 242-unit apartment project called One Twenty Vine. Nationwide Realty Investors, the development arm of Nationwide Insurance, will build the project at 154 Vine St., readdressed as 120 Vine St. after combining nine parcels. The seven-story buildings will include 56 enclosed parking spaces, a leasing office, fitness center and resident lounge, with a third-floor skywalk connecting to the neighboring Kilbourne Garage."
detail: "Nationwide Realty Investors, the real estate development affiliate of Nationwide Insurance, plans to build One Twenty Vine on the block between Kilbourne and Armstrong streets, on a site currently used for public parking. The buildings will rise seven stories, with five floors of wood construction atop a two-story podium, and will contain apartments without ground-floor retail. NRI President Brian Ellis told the Downtown Commission the layout accommodates a high-voltage utility line running through the site while still leaving room for a shared courtyard, pool deck and outdoor gathering space between the two buildings. The site drops roughly one story from northeast to southwest, so designers used a split-level entrance along Armstrong Street to work with the grade, making the building appear six stories at one corner and seven at another. Exterior plans call for brick and stone at the lower levels with bronze-colored fiber cement panels above, plus parking for 10 bicycles. The project continues Nationwide Realty's redevelopment of the former Hamilton Parker building-supply site, which relocated to Leonard Avenue in 2000, and sits beside Two Twenty Vine, a 124-unit building the company opened last year. City officials have described replacing downtown surface parking lots with denser housing as a long-running goal of Columbus's adopted downtown plan."
