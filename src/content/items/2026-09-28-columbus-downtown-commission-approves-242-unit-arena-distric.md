title: "Columbus Downtown Commission approves 242-unit Arena District project"
summary: "The Columbus Downtown Commission approved One Twenty Vine, a two-building, 242-unit apartment development from Nationwide Realty Investors, on September 22. The project will replace a gravel surface parking lot at 154 Vine Street in the Arena District with seven-story buildings including 56 enclosed parking spaces and a skywalk connection to the Kilbourne Garage. It is the second phase of Nationwide Realty's redevelopment of the former Hamilton Parker supply-yard site, following the 124-unit Two Twenty Vine building completed last year."
detail: "Nationwide Realty Investors, the development arm of Nationwide Insurance, won design approval from the Columbus Downtown Commission for One Twenty Vine, a two-building apartment project at 154 Vine Street that will be readdressed as 120 Vine Street once its nine parcels are combined. The buildings rise five to seven stories, using wood construction above a two-story podium, and will include a leasing office, fitness center, and resident lounge, but no ground-floor retail. Fifty-six enclosed parking spaces sit on the first floor, and a third-floor skywalk will connect to the neighboring Kilbourne Garage.\n\nThe site currently operates as public parking and sits next to Two Twenty Vine, a 124-unit building Nationwide Realty opened last year; the two buildings will share a courtyard. NRI president Brian Ellis told the commission the layout works around a high-voltage utility line running through the site. The project continues Columbus's push to replace downtown surface parking with density as the city works toward a stated goal of 40,000 downtown residents by 2040. The commission's approval covers design review only; the project still needs construction permits, and no construction timeline has been released."
