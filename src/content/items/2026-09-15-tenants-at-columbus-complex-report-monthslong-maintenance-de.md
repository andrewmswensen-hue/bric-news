detail: "Residents at The Quarry Luxury Apartments in Columbus say they are still struggling to get maintenance issues resolved, months after a local TV station first reported tenant complaints about units without air conditioning. Tenant Luke Buzard, who moved in this summer, said he has filed five maintenance requests, including a leaking dishwasher, a leaking kitchen sink, and an air conditioner that has not cooled his apartment since move-in. He said staff told him he was 80th in line among residents waiting for AC repairs. In a recent email, the property's leasing office told residents that maintenance voicemails sent before Sept. 9 would be deleted as it reset its request system, and asked tenants with unresolved issues to contact the office again. The property's corporate owner, West Shore LLC, did not respond to phone or written requests for comment. Earlier this year, one resident with an unrepaired AC unit broke her lease, and another placed her rent into escrow and filed a complaint with the city of Columbus before her issue was addressed. Housing advocates recommend that tenants document every repair request and contact the city's 311 line or the Legal Aid Society of Columbus if a landlord does not respond, and note that Ohio law sets specific requirements before a tenant can legally place rent into escrow."
