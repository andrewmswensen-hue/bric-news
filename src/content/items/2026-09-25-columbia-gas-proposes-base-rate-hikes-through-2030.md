detail: "Columbia Gas of Ohio filed a preliminary proposal with the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio on September 18 seeking to raise its residential base rate in three steps: from $53.26 to $68.05 per month in 2028, to $72.92 in 2029, and to $77.12 in 2030. The base rate is separate from the cost of the gas itself and from delivery charges, and customers pay it regardless of usage. Columbia Gas said the increase would fund replacement of aging infrastructure, system monitoring technology, and emergency response capability, and noted its last rate increase was filed in 2021.\n\nIncluding estimated gas costs, Columbia Gas projects a household using 10,000 cubic feet a month would pay about $137.56 in 2026 under the new base rate, up from an estimated $122.77 under current rates, though actual gas prices could move that figure. Columbia Gas serves about 1.5 million customers across 61 of Ohio's 88 counties. The Ohio Consumers' Counsel said it will intervene in the case on customers' behalf. Columbia Gas must still file a formal application; PUCO has up to 180 days to review it once filed, with public hearings included, and a decision is unlikely before summer 2027."
why_it_matters: "If this proposal is approved as filed, a Columbus-area landlord paying gas for common areas or master-metered buildings would see the base charge alone climb roughly 45 percent by 2030 regardless of consumption, worth building into a multi-year operating budget now rather than at the 2028 renewal."
