title: "Licking County Hearing Draws Opposition to Second Data Center Power Plant"
summary: "About 50 people attended an Ohio Power Siting Board hearing October 6 in New Albany on a proposed 500-megawatt gas-fired power plant and a 260-megawatt battery storage project from Will-Power in western Licking County. Most speakers opposed the project, citing air pollution and stormwater runoff from natural gas drilling and fracturing. The plant would help power Meta's Prometheus data center campus in the New Albany International Business Park. Licking County now hosts 50 data centers and six proposed power plants to serve them, with one already operating."
detail: "The Ohio Power Siting Board held a public hearing October 6 at the New Albany-Plain Local Schools Annex Building on a proposed 500-megawatt gas-fired power plant, the Neo Power Generation Facility, and a 260-megawatt battery storage system from Will-Power, both sited in western Licking County's Jersey Township.\n\nOf roughly 50 attendees, about half testified, and most opposed the project. Opponents, several of whom live near the proposed site, raised concerns about air pollution, stormwater runoff, and the effects of natural gas drilling and hydraulic fracturing, including disposal of fracturing wastewater. Supporters, including a representative of the International Union of Operating Engineers Local 18, cited construction jobs and tax revenue.\n\nThe plant would help supply power to Meta's hyperscale Prometheus data center campus in the New Albany International Business Park, which sits largely in Licking County. A separate hearing on another proposed plant, the PowerConneX New Albany 3 Energy Center, a 450-megawatt facility on about 30 acres serving a data center on the same site, is scheduled for October 22 at New Albany High School.\n\nLicking County now hosts 50 data centers and six proposed or operating power plants built to serve them. One, the 200-megawatt Socrates South plant near Beech and Morse roads, began operating in August. An evidentiary hearing on the Neo Power Generation Facility proposal, case number 26-0627-EL-BGN, is set for October 21 at the Commission's Columbus offices, where the public can file comments on the case docket."
