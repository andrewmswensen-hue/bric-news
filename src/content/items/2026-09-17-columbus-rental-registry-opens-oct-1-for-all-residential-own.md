title: "Columbus rental registry opens Oct. 1 for all residential owners"
summary: "Columbus City Code Chapter 4515 requires owners of residential rental property to register with the city between October 1 and December 31, 2026. Registration costs $15 per dwelling unit per year, capped at $1,500 per complex. Single-family homes, duplexes, and condos are included, with four narrow exemptions. Owners of 10 or more units must name a local operator living within 100 miles. As of September 1, the city had not published a registration portal."
detail: "Columbus City Council passed ordinance 0923-2026 on April 20, 2026, by an 8-1 vote, creating a Registry of Residential Rental Properties under Chapter 4515. According to RL Property Management, the initial registration window runs October 1 through December 31, 2026, with annual renewal afterward and no phase-in by portfolio size or location.\n\nThe registry collects the owner, the responsible party, unit count, parcel identification, and an emergency contact. Exemptions are limited to properties under the Vacant Building Registry, hotel, motel, or short-term rental permits, dormitories, and facilities licensed under Chapter 4561. A single rented house is covered the same as a large complex.\n\nFees are $15 per dwelling unit each year, capped at $1,500 per complex. Late fees are 10 percent of annual fees under 120 days and 20 percent after that, and conditional status costs $250 plus $10 per unit. July 2026 legislation reportedly froze the fees for at least three years. Registrants have 30 days to report changes such as a new manager, owner, or emergency contact.\n\nOwners of 10 or more units must name a local operator whose primary residence is within 100 miles of Columbus. The routine three-year inspection covers exteriors, common areas, and shared mechanical systems, not unit interiors unless requested. The civil penalty for failing to comply with a notice of violation is $2 per dwelling unit per day, and the publisher notes that higher figures circulating online came from a January draft. This is a separate filing from the county auditor registration required under ORC 5323.02. As of September 1, the city had not published a portal, so preparation now means gathering parcel IDs, unit counts, and contacts. The publisher notes the article is informational and not legal advice."
