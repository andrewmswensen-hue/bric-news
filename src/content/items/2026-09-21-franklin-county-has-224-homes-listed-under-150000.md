detail: "A Zillow search for Franklin County homes listed at $150,000 or less returned 224 results as of September 18, according to Columbus Dispatch reporting. Nearly all were concentrated in four pockets: Linden and Hilltop, on Columbus' Northeast and Southwest sides, plus the South Side and parts of the Southeast Side, including Eastmoor, Whitehall and Blacklick Estates. No such listings existed in Clintonville, Upper Arlington, Hilliard, Dublin or Worthington.\n\nColumbus' median home sale price reached $350,000 last month, up from $213,500 in August 2019. Below the $150,000 mark, listings skew toward smaller, older homes, typically one-story houses of roughly 1,000 square feet built in the mid-1900s, and many carry marketing language aimed at investors or note they need significant rehabilitation. A handful of properties under $100,000 were identified in Linden and Hilltop, though most of those were listed as auction or investment properties rather than move-in ready.\n\nThe pattern illustrates where entry-level inventory still exists in Franklin County and where it has effectively disappeared as prices have risen across the county over the past several years."
