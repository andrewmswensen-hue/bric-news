title: "Northwest Bank to build Dublin headquarters in the Bridge Street District"
summary: "Northwest Bank announced plans to build a new corporate headquarters in Dublin at Bridge North, a mixed-use development by the Daimler Group in Dublin's Bridge Street District. The bank expects to move from its current Easton headquarters into a 75,000-square-foot building in the first half of 2029, on an initial 15-year lease. The space is designed to let the headquarters workforce more than double to 300 by 2034. The City of Dublin will provide a performance-based incentive under an economic development agreement. Northwest moved its corporate headquarters to Columbus in 2021."
detail: "The Bridge North development will include office, hospitality, retail and public gathering space in a campus-style environment, according to the Daimler Group. Northwest Bank's building is planned at 75,000 square feet with a dedicated financial center on the ground floor offering wealth management advisory services.\n\nThe bank said the move supports long-term growth and its ability to attract staff, and that the headquarters workforce is expected to more than double to 300 employees by 2034. The initial lease term runs 15 years, with occupancy planned for the first half of 2029.\n\nNorthwest Bank has a four-state footprint and traces its founding to Pennsylvania 130 years ago. It moved its corporate headquarters to Columbus in 2021 and currently occupies space at Easton. The company said its Warren, Pennsylvania operations remain part of the organization, though the effect of the headquarters move on that location is not yet known.\n\nThe City of Dublin agreed to provide a performance-based incentive under an economic development agreement tied to the project."
