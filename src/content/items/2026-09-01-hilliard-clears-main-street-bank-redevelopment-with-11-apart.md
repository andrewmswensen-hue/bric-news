detail: "Westwood Collective, founded by Andy Warnock, is redeveloping a downtown Hilliard site anchored by a former Chase Bank at 4056 Main St. Hilliard City Council approved a conditional use for a restaurant in the bank building at its Aug. 24 meeting. According to NBC4, public records show the wider project totals about 22,847 square feet across three buildings: the renovated 2,252-square-foot bank connected to a new two-story addition, plus two new three-story buildings holding retail, commercial and office space with 11 residential units on their upper floors. Plans include 39 parking spaces and outdoor seating areas. The restaurant, Lira Italian Kitchen, is a partnership between Westwood Collective and Around The Clock Hospitality, which operates The Clocktower Kitchen and Bar in Plain City. Co-founder Benjamyn Greig said the group chose Italian based on what Hilliard lacked and plans to keep elements of the bank, including converting the vault into a wine cellar. The addition will hold the kitchen and a second-floor mezzanine for private dining, with 140 indoor seats and 34 outside. Greig said the land deal is expected to close in November, with groundbreaking to follow and an opening targeted for the first quarter of 2028."
