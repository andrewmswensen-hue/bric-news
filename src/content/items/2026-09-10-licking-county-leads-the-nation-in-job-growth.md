title: "Licking County leads the nation in job growth"
summary: "Licking County posted the largest year-over-year employment increase of any of the nation's 376 largest counties, growing 3.6% from March 2025 to March 2026 and adding about 2,700 jobs, according to a U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics report. Average weekly wages in the county rose 5.7% to $1,287. Ohio employment overall fell 0.2% in the same period, while national employment rose just 0.1%. Licking County ranked second among Ohio's 15 largest counties for wage growth, behind Delaware County's 10.6% increase."
detail: "The BLS report found Licking County's total employment reached about 77,000 after adding roughly 2,700 jobs over the year, with the largest gains in the trade, transportation and utilities sectors. Grow Licking County Executive Director Cameron Garczyk credited years of business investment and new companies choosing the area. Recent additions cited in the report include manufacturing jobs at Kansai Helios in Johnstown and Westerman Inc. in Hebron, continued construction hiring at the delayed Intel plant in New Albany, growth at Illuminate USA in Pataskala, a new 68,000-square-foot Prudential facility in Heath, a planned 300,000-square-foot expansion by Axium Packaging in Jersey Township, and an expansion by Covestro in Hebron. The county also has dozens of data centers either operating or under discussion with local municipalities. Licking County ranked second among Ohio's 15 largest counties for wage growth, behind Delaware County's 10.6% increase, while statewide employment shrank 0.2% and national employment grew just 0.1% over the same period, according to the BLS data. Licking County Commissioner Tim Bubb called the ranking evidence that the county's economic development investment is paying off."
