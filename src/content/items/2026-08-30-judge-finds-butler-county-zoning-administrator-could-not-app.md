title: "Judge finds Butler County zoning administrator could not appeal a BZA ruling"
summary: "A Butler County Common Pleas judge dismissed the county's court challenge to a Board of Zoning Appeals decision that let Garver Family Farm Market keep agricultural zoning status. Judge Greg Stephens ruled Aug. 27 that the county zoning administrator lacked standing to appeal a decision made in the property owner's favor, citing Ohio Supreme Court and appellate precedent that zoning administrators may join an appeal to defend a board's decision but not to overturn it. He also found the county lacked statutory authority to appeal."
detail: "Butler County challenged a March decision by its own Board of Zoning Appeals that allowed Garver Family Farm Market at 6790 Hamilton Lebanon Road in Lemon Township to keep agricultural status. The county argued the operation went beyond what its rural zoning resolution permits for roadside farm stands. The Garver family argued the business is an agricultural use because more than half of gross sales come from agricultural products and the primary use is a winery. Common Pleas Judge Greg Stephens dismissed the appeal on Aug. 27 without reaching that question. According to the Journal-News, the ruling turned on standing: Stephens found that Zoning Administrator Dennis Dickard could take part in an appeal to defend the board's decision but not to overturn it, citing Ohio Supreme Court and appellate precedent, and that the county had no statutory authority to appeal. County Prosecutor Mike Gmoser said the county has 30 days to appeal to the Twelfth District Court of Appeals and is weighing it, and stressed that the ruling did not address the underlying zoning question or the county's public safety objections. The market building was planned beginning in 2020 and opened in May 2024. The county issued a zoning violation notice in September 2025."
why_it_matters: "When an Ohio board of zoning appeals rules in a property owner's favor, this decision reads the statute to say the zoning administrator is not the party who can take it to court, which narrows who can unwind a variance or use determination already granted. The county has until late September to test that reading in the Twelfth District."
