title: "Columbus Rental Registry Opens Oct. 1: What Owners Must File by Dec. 31"
summary: "Columbus City Code Chapter 4515 requires every owner of residential rental property in the city, from a single rented house to a large complex, to register between Oct. 1 and Dec. 31, 2026. Registration costs $15 per unit, capped at $1,500 per complex, and owners of 10 or more units must name a Local Operator living within 100 miles of Columbus. Failing to fix a cited violation can bring a civil penalty of $2 per unit per day. As of early September, the city had not yet published a registration portal."
detail: "Columbus City Council passed ordinance 0923-2026 in April 2026, creating a Registry of Residential Rental Properties under Chapter 4515. Only four narrow categories are exempt: properties already on the Vacant Building Registry, short-term rental permit holders, dormitories, and certain licensed facilities. Single family homes, duplexes and condos are not exempt, and there is no phase-in by portfolio size. The initial registration window is Oct. 1 through Dec. 31, 2026, and the registry becomes annual starting in 2027. The fee is $15 per dwelling unit, capped at $1,500 per complex, though the ordinance does not clearly address how the cap applies to scattered single-family portfolios, which may end up paying separate fees per address. Owners of 10 or more total units, counted across their whole portfolio, must designate a Local Operator, an individual with responsibility for the property who lives within 100 miles of Columbus. Routine inspections occur on a three-year cycle and cover only exteriors, common areas and shared mechanical systems, not individual unit interiors, unless a tenant or owner requests one. The enacted civil penalty for failing to fix a cited violation is $2 per unit per day, well below the $10 per day figure from an earlier draft that is still circulating online. A property can also be placed on \"conditional status,\" triggering inspection of up to 10 percent of its units, if it accumulates five or more Notices of Violation in 12 months on properties up to 100 units, among other triggers, and that status carries a $250-plus-per-unit fee and follows the building through a sale. Registration is also separate from, not a replacement for, the existing Ohio requirement that owners file contact and parcel information with the county auditor under ORC 5323.02."
