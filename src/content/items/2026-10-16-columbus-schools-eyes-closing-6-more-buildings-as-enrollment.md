summary: "Columbus City Schools is considering closing, moving or consolidating six schools, including Siebert and Highland elementary, Mifflin middle and high schools, and its two preparatory schools, as it seeks another $50 million in budget cuts. The school board has not approved the plan and is expected to vote in December. Superintendent Angela Chapman's broader realignment would close 10 schools and two other buildings in total. detail: "Columbus City Schools is weighing closures, moves or consolidations affecting six schools as it looks for another $50 million in budget reductions, Superintendent Angela Chapman told a town hall Monday. The latest recommendations would close Siebert Elementary, on the south side, after the 2026-27 school year, sending its fewer than 20 students to Stewart, Southwood and other unnamed south-side schools, saving an estimated $1.5 million. Highland Elementary, built in 1895 and operating at about 60% capacity, would close the same year, with students moving to West Mound and Burroughs elementary schools, saving $1.4 million.\n\nMifflin High School and the current Mifflin Middle School building would close after 2027-28, with the high school building converted into a new Mifflin Middle serving grades 6 through 8, saving $3.7 million. The district's two preparatory schools, enrolling fewer than 200 and fewer than 100 students respectively, would close or relocate after 2026-27 so the buildings can be repurposed, saving $4.1 million combined. The school board has not approved any of these changes and is expected to vote on options in December.\n\nThe recommendations are the latest phase of a realignment that would close 10 schools and two other buildings altogether; three elementary schools already stopped holding classes this fall. Columbus City Schools enrollment fell nearly 7% over the past decade, from more than 49,000 students in 2015-16 to under 46,000 in 2025-26, with some buildings filling less than 30% of available seats."
