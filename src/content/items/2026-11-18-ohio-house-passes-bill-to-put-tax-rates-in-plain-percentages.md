title: "Ohio House passes bill to put tax rates in plain percentages"
summary: "The Ohio House passed House Bill 391 on November 18, which requires county auditors and treasurers to show property tax rates as a percentage of a home's true value alongside the existing mill-rate figure. The bill also requires proposed income tax levies to disclose the revenue they are projected to raise in their first year. Sponsors Reps. Jack Daniels and Heidi Workman said the change is meant to make tax bills and ballot levy language easier for homeowners to understand. The bill now goes to the Ohio Senate for consideration."
detail: "Under House Bill 391, county auditors and treasurers would continue calculating effective property tax rates in mills, the traditional unit used in Ohio tax bills, but would also have to display those rates as a percentage of a property's true value, a figure auditors already calculate internally. The bill's sponsors, Reps. Jack Daniels (R-New Franklin) and Heidi Workman (R-Rootstown), said mills are a confusing, outdated unit that makes it hard for homeowners to understand what they owe and what a proposed levy would actually cost them.\n\nThe bill also requires any proposed income tax levy to disclose the estimated revenue it would raise in its first year, giving voters a dollar figure to weigh against the levy's rate before they vote. Daniels said the bill takes no position on any individual levy and is meant only to present tax information in units voters already understand. The sponsors framed the bill as a response to rising property valuations and the levies that have followed them. House Bill 391 passed the Ohio House on November 18 and now awaits consideration in the Ohio Senate."
why_it_matters: "If this becomes law, Franklin, Delaware and Licking County tax bills and ballot levies will show the tax rate as a percent of a property's value instead of only in mills, making it easier for a landlord running the numbers on multiple parcels to compare year-over-year changes without converting rates by hand."
