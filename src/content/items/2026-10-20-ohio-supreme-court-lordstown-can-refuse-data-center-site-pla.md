title: "Ohio Supreme Court: Lordstown can refuse data center site plan"
summary: "The Ohio Supreme Court ruled unanimously that the village of Lordstown was not obligated to keep reviewing a site plan filed by developer Bristolville 25 for a $3.6 billion, four-building data center complex, because the application was missing required stormwater, utility, wetlands and planning materials when the village later imposed a moratorium on data center projects. The court found Lordstown had no legal duty to continue the review once it adopted the ban. Bristolville had argued the village owed it a review under zoning rules in place when the application was first filed in 2025."
detail: "The Supreme Court of Ohio ruled unanimously that the village of Lordstown was within its rights to stop reviewing a site plan for a proposed $3.6 billion, four building data center complex submitted by developer Bristolville 25 on 130 acres straddling Trumbull and Mahoning counties. Bristolville filed its site plan in late 2025, but village council banned data centers two weeks later and later converted the ban to a moratorium running through the end of 2026. Bristolville petitioned the court for a writ of mandamus, arguing Lordstown was bound to complete its review under the zoning rules in effect when the application was filed, before the ban existed. The village countered that an initial review found the submission incomplete: it lacked a stormwater management plan, utility information, a wetlands accounting and a preliminary planning and evaluation form. The court agreed the application never cleared those procedural requirements, so the village incurred no legal duty to finish reviewing it once the moratorium took hold. Lordstown residents had opposed the project over concerns about higher utility rates tied to data centers' electricity and water use. The case sets a precedent for how Ohio municipalities can use permit application deficiencies together with the timing of a moratorium to block a project already in the pipeline."
why_it_matters: "The ruling gives any Ohio municipality, including Columbus area suburbs facing their own data center proposals, a model for pairing an incomplete site-plan review with a timely moratorium to block a project already filed, a tactic that could extend to future zoning fights over large commercial or multifamily developments."
