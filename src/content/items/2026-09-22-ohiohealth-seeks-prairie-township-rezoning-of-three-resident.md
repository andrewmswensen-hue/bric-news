detail: "According to WSYX, OhioHealth is asking Prairie Township to rezone three residential lots on Buena Vista Avenue for a parking lot connected to an expansion of the Hobbs Cancer Center. OhioHealth describes the work as a $16.6 million investment in radiation oncology, following an $18.1 million investment in the medical office building at OhioHealth Doctors Hospital. The system says patients report that parking has not kept pace with growth, and the expansion would remove some existing spaces.\n\nNearby residents Michael and Shawnee Davis said they worry about crime, traffic, light and noise, and said OhioHealth offered them far less than market value for their home before planning around it. OhioHealth said it has hired consultants and a civil engineer and is working with the township on a path forward.\n\nTownship Administrator James Jewell said the request goes first to the zoning commission for a public hearing, set for October, then to the trustees for a final decision, and that adjustments would go to the Board of Zoning Appeals. He said the process will take months and includes several chances for public comment."
