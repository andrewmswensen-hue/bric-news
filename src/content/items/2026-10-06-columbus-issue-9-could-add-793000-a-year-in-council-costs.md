summary: "Columbus voters will decide next month on Issue 9, which would expand City Council from nine members to 13, a change council leaders say would strengthen housing production and reduce competition among neighborhoods, WSYX reported. detail: "Issue 9 would expand Columbus City Council from its current nine members, eight council members plus a president, to 13, a change current council leaders say would strengthen housing production and reduce competition among city neighborhoods for projects and resources, according to WSYX. The station examined what the expansion would cost taxpayers across three categories: salaries, support staff and security.\n\nOn salaries, Columbus council members currently earn $96,453 a year and the council president earns $116,032, a combined base of nearly $900,000. The Citizens' Commission on Elected Official Compensation recommended a 32% pay increase for council members and the president starting in 2030, plus annual cost-of-living adjustments. Applying that recommendation to a 13-member council would bring the base salary total to about $1.68 million a year, an increase of roughly $793,000, though the figures are projections and Council must still pass an ordinance to adopt any raise. WSYX has pending records requests for council staffing numbers and for how many overtime hours the officers assigned to Council's security detail logged, to determine added staff and security costs. A competing measure, Issue 8, would keep Council at nine members but elect them by ward instead of at large."
