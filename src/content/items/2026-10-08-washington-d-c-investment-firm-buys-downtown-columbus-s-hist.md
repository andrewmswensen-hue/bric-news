The sale covers the apartment building at 309 S. Fourth St. plus nearby lots used for tenant parking. Franklin County Auditor records put the building sale at $8.4 million, with founder Michael Cournoyer telling NBC4 the additional $500,000 covered the parking parcels separately.\n\nConstructed in 1902, the Mercantile originally served as an industrial building for the United States Carriage Company, a Columbus manufacturer of horse-drawn hearses and ambulances. The building was later converted to office use before its current life as apartments. Cournoyer said the building's combination of historic character and modern residences in the heart of downtown drew Rampart's interest, and the firm plans to keep it as apartments while making improvements over time rather than repositioning the asset."
