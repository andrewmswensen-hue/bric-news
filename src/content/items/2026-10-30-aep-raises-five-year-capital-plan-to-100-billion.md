detail: "American Electric Power, the Columbus-based utility that serves AEP Ohio customers, announced Oct. 28 that it is raising its five-year capital investment plan to $100 billion through 2031, according to Daily Energy Insider. The increase reflects what CEO Bill Fehrman called significant opportunities tied to rapidly growing electricity demand. AEP expects the expanded spending to support more than 13% annual growth in its rate base, the value of utility infrastructure on which regulators allow the company to earn a return, reaching roughly $162 billion by 2031. Transmission investment is set to nearly double, approaching $70 billion.\n\nOf the total plan, AEP expects to spend about $37 billion on transmission infrastructure, $36 billion on electricity generation, and $22 billion on distribution systems. The company also raised its long-term earnings growth target to 9% to 11% annually from 2027 through 2031, and issued 2027 operating earnings guidance of $6.75 to $7.05 per share. AEP said it signed agreements for 15 gigawatts of new electricity demand in the third quarter of 2026, driven in part by manufacturers and large technology companies, and expects contracted load to reach 84 gigawatts by 2031."
