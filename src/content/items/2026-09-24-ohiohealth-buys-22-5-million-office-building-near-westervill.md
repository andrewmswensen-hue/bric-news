detail: "OhioHealth's purchase of the 141,000-square-foot office building at Hamilton Quarter, near the State Route 161 and Hamilton Road corridor, closed August 16 for $22.5 million, according to Franklin County property records. The property sits next to the roughly 24-acre former Big Lots corporate campus, which OhioHealth bought for $36 million in April of last year, giving the health system control of adjoining parcels for a combined $58.5 million. OhioHealth has not announced specific plans for either property; a spokesperson said the system has no immediate use for the vacant space in the newly purchased building.\n\nA Columbus city ordinance, which had its first council reading September 14 and a second reading scheduled after, would transfer the building's existing enterprise-zone agreement from HQ Office I LLC, an affiliate of developer Daimler Group, to OhioHealth. That agreement grants a 75 percent property tax abatement on real property improvements for 10 years, approved in 2020 in connection with a $19.2 million investment commitment and 15 new jobs; it can run through 2032 if transferred. Columbus separately rezoned the former Big Lots headquarters in May to permit medical office and outpatient use."
