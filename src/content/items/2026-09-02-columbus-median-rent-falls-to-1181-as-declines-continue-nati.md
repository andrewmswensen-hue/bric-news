summary: "Realtor.com's July rent report put the Columbus metro's median asking rent at $1,181, down 1.7% from a year earlier and below the $1,187 recorded in January. The decline mirrors a national trend now in its third straight year of year-over-year drops, with the median asking rent across the 50 largest metros down 1.4% to $1,695. Realtor.com estimated the monthly cost of buying a starter home in Columbus at $2,111, versus $1,181 to rent, a gap the report attributed largely to increased multifamily construction expanding rental supply nationally."
detail: "Nationally, median rent fell across all unit sizes tracked: studios to $1,435, one-bedrooms to $1,581, and two-bedrooms to $1,893, each down roughly 1.3% to 1.4% year over year. The national median asking rent is now $69 below its 2022 peak but still 15.3% above pre-pandemic levels. Realtor.com's cost-of-buying estimate assumes a 10% down payment, the average 30-year fixed mortgage rate for the month, and metro-level averages for HOA fees, property taxes, and homeowners insurance. The report identified seven metros, not including Columbus, where home listing prices are falling faster than rents and wage growth is keeping pace, a combination it said signals conditions turning more favorable to buyers. Realtor.com economist Jiayi Xu said cheaper renting today does not necessarily mean the window to buy is closing, noting that the rent-versus-buy gap is narrowing in some markets even as it holds wide in others."
