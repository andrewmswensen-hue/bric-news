title: "Delaware City Council discusses statewide property tax removal and local levy impact"
summary: "Delaware City Council on March 23 discussed how a statewide effort to remove or cap property taxes would affect the city's existing tax levies. Council members raised questions about replacement revenue and how operations funded by current levies would continue if property tax is eliminated. No local ordinance was introduced; the conversation was tied to state-level policy proposals being discussed in the Ohio Statehouse. Similar discussions were held at the Mid-Ohio Regional Policy Roundtable earlier in the month."
why_it_matters: "Statewide property tax reform would reshape rental operating economics across all of Ohio, not just Central Ohio; even at the discussion stage, tracking how municipalities are modeling the loss of this revenue helps you anticipate whether replacement taxes (income, levy, sales) are likely to land on residential owners."
