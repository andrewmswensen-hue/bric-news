detail: "Ohio's median rent has dropped to around $1,300 a month, down nearly 3.7% from a year earlier, according to a realtor.com study reported by NBC4. Carlie Boos, executive director of the Affordable Housing Alliance of Central Ohio, said the decline shows supply and demand working normally and gives renters some leverage heading into lease renewals, with possible concessions in the range of $25 to $50 a month. She noted the relief is tied to a wave of new apartment construction completed in recent months, calling the current conditions a narrower window that could close once that supply slows. The National Housing Conference, cited in the report, found the share of renters who could afford a two-bedroom apartment fell from 77% in 2020 to 69% last year, and mortgage rates remain near their highest level since 2023, keeping many prospective buyers in the rental market longer. Boos pointed to existing rental assistance and homebuyer resources for residents still priced out despite the softer rents."
