title: "Illinois sues landlords over tenant denial based on pending felony charge"
summary: "Illinois' attorney general sued two McHenry County landlords, John and Morgan Goorsky, alleging they illegally denied a rental applicant after a background check showed pending felony charges rather than a conviction. The Illinois Department of Human Rights found substantial evidence the denial violated a 2020 state law treating certain arrests without convictions as a protected category in housing decisions. The state is seeking a $16,000 penalty, damages for the applicant, and an order requiring the landlords to adopt fair housing policies and training."
detail: "The Illinois Attorney General's Office filed a civil lawsuit against John and Morgan Goorsky, owners of a McHenry townhouse, on behalf of the state's Department of Human Rights. The suit alleges the Goorskys violated the Illinois Human Rights Act by denying a rental application from Philip Rizzo after a background check and a Google search turned up pending felony charges against him, including a theft case tied to his former role as treasurer of a youth baseball league. No court has found Rizzo guilty of those charges.\n\nIllinois' arrest-record housing protection, in effect since January 2020, defines a protected arrest record to include arrests that have not led to a conviction, and bars housing providers from denying an application on that basis alone. The law does not bar background checks generally or require approval of applicants who fail legitimate screening criteria; a separate provision still lets landlords screen for a tenant's own unlawful conduct on the property. The Illinois Department of Human Rights found substantial evidence supporting Rizzo's complaint in April 2026 and filed a formal complaint in August; the Goorskys chose to move the case to circuit court rather than continue before the Human Rights Commission.\n\nThe state is asking for a $16,000 civil penalty, damages and costs for Rizzo, and an order requiring the landlords to adopt anti-discrimination policies, complete fair housing training, and post notices explaining the law. A scheduling conference in McHenry County Circuit Court is set for Dec. 8, 2026."
