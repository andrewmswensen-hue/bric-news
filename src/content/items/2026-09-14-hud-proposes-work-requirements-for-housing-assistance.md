summary: "HUD unveiled a proposed rule Sept. 12 that would let local housing authorities impose work requirements of up to 40 hours a week and time limits of two years or more on able-bodied adults receiving federal housing assistance, including Section 8 vouchers. Seniors, children and people with disabilities are exempt. HUD Secretary Scott Turner announced the rule alongside two Illinois housing authorities that will pilot the approach as the first members of a new \"Work and Dignity Coalition.\" HUD estimates the change could add up to $51 million in participant income nationally."
detail: "The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development unveiled a proposed rule that would give local public housing authorities the option to require up to 40 hours per week of work and set time limits of two years or more for able-bodied adults receiving federal rental assistance. HUD Secretary Scott Turner and Assistant Secretary Benjamin Hobbs announced the proposal alongside the Housing Authority of Champaign County and the Vermilion Housing Authority in Illinois, the first two agencies to join HUD's new \"Work and Dignity Coalition.\" Seniors, children and people with disabilities would not be subject to the requirements. HUD cited federal data showing nearly half of non-elderly, able-bodied assisted households had no working adult in 2024, and that roughly half of able-bodied Section 8 voucher recipients have spent more than 15 years in subsidized housing. The agency estimates the change could add up to $51 million in participant income nationally. The rule is a proposal only: it must go through a federal public comment period before any housing authority can adopt it, and adoption would remain optional for each local authority rather than mandatory nationwide."
why_it_matters: "This is a proposed rule, not yet law, but if adopted it gives housing authorities like the Columbus Metropolitan Housing Authority the option to add work requirements to Section 8 vouchers, which could change how long some tenants stay eligible and how often a voucher unit turns over."
