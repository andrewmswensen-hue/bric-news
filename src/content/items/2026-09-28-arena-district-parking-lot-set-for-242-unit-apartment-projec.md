detail: "The Columbus Downtown Commission approved the design for One Twenty Vine, a 242-unit apartment project from Nationwide Realty Investors, the real estate development arm of Nationwide Insurance, on Sept. 22. The project would replace a gravel surface parking lot at 154 Vine St. in the Arena District, combining nine parcels into a single address at 120 Vine St. The two buildings would rise seven stories, with five floors of wood construction over a two-story podium, and would include a leasing office, fitness center, and resident lounge but no retail space. Plans call for 56 enclosed parking spaces on the first floor, a skywalk connection to the neighboring Kilbourne Garage, and parking for 10 bicycles. The site drops roughly one story from northeast to southwest, which the design addresses with a split-level entrance along Armstrong Street. The two buildings would share a courtyard with Two Twenty Vine, a 124-unit building Nationwide Realty opened on the adjacent parcel last year. The project continues the redevelopment of a site formerly used by building supplier Hamilton Parker, which relocated in 2000, and advances the city's downtown plan, which prioritizes replacing surface parking with housing as Columbus works toward a goal of 40,000 downtown residents by 2040. The commission's approval covers design review only; construction permits and a project timeline have not yet been set."
