title: "German Village Commission nears vote on 52-unit Cedar Square proposal"
summary: "The German Village Commission was scheduled to hold a final review on September 2 of Cedar Square, a mixed-use development proposed by Mershad Development along East Livingston Avenue, and could vote on approval. The current version calls for 52 dwelling units and a three-story residential building along East Blenkner Street, with buildings on East Livingston not exceeding 36 feet. The original concept called for 96 apartments in four stories. A petition created August 18 has collected more than 750 signatures asking the commission and the city to reject the proposal in its current form."
detail: "Mershad Development has revised the proposal several times since first bringing it to the commission in early 2025, cutting it from 96 apartments in a four-story building to 52 units in three stories.\n\nOpposition has continued through those revisions. The petition, created August 18, objects primarily to the scale of the building proposed along Blenkner Street, which organizers said would create more than 300 feet of continuous building frontage. Signers are asking that the third story be removed and that the developer further address pedestrian access, traffic, service and delivery activity, and the constrained conditions along Blenkner Street and Dixon Alley. Organizers said they support redevelopment of the property but want a project that follows the scale and development patterns of the historic district.\n\nThe application package submitted for the September 2 review shows further revisions since the commission's August 5 meeting: reduced visibility of a rooftop garden, more brick along the north and south facades, and added landscaping along Blenkner Street. Commissioners have acknowledged the changes while continuing to raise questions."
