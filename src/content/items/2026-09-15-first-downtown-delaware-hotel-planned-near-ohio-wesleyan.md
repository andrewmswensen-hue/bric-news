detail: "The city of Delaware selected Westwood Collective to develop a roughly 100-room hotel, tentatively called Hotel Wesleyan, on nearly one acre of city-owned land on Spring Street between South Franklin and South Sandusky streets, according to the Columbus Dispatch. The site is currently used mostly as surface parking and would become the first hotel in the walkable core of downtown Delaware, near Ohio Wesleyan University. City Council placed an ordinance on first reading September 14 conveying the property to the Community Improvement Corporation of Delaware County, which will handle the sale to the developer, likely below market rate, a structure the city's economic development director said is required by how state law treats municipal property transfers. The agreement includes deadlines: the developer must obtain a building permit within two years and largely complete the project within five years, or the city can reacquire the property. The project could cost close to $50 million and includes 280 parking spaces, a two-story parking structure, and two ground-floor retail tenants that have already expressed interest. The hotel could be branded through Marriott's Tribute Collection or Hilton's Tapestry Collection. It could also be the first phase of a larger mixed-use development on Spring Street, depending on additional land and city support."
