title: "Hilliard transfers 333 acres to expand Homestead Metro Park eightfold"
summary: "Hilliard City Council voted 7-0 on September 28 to transfer 333 acres of city-owned and private land to Columbus and Franklin County Metro Parks, expanding the 44-acre Homestead Metro Park nearly eightfold. The ordinance sends 69 acres of city land directly to Metro Parks, lets builder M/I Homes add land near its Hill Farm developments, and sets a trail maintenance pact. The acreage sits in the Big Darby Watershed, which Hilliard and other Central Ohio communities agreed to protect under the 2006 Big Darby Accord."
detail: "Hilliard City Council unanimously approved a single ordinance on September 28 combining three land actions to expand Homestead Metro Park from 44 acres to nearly 377 acres. The city will transfer 69 acres of city-owned land directly to Columbus and Franklin County Metro Parks, builder M/I Homes, the company behind the nearby Hill Farm and Hill Farm II developments, will transfer some of its adjoining land to the park system, and the city will enter a memorandum of understanding letting Metro Parks maintain and patrol certain trails. The park, located at 4675 Cosgray Road, currently has a pond, a train station replica, a caboose, two pavilions, and a three-quarter-mile paved trail; Columbus and Franklin County Metro Parks executive director Tim Moloney said in a September 15 video that developing wetlands, trails, and prairies on the new acreage will take a couple of years. The land sits within the 556-square-mile Big Darby Watershed, which Hilliard and other Central Ohio communities agreed to protect under the 2006 Big Darby Accord; a replacement for that 20-year-old cooperative agreement has been proposed but not yet approved by all member municipalities. The transfer follows a June council vote allowing developer Grand Communities to send about 35 acres near Clover Groff Run, just southwest of the park, into the same park system."
