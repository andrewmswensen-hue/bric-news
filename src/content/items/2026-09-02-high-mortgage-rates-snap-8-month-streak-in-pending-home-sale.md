A pending sale is one where a seller has accepted an offer but the deal has not yet closed, making it an early indicator of finalized sales a month or two later.\n\nThe slowdown tracks a steady rise in mortgage rates that began in May, when the 30-year fixed rate started climbing amid Middle East conflict driving up oil prices and inflation expectations. Freddie Mac recorded the 2026 high of 6.69% on Aug. 6, and rates stayed in that range for three weeks before closing the month at 6.66%, more than 20 basis points above early July.\n\nRealtor.com senior economist Jake Krimmel said August was when higher rates caught up with demand, noting rates crossed above year-ago levels for the first time this cycle. Regionally, the Midwest saw the steepest decline, with pending sales down 4.3% year over year, more than the West's 3.3% drop; the South and Northeast still posted gains. Price cuts also picked up, with 20.4% of active listings seeing reductions in August, a share catching up to 2025 levels."
