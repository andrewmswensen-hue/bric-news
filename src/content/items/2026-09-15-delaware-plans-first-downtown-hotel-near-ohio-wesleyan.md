detail: "The city of Delaware is planning to transfer nearly an acre of downtown land on Spring Street, currently used as surface parking, to make way for the city's first hotel in its walkable downtown core. Developer Westwood Collective, led by Delaware native and Ohio Wesleyan alumnus Andy Warnock, plans a roughly 100-room hotel, potentially branded Marriott Tribute or Hilton Tapestry Collection, with 280 parking spaces and two ground-floor retail tenants already interested, including a bowl shop and a student-run coffee shop. City council gave the transfer ordinance a first reading Sept. 14. Because of how Ohio law structures municipal property transfers, the city will convey the land to the Community Improvement Corporation of Delaware County, which will then handle selling it to the developer, likely below market rate, rather than putting it up for public auction. The deal includes deadlines: if Westwood Collective does not obtain a building permit within two years, or has not substantially completed the project within five years, the city can reacquire the property. The total project cost could approach $50 million, funded partly through alumni investment. City officials and the developer describe the hotel as a template for future infill redevelopment in downtown Delaware, with a possible second phase of additional mixed-use and residential development on the same block depending on future land availability."
why_it_matters: "This deal sets a concrete below-market land price and a hard completion deadline that other Delaware developers can point to when negotiating their own city-backed redevelopment deals downtown, and the possible second-phase residential component is worth tracking for investors watching downtown Delaware inventory."
