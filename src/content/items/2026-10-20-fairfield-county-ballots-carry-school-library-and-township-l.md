title: "Fairfield County ballots carry school, library and township levies"
summary: "Several Fairfield County communities have property or income tax levies on the Nov. 3 ballot, including a Pickerington Local Schools 0.75% income tax (after a larger 1.25% increase failed in May), a Southwest Licking Local Schools 2.87-mill bond levy for $80.7 million in school construction, and new or renewed mills for Madison Township roads, Pleasant Township fire and EMS, and the Pataskala and Pickerington public libraries. The county auditor's levy estimator lets residents calculate the cost per $100,000 of a property's appraised value for each measure."
detail: "Fairfield County voters in several communities will decide local tax levies on the Nov. 3 ballot, apart from the countywide Issue 3 on photo voter ID. Pickerington Local Schools is asking for an additional 0.75% income tax for 10 years, after a larger 1.25% increase failed in the May primary; the tax applies to those who own or rent within the district, excluding Social Security income. Southwest Licking Local Schools has two measures: replacing its existing 0.75% income tax with a 2% tax starting Jan. 1, 2027, and a 2.87-mill, 35-year property tax levy to repay an $80.7 million bond for school construction and renovation, which the county auditor estimates would cost $100 a year per $100,000 of appraised value. Madison Township is seeking an additional 1.5 mills for road and bridge work ($53 per $100,000 of value), Pleasant Township an additional 2 mills for fire and EMS services ($70 per $100,000), and both the Pataskala and Pickerington public libraries have levies on the ballot, generating an estimated $901,850 and $1.4 million annually. The Fairfield County Auditor's Levy Estimator lets residents calculate the specific cost of each measure on their own parcel."
why_it_matters: "Any of these levies that pass will show up as added mills on 2027 property tax bills for owners in those specific Fairfield County districts and townships, so investors holding rentals in Pickerington, Southwest Licking, Madison Township or Pleasant Township should check which levies apply to their parcel before budgeting next year's taxes."
