summary: "Residents of The Quarry Luxury Apartments in Columbus say maintenance problems continue months after earlier complaints about missing air conditioning. One tenant said he was told he was 80th in line for an AC repair. The leasing office said maintenance voicemails left before Sept. 9 would be deleted during a system reset. detail: "WSYX reports that dozens of residents have emailed its tip line about maintenance and other problems at The Quarry Luxury Apartments, following a July report of tenants without air conditioning. One resident who moved in this summer said he filed five requests covering a leaking dishwasher, a leaking sink, and an AC that did not cool, and that he was told he was 80th in line for AC repair.\n\nIn July, one tenant broke her lease and another placed rent in escrow and filed a complaint with the City of Columbus before the issue was fixed. A recent email to residents said voicemails left before Sept. 9 would be deleted as the property reset its maintenance system and asked residents with unresolved issues to contact the office again.\n\nThe leasing office gave no timeline and referred questions to the corporate owner, which did not respond by publication. The resident said he is considering rent escrow. The station notes tenants can report concerns through Columbus 311 and that the Legal Aid Society of Columbus provides information on tenant rights. The claims are tenant accounts and have not been independently confirmed."
