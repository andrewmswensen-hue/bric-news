title: "Grandview Heights reviews First & Grandview area parking study findings"
summary: "Grandview Heights council reviewed on April 15 the findings of the First & Grandview Plan parking study. The study assessed current parking supply, demand, and turnover in the commercial corridor and identified potential code changes to accommodate expected residential and mixed-use development. Specific recommendations included reviewing minimum parking requirements and considering shared-parking provisions. No ordinance has been introduced to implement changes yet. Staff indicated the study would inform upcoming zoning code updates."
why_it_matters: "Parking minimums are often the invisible cost driver on small multifamily and mixed-use deals; if Grandview Heights moves to relax minimums or accept shared-parking arrangements in the First & Grandview area, deal math could improve meaningfully for infill projects there."
