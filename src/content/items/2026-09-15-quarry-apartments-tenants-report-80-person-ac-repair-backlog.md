detail: "Residents at The Quarry Luxury Apartments in Columbus say maintenance problems have continued for months, according to Fox 28 Columbus, which first reported tenant complaints about a lack of air conditioning in July. Resident Luke Buzard, who moved in this summer, said he has filed five separate maintenance requests, including a leaking dishwasher, a leaking kitchen sink with visible water damage, and an air conditioning unit that has not cooled his apartment since it was turned on. Buzard said he visited the leasing office roughly twice a week and called the property's maintenance and corporate lines repeatedly without resolution, and was eventually told he was 80th in line for air conditioning repairs. He then contacted the station's tip line, which has since received complaints from dozens of additional residents describing similar issues. The new wave of complaints follows an earlier round of reporting in July, when two other residents separately raised concerns about the same air conditioning shortfall. The report does not identify the property's management company or say what is causing the repair backlog."
