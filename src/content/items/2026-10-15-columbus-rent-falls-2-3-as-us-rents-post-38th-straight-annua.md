Realtor.com attributes the national cooling to three forces: slowing new supply in Sun Belt metros such as Austin and Phoenix after a construction boom, renters relocating from expensive metros like Chicago to more affordable ones such as St. Louis and Minneapolis, and AI industry hiring pushing rents up in San Jose and San Francisco. The report says continued multifamily construction already in the pipeline should keep rents easing into 2026, but cautions it is too early to call the slowdown a rent recovery given renters' continued preference for renting over buying at current mortgage rates."
