title: "Powell Commission discusses a potential rental registration program"
summary: "The Powell Commission discussed on April 8 the outline of a potential rental registration program that would require local rental properties to register with the city, pass code inspections, and designate a local point of contact. Specific fee amounts, unit-count thresholds, and an effective date were not disclosed in the meeting packet. No ordinance has been introduced or voted on. City staff indicated further public discussion and drafting would occur in upcoming commission meetings."
why_it_matters: "Delaware County has not historically had city-level rental registration programs, so Powell joining the list would mark a meaningful shift for northern-metro landlords; tracking the draft text when it lands is the cheapest way to push back on terms that do not work for small operators."
