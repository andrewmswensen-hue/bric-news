Council members noted that density decisions in Columbus have downstream effects on surrounding suburbs, including Hilliard, through school-enrollment shifts, infrastructure capacity, and housing-market dynamics. No Hilliard ordinance was introduced on the topic; the conversation was background context for other planning discussions."
