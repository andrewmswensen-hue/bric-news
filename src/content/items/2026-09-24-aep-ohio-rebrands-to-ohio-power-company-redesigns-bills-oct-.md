title: "AEP Ohio Rebrands to Ohio Power Company, Redesigns Bills Oct. 1"
summary: "AEP Ohio will begin operating under the name Ohio Power Company starting Oct. 1, changing its branding on bills, its website and other customer communications, the company announced Sept. 24. The rename brings the utility's public name in line with its longstanding legal name. Alongside the switch, customers will start receiving a redesigned monthly electric bill approved earlier this year by the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio, intended to make generation, distribution and transmission charges clearer and to show customers their generation supplier."
detail: "AEP Ohio's parent, American Electric Power, said the rebrand to Ohio Power Company, the utility's original legal name, is meant to give that name \"greater visibility.\" Marc Reitter, president of Ohio Power Company, said the change underscores the company's commitment to the roughly 1,400 employees who live and work in the communities it serves. Customers do not need to take any action: account numbers, service terms, rates and the mobile app will all continue unchanged, and the former AEP Ohio website will redirect automatically to OhioPowerCo.com. The more consequential change is the bill redesign the PUCO approved earlier this year. The new format, which starts appearing in October, is meant to make it easier for customers to see how distribution, transmission and generation charges combine into a total bill and to identify their generation supplier, along with monthly energy-use information the company says is meant to help customers make more informed decisions about usage."
why_it_matters: "A landlord who pays AEP Ohio bills directly on vacant units or common areas will see the same charges under a new name and a new layout starting in October, so the practical task is matching last month's line items to the new format rather than expecting any change in what is owed."
