detail: "The Trump administration approved $36 million in FEMA funding for Ohio, Michigan, and Wisconsin two days after Gov. Mike DeWine requested a presidential major disaster declaration for flooding that struck 23 Ohio counties, including Licking County, from Aug. 10 to 23, the Reporting Project reported. Statewide, initial FEMA and Small Business Administration damage assessments found major damage to 806 properties and minor damage to 475, with at least 50 destroyed beyond repair. Pataskala, Heath, Buckeye Lake, and parts of Newark were among the hardest-hit areas in Licking County, according to the county's emergency management office. The funding approved so far draws on FEMA's Public Assistance, Hazard Mitigation Grant, and Building Resilient Infrastructure and Communities programs, all requested in DeWine's letter. Trump has not yet issued the major disaster declaration itself, which means DeWine's other requests, FEMA's Individual Assistance Grant Program, Transitional Sheltering Assistance, and SBA physical damage loans, remain unapproved; those programs would cover temporary housing, home repairs, and rental assistance for individual property owners. Licking County Emergency Management is hosting resource events this month where the county auditor's office will help affected residents with property tax questions, and officials are urging owners to keep receipts and repair records in case more federal assistance becomes available later."
