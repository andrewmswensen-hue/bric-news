title: "Westerville approves ADU zoning amendment for single-family districts"
summary: "Westerville Planning Commission approved on April 10 an amendment to Chapter 1183 allowing accessory dwelling units in R-1 and R-2 single-family zoning districts by right, subject to size and lot-coverage limits. Detached ADUs are capped at 800 square feet; attached ADUs at 40% of the principal dwelling's floor area. Owner-occupancy of either the principal dwelling or the ADU is required. The amendment takes effect 30 days after City Council ratification, expected May 6."
why_it_matters: "Westerville has historically been one of the tighter suburbs for ADU development, so this is a meaningful loosening for owners with deep lots; if an ADU was part of a Westerville property plan that was previously blocked, the math changes as of May."
