summary: "With Columbus's median home sale price at $350,000 last month, up from $213,500 in August 2019, a Zillow search found 224 houses listed under $150,000 in Franklin County as of Sept. 18, concentrated almost entirely in Linden, Hilltop, the South Side and pockets of the Southeast Side. detail: "Columbus's median home sale price reached $350,000 last month, up from $213,500 in August 2019, pushing traditional starter homes out of reach in most of the city. A Zillow search for Franklin County listings at $150,000 or below turned up 224 results on Sept. 18, concentrated in four areas: Linden, Hilltop, the South Side, and parts of the Southeast Side, including Eastmoor, Whitehall and Blacklick Estates. No such listings appeared in Clintonville, Upper Arlington, Hilliard, Dublin or Worthington. Typical examples include a three-bedroom, roughly 1,000-square-foot house in North Linden built in 1942, and a similarly sized, recently updated home in Hilltop listed at $145,000. Listings below $100,000 in these neighborhoods were mostly marketed as investment opportunities, in need of major repair, or available only through auction or as foreclosures. The article notes many of the cheapest available properties specifically target investors rather than owner-occupants, reflecting both their condition and the financing challenges a typical buyer would face on a home needing significant rehab work."
