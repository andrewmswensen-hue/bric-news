summary: "Ohio's Transportation Review Advisory Council released a draft list on Nov. 10 funding 13 major capacity projects statewide starting in fiscal year 2028, including four in central Ohio totaling about $15 million. They include right-of-way work for a Mink Street interchange on State Route 161 in Franklin and Licking counties, early work on the Sunbury Parkway interchange project in Delaware County, design work for State Route 315 at Lane Avenue in Columbus, and preliminary engineering for the U.S. 33 widening project in Franklin and Fairfield counties."
detail: "The Ohio Department of Transportation's Transportation Review Advisory Council (TRAC) released a draft funding list Nov. 10 covering 13 major capacity projects statewide for fiscal years 2028 and 2029, worth a combined $46.7 million. Four of the projects sit in central Ohio. The State Route 161 widening in Franklin and Licking counties would get $4 million in fiscal 2029 for right-of-way work tied to a new Mink Street interchange. The Sunbury Parkway project in Delaware County, a roughly $32 million plan to build a new interchange at Interstate 71 and U.S. 36/State Route 37 along with a connecting road, would get $3 million in fiscal 2028 for right-of-way work and project development. State Route 315 at Lane Avenue in Columbus would get $4.1 million in fiscal 2029 for detailed design. The U.S. 33 widening in Franklin and Fairfield counties would get $4 million in fiscal 2028 for preliminary engineering. TRAC, which includes ODOT's director, six gubernatorial appointees, and one appointee each from the Ohio House speaker and Senate president, funds projects that increase roadway capacity or reduce congestion."
