title: "HUD finalizes rule requiring citizenship checks in assisted housing"
summary: "HUD finalized its Verification of Eligible Status rule on Nov. 10, requiring applicants and current tenants in HUD-funded housing, including public housing and housing choice vouchers, to document citizenship or eligible immigration status. The rule also reaches tenants in mixed-status households already receiving assistance. HUD said a joint audit with the Department of Homeland Security found nearly 200,000 tenants across HUD-assisted households with incomplete or unknown eligibility verification."
detail: "The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development said Nov. 10 it has finalized its Verification of Eligible Status rule, requiring documentation of U.S. citizenship or eligible immigration status from everyone applying for or currently living in HUD-funded housing. The rule extends to \"mixed status\" households, meaning a household that includes both eligible and ineligible members under existing law.\n\nHUD said the requirement builds on steps it has taken since 2025, including a data-sharing agreement with the Department of Homeland Security to identify ineligible residents and a separate requirement that public housing authorities validate the citizenship status of all tenants or risk losing HUD funding. According to HUD, a joint audit with DHS found close to 200,000 tenants across HUD-assisted households with incomplete or unknown eligibility verification.\n\nThe rule applies broadly to HUD programs, including public housing and the Housing Choice Voucher program, commonly called Section 8, which are administered locally by public housing authorities such as the Columbus Metropolitan Housing Authority. HUD's announcement did not specify an implementation deadline."
why_it_matters: "Landlords renting to tenants on housing choice vouchers through a local public housing authority should expect new eligibility paperwork requests this cycle, since a tenant later found ineligible under the rule would lose HUD assistance, ending the portion of rent HUD pays directly to the landlord on that unit."
