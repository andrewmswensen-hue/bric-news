detail: "Dublin's Planning and Zoning Commission reviewed a concept plan Thursday for a 633-unit housing development on 137 acres at 7495 Rings Road, on the city's rural southwestern edge, and gave nonbinding feedback. The property is currently zoned rural; the applicant would need a rezoning and a preliminary development plan approved by the commission and Dublin City Council before construction could begin. The plan places 216 garden apartments and 64 cottages near roughly 6 acres of neighborhood retail, with 198 townhomes and 155 detached single-family homes farther into the site. It also sets aside 4.6 acres of public parks and plazas, 23.6 acres of native meadow and landscape buffers, and about 14 acres for stormwater management, linked by a multi-use trail. New Jersey-based Saxum Investment Company owns the property; Columbus planning firm MKSK, which designed Grandview Yard, is the applicant. The proposal has grown by 44 units since a June review, when commissioners asked for less density; nearby residents have raised concerns about traffic. Dublin planning staff said the density runs slightly higher than the city's Southwest Area Plan recommends but generally supported it because it would align with roughly 3,200 homes Columbus has already rezoned immediately to the south and west, at Jameson Estates and Hayden Place."
why_it_matters: "Investors watching Dublin's west side should note the density here, since this project would add 633 units atop the nearly 3,200 homes Columbus has already approved next door at Jameson Estates and Hayden Place, a supply wave that could soften rent growth for comparable product in that submarket over the next several years."
