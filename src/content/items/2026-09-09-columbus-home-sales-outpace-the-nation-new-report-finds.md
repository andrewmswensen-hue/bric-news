detail: "The report from Columbus Realtors shows central Ohio home sales growing well ahead of the national pace: more than 3,000 closings in July, up 6% from a year earlier, against 0.7% growth nationally. The median sale price came in around $350,000, and active listings topped 6,000, the most available at one time in the region in ten years. Carlie Boos, executive director of the Affordable Housing Alliance of Central Ohio, attributed the improvement partly to years of effort addressing the region's housing shortage, but said the underlying imbalance between buyers and available homes remains. \"We have taken on this monster, and we have tried to tame it and we're beginning to see the results,\" Boos said. She pointed to programs such as Zone In, Columbus's initiative allowing more duplexes, triplexes and senior condos in existing neighborhoods, as a way to widen the range of price points available to buyers rather than relying solely on new single-family construction."
why_it_matters: "The jump to more than 6,000 active listings, the highest in a decade, gives Columbus investors buying to hold or flip more room to negotiate than in recent years, and the Zone In rezoning push Boos cited would let owners add duplexes or triplexes on lots now limited to a single home."
