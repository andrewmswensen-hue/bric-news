title: "Franklin County Auditor opens 2026 reappraisal informal review window"
summary: "The Franklin County Auditor's office announced April 8 that informal review requests for the 2026 triennial reappraisal will be accepted through June 30. Property owners who disagree with their preliminary value can submit a review form with supporting documentation (recent appraisal, comparable sales, photos of condition issues) at franklincountyohio.gov/auditor. The office expects to issue final values in early fall, with tax impacts reflected on January 2027 bills."
