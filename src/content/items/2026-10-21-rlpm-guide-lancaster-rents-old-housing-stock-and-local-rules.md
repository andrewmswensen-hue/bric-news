detail: "RL Property Management's guide positions Lancaster, 33 miles southeast of Columbus in Fairfield County, as a lower entry alternative to the Columbus core. September 2026 listing data puts typical rent between $1,100 and $1,292 across unit types, with three bedroom rents running $1,560 to $1,780, against a roughly $1,495 Columbus citywide average. Home prices run about $235,000 in the city proper and $274,000 across ZIP 43130, compared with roughly $299,802 in Columbus. Using those medians, the guide illustrates a gross rental yield of 7.4% to 8.7% on a $1,700 a month three bedroom, though it falls short of the 1% rule at that price. About 71% of Lancaster's housing was built before 1980, with a median construction year of 1963, so the guide recommends larger maintenance reserves and flags lead paint disclosure duties for pre-1978 homes under federal rules. Because Lancaster has adopted none of Columbus's landlord specific ordinances, rules including the city's rental registry (opening for registration Oct. 1, 2026), Pay to Stay, the 60-day rent increase notice requirement and source-of-income protections stop at the Columbus line and do not reach Lancaster. Statewide landlord-tenant law under Ohio Revised Code Chapter 5321 still applies, including 24-hour entry notice and 30-day deposit return. Evictions are filed in Fairfield County Municipal Court, which charges a $140 filing fee and sets trial about 21 days after filing, a different cost and timeline than Franklin County."
