detail: "The Columbus Dispatch rounded up the local issues and referendums on November's general election ballots across Franklin County. Aside from the statewide photo ID constitutional question, Issue 3, there are no countywide issues this cycle, but several cities have multiple charter amendments on their own ballots: Columbus has two, Worthington has four, and Gahanna has ten. Grove City has one proposed amendment aimed at data centers, and Brown Township has an amendment involving a zoning plan. Beyond the charter questions, several other municipalities or public agencies have levies on the ballot, along with four school districts and a career center. Early in-person voting in Ohio began Oct. 6 and continues through Nov. 1 with varying hours, and absentee ballot applications must reach county boards of elections by close of business on Oct. 27. Absentee ballots themselves must be received by mail or returned to county boards of elections by 7:30 p.m. on Nov. 3. Polls are open 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. that day. The Dispatch noted that local liquor-option questions, which are decided only by voters in the specific precincts where the businesses applying are located, are not included in its round-up."
why_it_matters: "Property owners in Grove City and Brown Township should look closely at that city's data-center charter amendment and the township's zoning-plan amendment, since either could change what gets built nearby and shift local property values and tax bases."
