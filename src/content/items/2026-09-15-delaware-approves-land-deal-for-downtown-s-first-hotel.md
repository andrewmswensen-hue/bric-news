detail: "Delaware City Council placed an ordinance on first reading September 14 to convey nearly one acre of downtown land on Spring Street, currently used as surface parking, to the Community Improvement Corporation of Delaware County. The CIC will then sell the property, likely below market rate, to developer Westwood Collective for a roughly 100-room hotel called Hotel Wesleyan, located between South Franklin and South Sandusky streets near Ohio Wesleyan University.\n\nThe city structured the deal through the CIC because direct municipal land sales in Ohio otherwise require a public auction, according to the city's economic development director. The agreement includes deadlines: the developer must obtain a building permit within two years and substantially complete the project within five, or the city can reclaim the property. The hotel could be branded Marriott Tribute or Hilton Tapestry Collection, and the project, estimated to cost close to $50 million, includes a two-story parking structure and ground-floor retail space, with two tenants already interested. City officials describe it as a potential first phase of broader mixed-use and residential development on the same block."
