detail: "A proposed seven-story apartment and retail development near Ohio State's campus has expanded its footprint to include the building that housed PJ's Grill, a longtime restaurant at 15 E. Frambes Ave. that closed in September. The project, proposed by Texas-based developer RPM Living near the intersection of North High Street and East Woodruff Avenue, originally did not include the PJ's and neighboring Donatos Pizza buildings, but updated filings with the University Impact District Review Board now fold both into the site. The current plans call for 228 residential units totaling 866 beds, along with roughly 4,000 square feet of ground-floor retail space that includes a planned coffee and juice bar. The closure ends a restaurant students described as a campus-area fixture known for its oversized, unconventional sandwich combinations. The project has not yet received final approval from the review board, but the expanded site plan signals the scale of redevelopment underway in the blocks immediately north of campus, where several longtime small businesses have closed or relocated as larger multifamily projects move through the city's review process."
