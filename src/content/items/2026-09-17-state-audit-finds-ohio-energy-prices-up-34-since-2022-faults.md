detail: "State Auditor Keith Faber's office found that Ohio energy prices have risen 34 percent since 2022 even as utility companies continue petitioning for additional rate increases, and that the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio lacks a strategic plan to handle rising electricity demand, data center growth, and new obligations created by 2025's House Bill 15, a broad energy policy overhaul. The audit found PUCO consistently misses deadlines on rate cases and does not fully gather data on its own operations. State Rep. Adam Mathews, a Republican, said lawmakers still need to finalize data center policy and argued that companies bringing heavy power demand should cover their own infrastructure costs rather than push them onto other ratepayers. State Sen. Kent Smith, a Democrat, pointed to a sharp decline in utility-scale solar projects over the past five years, which he attributed to Statehouse Republicans' energy policy choices restricting renewable development. The audit's release follows years of scrutiny of PUCO tied to the House Bill 6 bribery scandal involving former Chair Sam Randazzo and FirstEnergy. Northeast Ohio customers have separately reported repeated blackouts from FirstEnergy, which apologized to customers in September and outlined reliability improvement plans."
