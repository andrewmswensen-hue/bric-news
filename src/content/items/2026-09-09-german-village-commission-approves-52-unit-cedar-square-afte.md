title: "German Village Commission approves 52-unit Cedar Square after year of revisions"
summary: "The German Village Commission voted 3-2 on Sept. 2 to approve Cedar Square, a mixed-use development from Mershad Development at 245-277 E. Livingston Ave. that will demolish an existing two-story brick building. The approved plan calls for 52 units, mostly in a three-story residential building along East Blenkner Street, plus a small retail component on Livingston Avenue and first-floor covered parking."
detail: "Cedar Square has been under German Village Commission review for more than a year. Mershad Development's original concept, brought to the commission in February last year, called for 96 apartments in a four-story building. After repeated feedback from commissioners and nearby residents, the developer cut the project to 52 units in a three-story building, and made further design changes ahead of the September vote, including more brick on the north and south facades, less siding, additional landscaping along Blenkner Street, and a rooftop garden redesigned to be less visible from the street.\n\nThe approved plan calls for 52 residential units, mostly in the three-story building fronting East Blenkner Street, with a small retail component along Livingston Avenue and much of the ground floor used for covered resident parking. The commission voted 3-2 to approve both the design and the demolition of the existing two-story brick building on the site."
