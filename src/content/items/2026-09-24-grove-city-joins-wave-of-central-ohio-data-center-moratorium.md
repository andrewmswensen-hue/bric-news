detail: "Grove City Council approved a year-long moratorium, a legal ban, on new data center development in June. Voters will decide in the Nov. 3 election whether to make that ban permanent. Grove City is one of more than 130 Ohio municipalities that have approved some form of data center development moratorium, a list that includes Granville, Pataskala, Sunbury and South Bloomfield. Granville, Pataskala and Sunbury have also placed items on the November ballot that would indefinitely prohibit construction of \"large\" data centers, defined as facilities with an electrical load above 25 megawatts. Opponents of data center development, including Grove City resident and congressional candidate Sam Ronan, point to the facilities' strain on local electrical grids, water and other resource consumption, and environmental impact as their main objections. The wave of local moratoriums comes as Central Ohio has become one of the country's most active data center development markets, drawing large investments from technology companies alongside the pushback now playing out at the ballot box in multiple suburbs."
