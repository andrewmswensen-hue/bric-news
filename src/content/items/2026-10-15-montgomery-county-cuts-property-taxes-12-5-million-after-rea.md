The reduction is required under Ohio House Bill 335, a state law intended to prevent sharp property tax increases in the wake of countywide reappraisals. The commission's action is expected to save residential property owners an estimated $9.5 million in total.\n\nThe reduction comes as Montgomery County finishes its 2026 property reappraisal, which produced an average 19% increase in residential property values countywide, an increase that would otherwise translate into higher tax bills. The adjusted millage rates and inside-millage changes will take effect on property owners' 2027 tax bills, so the lower rates will not show up until bills arrive next year.\n\nMontgomery County includes Dayton and sits outside BRIC's core Columbus-metro coverage area, but House Bill 335's reappraisal safeguard is a statewide law, meaning it also applies to counties such as Franklin, Delaware, and Licking as they move through their own reappraisal cycles."
