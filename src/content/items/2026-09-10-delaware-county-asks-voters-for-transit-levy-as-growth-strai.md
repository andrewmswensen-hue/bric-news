detail: "Delaware County commissioners say the county is now the 22nd fastest-growing in the country, and Delaware County Transit is asking voters to approve a 0.2% sales tax levy this November to fund service expansion, WSYX reported. The city of Delaware alone has more than 4,000 homes under construction. Commissioner Jeff Benton said keeping pace with growth means expensive road and sewer work, and pointed to a $17 million county purchase of 141 acres along Sawmill Parkway, a site JobsOhio ranks among its top five in the state, as one piece of that build-out. The Ohio Department of Transportation is separately running a corridor study for the 23.5-mile stretch of U.S. 23 between I-270 and Waldo, ranking dozens of proposed fixes, from interchanges to added lanes, into priority tiers because Benton said the road cannot handle projected traffic. A related, more contested idea, a U.S. 23/71 connector, is still in early feasibility study and has drawn opposition from farmers in Delaware, Marion, and Morrow counties over agricultural land loss. If the transit levy fails, Delaware County Transit executive director Andy Volenik said the agency will likely cut service rather than expand it; ridership has already grown 174% recently, with 25% to 30% of trips tied to jobs."
