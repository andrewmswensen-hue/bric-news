title: "Granville discusses allowing living space in accessory structures"
summary: "Granville Village Council discussed on March 18 a proposed ordinance that would allow accessory structures to contain living space, aimed at increasing housing options within the village. The discussion focused on how the change would interact with existing zoning and occupancy rules. The ordinance was not voted on at that meeting; staff indicated further drafting and public input would precede any final vote."
why_it_matters: "Granville's housing stock is constrained by a combination of historic character and tight zoning, so expanding ADU-style living space is one of the few ways inventory grows here; if you own a Granville lot with a legacy outbuilding, the final ordinance text will determine whether you have a new rentable unit or the same detached shed."
