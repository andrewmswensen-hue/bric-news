title: "Delaware County voters face first-ever transit tax, two property tax levies"
summary: "Most Delaware County voters will decide a first-ever 0.2% sales and use tax for the county's transit authority on the Nov. 3 ballot, raising the area's total sales tax to 7.20% and generating an estimated $10.5 million a year. Two countywide levies also appear: a 1.8-mill, five-year tax for developmental disabilities services ($63 per $100,000 of value) and a 0.5-mill increase to a mental health and recovery levy ($50 per $100,000)."
detail: "Delaware County voters will face a long list of local tax measures on the Nov. 3 ballot, according to the Columbus Dispatch. The Delaware County Regional Transit Authority is asking for the county's first-ever sales and use tax, a 0.2% levy for a continuing period, outside areas already served by COTA. The authority spent $6.75 million in fiscal year 2026 and ran a $2.2 million operating deficit covered by reserves; the new tax would generate an estimated $10.5 million a year for new and expanded routes in Powell, Liberty Township, Delaware city and Orange Township. Two countywide levies would fund social services: an additional 1.8-mill, five-year tax for the county's developmental disabilities board, and a renewal plus 0.5-mill increase for the Delaware-Morrow mental health and recovery board. Both carry a published cost per $100,000 of county-auditor market value. Several townships and fire districts are also seeking levy renewals or increases for roads, fire protection and parks. Separately, Sunbury voters will decide a charter amendment banning construction of data centers in the village. The measures show how multiple, overlapping local levies move through the same ballot cycle, each with its own cost per $100,000 of assessed value that a property owner can calculate directly from the county auditor's figures."
why_it_matters: "A Delaware County property owner outside the COTA service area would see area sales tax rise to 7.20% if the transit levy passes. Owners there can check their own cost by multiplying a levy's published rate, for example $63 per $100,000 of county-auditor value for the disabilities levy, against their property's valuation."
