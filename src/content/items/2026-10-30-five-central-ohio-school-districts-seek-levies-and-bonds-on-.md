title: "Five central Ohio school districts seek levies and bonds on Nov. 3 ballot"
summary: "Bexley City, Upper Arlington, South Western City, and Southwest Local schools are seeking bond issues for new construction, while Pickerington Local and Jefferson Local are seeking tax levies for operations and improvements on the Nov. 3 ballot. Bexley's $115 million middle school and renovation plan would cost $72 per $100,000 of appraised value under a 2.05-mill, 30-year bond, plus a 0.5% income tax increase starting Jan. 1, 2027. Jefferson Local's 2.55-mill, five-year levy would cost $89 per $100,000 of county auditor market value."
detail: "Multiple central Ohio school districts are asking voters to approve new taxes and bonds on the Nov. 3 ballot. Bexley City Schools is seeking a 2.05-mill, 30-year bond levy to fund a $115 million new middle school, stadium renovations at Carlton Smith Field, and other maintenance; the Franklin County Auditor's office estimates the bond would cost $72 annually per $100,000 of appraised home value. The district is separately asking voters to raise its income tax from 0.75% to 1.25% starting Jan. 1, 2027, which it projects would raise $85 million for capital improvements. Jefferson Local Schools, in Madison County, is seeking a 2.55-mill, five-year permanent improvement levy expected to generate about $800,000 annually for facility repairs, HVAC and infrastructure work, and security upgrades, at a cost of $89 per $100,000 of county auditor market value starting in 2026. Upper Arlington City, South Western City, and Southwest Local schools are also seeking construction bonds this cycle, and Pickerington Local is asking voters for a 0.75% income tax over 10 years after cutting more than $5 million from its budget when a May levy failed."
why_it_matters: "Landlords in Bexley and Franklin County districts with passing levies should expect their property tax bills, and for Bexley specifically local income tax withholding, to rise starting in the 2027 tax year, with the exact dollar impact tied to a property's appraised value."
