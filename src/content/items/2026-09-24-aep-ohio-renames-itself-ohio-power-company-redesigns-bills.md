title: "AEP Ohio Renames Itself Ohio Power Company, Redesigns Bills"
summary: "Starting Oct. 1, AEP Ohio is rebranding as Ohio Power Company across its website, bills, and communications, a change the utility says brings greater visibility to its longstanding legal name. The company says the rename will not affect customers' service, rates, account information, or how they contact the utility. Alongside the rebrand, AEP Ohio will roll out a PUCO-approved redesigned monthly bill meant to show customers their generation supplier and how distribution, transmission, and generation costs make up their total charge, plus monthly energy-use information."
detail: "AEP Ohio customers will begin seeing the name Ohio Power Company on their bills, the company's website, and other communications starting Oct. 1, according to a Sept. 24 announcement from the utility. The company said the change brings greater visibility to its longstanding legal name and will not affect customers' electric service, account information, rates, or how they contact the company; the mobile app continues to work as before. The website is moving to OhioPowerCo.com, and visitors to the former AEP Ohio site will be redirected automatically. Alongside the name change, AEP Ohio is rolling out a redesigned monthly electric bill that PUCO approved earlier this year. The utility says the new format will make it easier for customers to identify their generation supplier and see how distribution, transmission, and generation costs contribute to the total bill, along with monthly energy-use information intended to help customers track their consumption. The company, whose president is Marc Reitter, said it employs 1,400 people across the communities it serves."
