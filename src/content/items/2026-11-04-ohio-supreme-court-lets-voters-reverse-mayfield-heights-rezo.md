detail: "Mayfield Heights voters used a citizen initiative petition to undo a city council rezoning decision, the Cleveland Jewish News reported. Council adopted Ordinance 2026-12 in May, rezoning about 8 acres on Parkland Boulevard from U-7 office park zoning to a planned-unit development district after Sommers Development Group LLC proposed 61 townhomes there in October 2025. Residents opposed to the project gathered nearly 1,300 signatures on an initiative petition, forcing the rezoning onto the Nov. 3 ballot as Issue 107. The developer sued to keep it off the ballot, but the Ohio Supreme Court unanimously rejected those arguments on Oct. 19 and ordered the measure to proceed to a vote. Voters passed it 52.59% to 47.41%, reverting the parcel to its prior office-park zoning and ending, for now, the planned-unit designation the townhome project needed. The vote is one of several recent instances of Ohio voters using referendum petitions to override council-approved zoning changes, with the state's high court declining to block the practice on procedural grounds."
