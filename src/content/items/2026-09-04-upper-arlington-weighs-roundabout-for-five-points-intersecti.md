title: "Upper Arlington weighs roundabout for Five Points intersection"
summary: "Upper Arlington will present refined design concepts for the long-troubled Five Points intersection to City Council on Sept. 14, after a public meeting drew about 120 residents and a follow-up survey collected 1,475 responses favoring a roundabout. The city says feedback centered on pedestrian and cyclist safety, effects on nearby businesses, and emergency vehicle access. Detailed design is expected in 2027, with construction targeted for 2028, following the completion this fall of related improvements on Northwest Boulevard."
detail: "City officials say the intersection has struggled to handle traffic safely and efficiently even as surrounding roads, including Northwest Boulevard, have undergone significant improvements over the past year. The refined roundabout concepts follow roughly a year of community outreach, including the public meeting and survey, and are meant to address the three priorities residents raised most: pedestrian and bicycle safety, potential effects on nearby businesses, and clear access for emergency vehicles. The Sept. 14 meeting begins at 6 p.m. at the Municipal Services Center on Tremont Road, and the city says it will post the presentation and meeting video afterward on the project's webpage. No funding source or budget figure for the intersection work has been released yet; the city says detailed design is expected in 2027 ahead of construction in 2028."
why_it_matters: "For an owner of rental property near Five Points, a roundabout redesign with construction still two years out means the immediate disruption is limited to planning meetings for now, but access near the intersection could change once construction begins in 2028."
