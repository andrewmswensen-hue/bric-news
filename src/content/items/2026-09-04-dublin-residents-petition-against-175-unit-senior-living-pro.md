title: "Dublin residents petition against 175-unit senior living proposal's density"
summary: "A proposed senior living development in Dublin, from Sentry Land Company, is drawing opposition from nearby homeowners over its density and fit with the neighborhood, NBC4 (WCMH) reported. The concept, presented to Dublin's Planning and Zoning Commission last month, calls for roughly 175 senior apartments and 19 single-story villas with independent living, assisted living and memory care on 17.6 acres near Bright Road and Emerald Parkway, next to Hopewell Elementary School."
detail: "Sentry Land Company presented its latest concept plan to the Dublin Planning and Zoning Commission last month for a 17.6-acre site southwest of Bright Road and Emerald Parkway, adjacent to Hopewell Elementary School. The proposal includes roughly 175 senior apartments and 19 single-story villas, combining independent living, assisted living and memory care.\n\nCommission feedback at the concept stage is nonbinding; the project would still need to advance through rezoning and both preliminary and final development plan review before it could move forward. Dublin told NBC4 it has not yet received a formal application for the next stage, so no hearing date has been scheduled.\n\nAfter the August meeting, residents started a Change.org petition asking the city to reconsider the project's density, traffic configuration and compatibility with the surrounding neighborhood; it had collected more than 100 signatures as of the report. City staff noted in an August report that about 11.53 acres of the site carry a 'neighborhood office' future land-use designation under the city's planning framework, a classification the petitioners argue does not support a development of this scale."
why_it_matters: "The site's 'neighborhood office' land-use designation on part of the parcel means the developer needs a formal land-use change approved before rezoning can even begin, making this an early test of how much density Dublin will allow next to established single-family neighborhoods."
