summary: "Voters in Pataskala and the Village of Granville, both in Licking County, approved charter amendments banning new data center construction within city limits in the Nov. 3 election, according to unofficial results from The Reporting Project. Pataskala's ban passed 73.62% to 26.38%, and Granville's passed 82.45% to 17.55%. Both measures add a permanent prohibition section to each community's charter rather than a zoning overlay or moratorium. The votes follow several years of large-scale data center development elsewhere in Licking County."
detail: "Licking County voters in two municipalities moved to permanently restrict data center development on Nov. 3. In Pataskala, voters approved adding Section 11.09 to the city charter, prohibiting construction of a data center within city limits, by a margin of 73.62% to 26.38%, according to unofficial results reported by The Reporting Project. In the Village of Granville, voters approved a similar charter addition, Section 10.12, by 82.45% to 17.55%. Both changes amend each community's charter directly rather than relying on a zoning ordinance, making them harder to reverse through a future council vote alone. The votes follow several years of large-scale data center development in Licking County, including campuses near New Albany and Johnstown that have drawn both economic development investment and local pushback over land use, water and power demand, and truck traffic. Pataskala and Granville are both within the wider Columbus metro area. The results do not affect existing data center facilities already permitted or under construction in the county, and they apply only within each municipality's own boundaries, not countywide."
