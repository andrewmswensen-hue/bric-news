title: "HUD starts scoring NSPIRE inspection standards, ends grace period"
summary: "HUD published a notice Sept. 29 proposing revisions to how it scores physical inspections at HUD-assisted properties under the NSPIRE protocol. The notice begins scoring for newly inspected standards starting Oct. 1 and ends the grace period during which some deficiencies went unscored, giving providers less than a day's notice before the change took effect, according to LeadingAge, an aging-services trade group. The scoring change does not apply to Housing Choice Voucher or Project-Based Voucher inspections. Public comments on the proposal are due to HUD by Nov. 30."
detail: "HUD's National Standards for the Physical Inspection of Real Estate (NSPIRE) protocol sets how inspectors identify and weigh deficiencies at HUD-assisted housing, then convert them into a property's inspection score. On Sept. 29, 2026, HUD published a notice proposing three changes to the NSPIRE Scoring Notice: starting scoring for a set of newly inspected standards on Oct. 1, plus two technical corrections to how inspection sample sizes are determined. According to LeadingAge, HUD had previously agreed to delay scoring for the newly added affirmative standards, giving owners a grace period during which deficiencies still had to be fixed but were not scored against a property's rating. HUD ended that grace period for certain standards with a notice published just a day before the change took effect, a timeline LeadingAge says did not give providers adequate notice. The scoring change does not apply to inspections under the Housing Choice Voucher or Project-Based Voucher programs, and it does not change how often HUD-assisted properties are inspected. HUD is accepting public comment on the proposal through Nov. 30, 2026."
why_it_matters: "Columbus landlords with HUD-assisted properties outside the Housing Choice Voucher and Project-Based Voucher programs should check whether deficiencies that only had to be fixed under the grace period now count against their NSPIRE score, since comments on the change are due to HUD by Nov. 30."
