summary: "Grove City Council voted unanimously to place a citizen-initiated charter amendment on the Nov. 3 ballot that would require citywide votes on data centers and other large development projects. The council took the vote after rejecting a shorter, alternative version of the amendment sought by the local business community. The amendment was brought by an anti-data center group, and its outcome will determine whether future large developments in Grove City need voter approval before they can proceed."
detail: "Grove City Council's unanimous vote sends a citizen-initiated charter amendment to voters this November. The amendment, brought by a local anti-data center group, would require a citywide vote before large development projects, including data centers, could move forward in Grove City. Before putting the citizen version on the ballot, council members considered and voted down a shorter alternative amendment that business groups in the city had proposed as a competing option.\n\nThe vote follows a broader pattern across Franklin County and the wider Columbus suburbs, where several communities have faced petition drives and legal fights this year over whether residents should get a direct vote on data center projects before they are approved. If Grove City voters approve the amendment, any large development proposal in the city, not only data centers, would need to clear a citywide vote in addition to the normal zoning and permitting process."
