detail: "The Journal-News reports that Butler County Treasurer Mike McNamara collected $659.7 million in new property taxes this year compared with $664.9 million last year. The total billed was $681 million before delinquent charges. At certification, $33.9 million was outstanding and the delinquency rate was 3.14 percent, compared with 3.2 percent last year and 2.85 percent in 2023.\n\nThe drop is difficult to attribute because new construction and levies also move the totals. The paper says state lawmakers passed a $3.8 billion property tax reform package last year that phases in over several years and gives tax credits to residents of 20-mill floor school districts. Auditor Nancy Nix put those credits at about $17.5 million in Butler County. Commissioners separately rolled back part of inside millage, worth about $20 million, and targeted relief to nearly 19,000 homeowners with the Homestead exemption.\n\nNix estimates an average 20 percent value increase in the current sexennial reassessment, still being negotiated with the state. State Rep. David Thomas told the paper that capping inside millage growth at inflation and raising the owner-occupied rollback to 15.38 percent over four years will separate value increases from tax increases, according to the Journal-News."
