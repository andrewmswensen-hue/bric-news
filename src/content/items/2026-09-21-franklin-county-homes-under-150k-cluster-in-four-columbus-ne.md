detail: "The Columbus Dispatch looked at what $150,000 buys in Franklin County as the traditional starter home becomes harder to find. It reports the median Columbus sale price was $350,000 as of last month, compared with $213,500 in August 2019.\n\nA Zillow search on September 18 for houses listed at $150,000 or less in the county returned 224 results, concentrated in Linden, Hilltop, the South Side and parts of the Southeast Side. The paper found no such listings in Clintonville, Upper Arlington, Hilliard, Dublin or Worthington.\n\nIn Linden, most listings in that range are one-story homes of about 1,000 square feet built in the mid-1900s, several with unfinished basements. A handful are under $100,000, and most of those were marketed as investment opportunities or sold by auction. Hilltop has similar stock, with some houses needing extensive refurbishment or aimed at investors, and a few under $100,000 that appear move-in ready. The Dispatch cites a three-bedroom, one-bath house in South Hilltop listed at $85,000. On the South Side, lower-priced homes cluster in the Deshler Park and Driving Park areas.\n\nThe article describes what is on the market rather than sale results, and asking prices may differ from closing prices. It notes that below $150,000 the condition of the homes tends to get considerably rougher."
