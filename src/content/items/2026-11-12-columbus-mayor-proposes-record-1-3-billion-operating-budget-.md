detail: "Columbus Mayor Andrew Ginther unveiled his proposed 2027 operating budget, the city's largest ever at $1.3 billion, covering day-to-day expenses rather than capital projects, NBC4 WCMH-TV reported. Public safety is the largest line item at about $895 million, funding two new police academy classes to add up to 120 officers and three firefighter classes, one more than in 2026, to add up to 135 firefighters. The city says the added classes are meant to curb overtime spending after both the police and fire divisions exceeded their overtime budgets in 2026. Housing stability and social services would get $30.2 million under the proposal, including $13 million routed through the Community Shelter Board for emergency services for people without housing. Ginther called the plan disciplined and said it stretches city dollars without cutting core services. The proposal arrives amid tension between Ginther and City Council President Shannon Hardin, who recently entered next year's mayoral race; Hardin said in a statement that the city has lacked a clear plan to control overall spending, and council will hold hearings on the budget before any vote."
