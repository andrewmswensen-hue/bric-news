title: "MORPC lowers Central Ohio's 2050 population forecast"
summary: "The Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission (MORPC) now projects its 15-county Central Ohio region will reach 2.7 million to 3.04 million residents by 2050, down from its 2023 estimate of 3.15 million, a cut of up to 450,000 people. MORPC said the lower range reflects recent local population counts coming in below earlier projections and national birth rates that have not rebounded. The region has about 2.54 million residents today."
detail: "MORPC's new report is the agency's first to present a three-outcome range rather than a single number, reflecting what it describes as real uncertainty in the forecast. The new projection uses a dataset ending in 2024, versus 2019 data used for the 2023 estimate of 3.15 million residents by 2050. The agency expects local deaths to outpace births across the region by 2040, with migration, particularly international immigration, driving most remaining growth. Central Ohio is still projected to grow faster than the rest of Ohio and the national average, but the report flags that counties farther from Columbus, including Hocking, Ross, and Marion, are projected to lose population even under the most optimistic of the three scenarios. MORPC did not say which of the three outcomes it considers most likely."
