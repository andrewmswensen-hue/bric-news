detail: "Grove City City Council approved a year-long moratorium on data center development in June, and the question of making that ban permanent now goes to voters on November 3. The proposed ban would apply to 'large' data centers, those drawing more than 25 megawatts of electrical load. Grove City joins a list of more than 130 Ohio municipalities that have approved some form of data center moratorium, including Granville, Pataskala, Sunbury, and South Bloomfield, several of which have their own permanent-ban measures on the same ballot.\n\nOpponents cite the strain large data centers place on local electrical grids, their water consumption for cooling, and environmental impact as their central concerns; supporters of data center development in other Central Ohio communities have pointed to tax revenue and construction jobs. The moratorium wave has become a live political issue beyond the ballot measures themselves, drawing candidates in nearby congressional races into the debate over data center development and its effect on residential electric rates."
