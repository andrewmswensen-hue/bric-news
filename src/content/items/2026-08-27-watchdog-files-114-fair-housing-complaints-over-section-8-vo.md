title: "Watchdog files 114 fair housing complaints over Section 8 voucher denials"
summary: "The Housing Rights Initiative and the law firm Cohen Milstein filed complaints on July 14 alleging that Greystar, the largest apartment manager in the United States, violated state and District of Columbia fair housing laws at 114 properties. Testers posing as renters called each property and reported that housing vouchers were either refused outright or accepted only with conditions. One alleged practice was excluding voucher assistance when calculating whether an applicant met income requirements."
detail: "The complaints, submitted July 14 by the Housing Rights Initiative and the national firm Cohen Milstein, allege 114 separate violations of state and District of Columbia fair housing laws by Greystar, which manages more than 1 million apartments across the United States and about 235,000 in the jurisdictions named.\n\nAccording to the watchdog group, the claims grew out of undercover testing that began in October 2025. Testers posing as prospective renters contacted 114 Greystar properties and reported the same outcome each time: housing choice vouchers were either rejected outright, or accepted only under conditions the group says are unlawful where source-of-income protections apply.\n\nOne practice singled out in the filings is the exclusion of voucher assistance when a property evaluates whether an applicant meets a minimum income threshold. Where a landlord is required to accept vouchers, screening that counts only a tenant's own earnings can disqualify applicants whose rent would largely be paid by the program.\n\nOhio has no statewide source-of-income protection, and none of the seven jurisdictions named is in Ohio. These are administrative complaints rather than court rulings, and Greystar has not been found liable."
