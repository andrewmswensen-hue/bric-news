detail: "RL Property Management's guide explains that Blacklick and Pataskala share a commuter corridor east of I-270 near the New Albany employment cluster but are separate investment markets. Blacklick is an unincorporated community in Jefferson Township, Franklin County, and the 43004 ZIP reaches into Columbus, Reynoldsburg, Gahanna and township land. Blacklick-addressed properties are divided among five school districts: Columbus, Gahanna-Jefferson, Licking Heights, New Albany-Plain and Reynoldsburg. Pataskala is an incorporated city in Licking County with two districts inside its limits, Licking Heights and Southwest Licking.\n\nThe guide reports three-bedroom rents in Blacklick of roughly $1,945 to $2,445 in late August 2026, depending on the source, and places Blacklick in a 7 to 10 percent cap rate band in its 2026 market report, alongside Whitehall and Eastmoor. About 72 percent of housing in the 43004 ZIP was built in 2000 or later, compared with a median construction year of 1998 in Pataskala.\n\nOn compliance, Blacklick addresses inside Columbus city limits must register under City Code Chapter 4515 from Oct. 1 to Dec. 31, 2026, at $15 per unit. Township and Pataskala addresses are not covered by that registry. The guide also notes that Intel's New Albany fab is targeted for 2031 with a slower pace, and advises underwriting on current rents rather than the corridor story."
