The financing replaces the property's existing construction debt with a permanent Fannie Mae loan carrying a 10-year term, three years of interest-only payments, a 30-year amortization schedule and a 100% loan-to-cost ratio, terms the lender described as reflecting the strength of the property and borrower, and the deal also returns equity to the project's original investors.\n\nThe community, located on Glacial Lane as part of the master-planned Beulah Park development, contains 124 units split among independent living, assisted living and memory care, with amenities including a library, billiards room, fitness room, spa and on-site transportation for residents. BWE senior vice president Taylor Hawkins, who originated the deal from the firm's Cleveland office, said Fannie Mae's senior housing lending book is shrinking nationally even as demand for senior housing keeps growing with an aging population, and that the property's strong occupancy made it an attractive loan despite that pullback."
