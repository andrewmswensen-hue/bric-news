title: "OHFA approves financing for 152 affordable units in Columbus"
summary: "The Ohio Housing Finance Agency's board approved financing on November 19, 2026, for six affordable housing developments statewide, including two in Central Ohio. The Archive Columbus, developed by Roers Companies, received up to $46 million in Multifamily Housing Revenue Bonds plus a 4% Low-Income Housing Tax Credit reservation to build 152 affordable units in Columbus (Franklin County)."
detail: "OHFA's board approval covers six developments using a mix of Multifamily Housing Revenue Bonds, Housing Development Loans, Housing Development Assistance Program funding, and 2026 Low-Income Housing Tax Credit reservations. In Central Ohio, The Archive Columbus will construct 152 new affordable units in Columbus with up to $46 million in bonds and a 4% LIHTC reservation, while Homeport's Pheasant Run will rehabilitate 136 existing affordable units in Reynoldsburg using a $2.5 million loan, $3 million in HDAP funding, up to $20 million in bonds, and a 4% LIHTC reservation. Outside the region, OHFA also approved a $1.75 million loan for Bridge Creek Manor, a 65-unit senior development in Greenville (Darke County); $3.1 million in Single Family Tax Credits for 31 Habitat for Humanity townhomes in Oxford (Butler County); financing for Kentway Apartments, a 148-unit senior rehab in Kent (Portage County); and a $1.75 million loan for Riverbend Manor, a 78-unit senior development in Mount Gilead (Morrow County)."
why_it_matters: "The Archive Columbus and Pheasant Run add roughly 288 subsidized units to the Franklin County rental market over the next construction and rehab cycle, new LIHTC and bond-financed supply that will compete for the same renters an investor might otherwise serve in those submarkets."
