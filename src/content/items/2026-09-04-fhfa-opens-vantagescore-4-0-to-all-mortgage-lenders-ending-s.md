title: "FHFA opens VantageScore 4.0 to all mortgage lenders, ending single-bureau limit"
summary: "FHFA Director Bill Pulte directed Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac to let every lender use VantageScore 4.0 as an alternative to Classic FICO, expanding a rollout that had been limited to about 50 lenders. Pulte said FICO has raised its per-score price 1,800% since 2020 and criticized the credit bureaus for resisting a shift away from the tri-merge model, which pulls a report from all three bureaus on every loan. The Mortgage Bankers Association has separately pushed to let lenders pull a single credit file for borrowers with strong credit, arguing it would cut costs without adding risk."
detail: "The Federal Housing Finance Agency told Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac to approve all lenders, not a limited pilot group, to originate loans scored with VantageScore 4.0 instead of requiring Classic FICO. An analysis by Keefe, Bruyette & Woods found VantageScore's share of monthly loan volume, while still small overall, spiked to 4.4% in July, concentrated almost entirely at Rocket Mortgage and United Wholesale Mortgage. FICO issued a statement defending its FICO Score 10T model as the most predictive scoring tool available, leveraging trended and rental payment data. The Community Home Lenders of America praised the expansion as a step toward competition in a market it said FICO has long dominated. The change does not eliminate FICO scoring; it gives lenders a second option Fannie and Freddie will accept, and adoption so far remains concentrated among a handful of large lenders rather than spread across the industry."
why_it_matters: "For a Columbus investor financing a rental purchase, wider VantageScore acceptance means more lenders can now quote a loan using a scoring model that weighs rental payment history, an option that was previously available at only a small number of lenders."
