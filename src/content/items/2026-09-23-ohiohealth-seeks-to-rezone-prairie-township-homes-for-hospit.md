summary: "OhioHealth is seeking to rezone three residential lots on Buena Vista Avenue in Prairie Township for parking tied to a $16.6 million expansion of the Hobbs Cancer Center at Doctors Hospital. Longtime homeowners on the street say the health system offered below-market value for their properties and, when they declined, moved to rezone around them. detail: "OhioHealth has filed a rezoning application for three residential lots on Buena Vista Avenue in Prairie Township to build a parking lot supporting a $16.6 million radiation oncology expansion of the Hobbs Cancer Center, part of Doctors Hospital. The expansion follows a separate $18.1 million investment in the hospital's medical office building. OhioHealth said existing parking has not kept pace with patient growth, and the new lots on Buena Vista Avenue are meant to replace parking capacity lost to the expansion. Longtime residents Michael and Shawnee Davis, who have lived on the street more than 40 years, said OhioHealth's purchase offer came in well below their home's market value; when they declined, they believe the health system chose to build around them instead, which they say will lower their remaining property value and quality of life through added traffic, noise and light near their home. Prairie Township Administrator James Jewell said the rezoning must move through the zoning commission for a recommendation, then to township trustees for a final decision, with a possible further step to the Board of Zoning Appeals, a process that will take months and includes multiple opportunities for public comment. A public hearing before the zoning commission is scheduled for October. OhioHealth said it has engaged a civil engineer and consultants to fit the design into the surrounding community."
