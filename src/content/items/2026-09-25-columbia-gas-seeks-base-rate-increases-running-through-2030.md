detail: "Columbia Gas of Ohio filed a preliminary rate case proposal with the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio on Sept. 18, seeking base rate increases phased in from 2028 through 2030. Under the filing, the residential base rate would rise $14.79 to $68.05 per month in 2028, another $5.11 to $72.92 in 2029, and $4.41 more to $77.12 in 2030. The base rate applies regardless of how much gas a household actually uses, and does not include the separate costs of the gas itself or its delivery. Columbia Gas estimated a household using 10,000 cubic feet a month would pay $137.56 in 2026 under the new base rate and current gas cost estimates, up from about $122.77 today, though it cautioned gas costs can shift with global events and weather. The utility serves nearly 1.5 million customers across 61 of Ohio's 88 counties, including the Columbus area. In a statement, Columbia Gas said the increase would fund replacement of aging infrastructure, system monitoring, and emergency response capability, and noted this is its second rate request in five years after its last filing in 2021. The Office of the Ohio Consumers' Counsel said it will intervene in the case on customers' behalf. Columbia Gas must still file a full application explaining the rate math; PUCO then has 180 days to review it, including public hearings, with a decision unlikely before summer 2027."
