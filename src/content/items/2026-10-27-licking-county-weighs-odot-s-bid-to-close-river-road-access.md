detail: "The Ohio Department of Transportation has asked Licking County commissioners for a second time to close the eastbound Rt. 16 intersection with River Road south of Granville to all traffic except emergency responders, citing growing danger from increased traffic volume. Commissioners rejected an earlier, partial-closure version of the request in April, saying ODOT lacked sufficient crash data; ODOT has since compiled figures showing 14 crashes at the intersection from 2021 to 2025, none fatal but about half involving injuries, including six where motorists exiting Rt. 16 struck fixed objects like posts or guardrails. Commissioners will view the intersection and hold a public hearing at 1 p.m. on Oct. 30 at the Licking County Administration Building before a possible vote as soon as Nov. 2. Residents along River Road have asked Granville Township trustees to push for at least partial access, warning that a full closure would divert traffic onto Canyon Road and James Road, which are already busy near their intersection with Rt. 37. River Road is designated Township Road 131 and falls under the county road system, which is why commissioners, not just ODOT or the township, have decision authority. The request comes as ODOT begins a $47.2 million, three-year reconstruction of Rt. 16 through Granville."
why_it_matters: "A full closure would cut direct River Road access to Rt. 16 for homeowners and any future development along that corridor south of Granville, and the commissioners' Nov. 2 vote, following the Oct. 30 public hearing, is the point where that access gets decided one way or the other."
