detail: "Van Wert County's three commissioners, Stan Owens, Thad Lichtensteiger and Todd Wolfrum, approved a second consecutive cut to the county's inside millage in late October, according to the Van Wert independent. Inside millage is the portion of Ohio property tax that local governments and school districts can set without putting it to a public vote, unlike outside millage, which voters must approve. The latest change takes the rate from 1.8 to 1.3 mills, which commissioners estimate will return close to $600,000 to taxpayers. An earlier cut about two years ago took the rate from 2.4 to 1.8 mills and saved an estimated $400,000. Owens attributed the county's ability to cut the rate to stronger than expected sales tax revenue, saying residents have been shopping locally. Commissioners also renewed the county's homestead exemption, which reduces property tax bills for qualifying elderly and lower-income homeowners, for another year. Owens said the board would consider another reduction in 2027 if the county's tax base grows enough to cover its general fund obligations."
