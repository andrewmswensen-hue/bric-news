detail: "Under Ohio's current system, recovering a property tax overpayment is left to the discretion of each county treasurer's office, and practices vary across Ohio's 88 counties, according to Your Ohio News. House Bill 1022, introduced Oct. 5 by Reps. David Thomas (R-Jefferson) and Mark Hiner (R-Howard), would change that by requiring treasurers to issue refunds rather than simply crediting the amount toward a future tax bill.\n\nThe bill applies to overpayments of more than $25, including those that follow a successful property valuation complaint. Under current practice, a taxpayer who wins a valuation complaint typically receives an automatic credit toward the next tax bill rather than cash back. HB 1022 would let that taxpayer instead request a full refund, with applicable interest, within 60 days of the county's certification. Counties would also gain flexibility to offer installment repayment agreements for larger overpayments.\n\nThe changes would apply only to overpayments required to be refunded or credited after the bill takes effect, and nothing is retroactive. The bill has just been introduced and has not yet had a committee hearing, so its path to passage is unclear."
