detail: "A demolition application filed September 21 by S.G. Loewendick & Sons targets the former Holy Family School, a more than 45,000-square-foot, two-story building at 57 S. Grubb St. in Franklinton, according to city records. Franklin County property records show the Catholic Diocese of Columbus owns the roughly one-acre site, which sits south of West Broad Street beside State Route 315; the application does not describe any future use. Columbus Building and Zoning Services deputy director Tony Celebrezze said the city forwarded the application to the Franklinton Area Commission the day it was received; the commission has 60 days to support the request or work with the applicant on alternatives, though support from the commission could shorten that review period. If demolition goes forward, city inspectors would verify that a required share of materials was recycled and that the property is graded and seeded, a landscaping requirement waived if plans for a new building are submitted instead. The Romanesque Revival building served Holy Family Parish, sheltered Franklinton flood victims in 1913, and later housed a soup kitchen and the Jubilee Museum, which has since relocated downtown. Columbus Landmarks placed it on the city's 2020 list of most endangered sites, citing development pressure in Franklinton and surrounding vacant parcels."
