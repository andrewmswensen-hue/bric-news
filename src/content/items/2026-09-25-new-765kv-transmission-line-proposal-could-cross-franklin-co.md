detail: "PJM Interconnection, which manages the regional electric grid, selected developer Grid Growth in February to build the Grid Growth West project, a roughly 220-mile transmission expansion running from the Indiana-Ohio border to Guernsey County. The plan includes four new 765-kilovolt lines, the highest voltage class in use in North America, plus one 345-kilovolt line, built on above-ground structures 150 to 200 feet tall. The route would cross Franklin, Madison, Union, and Pickaway counties, though a final path has not been chosen.\n\nGrid Growth spokesperson Scott Blake said the project would bring construction jobs and additional local property tax revenue once the lines are in place. PJM cites nearly 4,800 megawatts of forecast electricity demand growth across the region by 2032, driven in part by data center construction, as the reason for the expansion. At a community open house in Marysville, Union County residents raised concerns that a line crossing their property could lower its value or interrupt farm operations that have been in their families for generations. Grid Growth said it is collecting community feedback alongside technical, environmental, and regulatory review to help choose a final route."
