title: "Ohio House committee hears testimony on security-deposit interest bill"
summary: "The Ohio House Financial Institutions Committee heard testimony April 7 on HB 412, which would require landlords statewide to hold tenant security deposits in interest-bearing accounts and pay accrued interest to tenants at lease end. The bill applies to leases of six months or more and to landlords holding 25 or more rental units statewide. Supporters cited similar laws in Illinois and Massachusetts. Opponents testified that small-portfolio landlords could see administrative costs outweigh interest amounts. No vote was scheduled."
why_it_matters: "HB 412 is only at hearing stage and the 25-unit threshold excludes many Ohio landlords, but the bill signals movement on a topic that has been dormant in Ohio for a long time; if you hold more than 25 units, track this one and consider how interest accrual would work operationally before it becomes a compliance question."
