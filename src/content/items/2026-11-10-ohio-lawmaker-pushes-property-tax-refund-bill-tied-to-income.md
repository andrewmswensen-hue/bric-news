detail: "State Rep. Derrick Hall, D-Akron, presented sponsor testimony Nov. 10 to the Ohio House Ways and Means Committee on House Bill 365, which he calls the Property Tax Refund Act. The bill would create a circuit breaker program, refunding homeowners or renters whose property tax bill exceeds 5% of their income. Hall said he introduced the bill because his constituents and Ohioans statewide have struggled with rising property taxes, and he described the circuit breaker approach as a \"tried and true\" method of providing targeted relief while preserving funding for schools, first responders and other local services.\n\nHall said his testimony came as the current General Assembly session was ending and the bill did not reach a vote this term. He said he plans to reintroduce the legislation when the next General Assembly convenes and continue advocating for it.\n\nThe testimony, released through the Ohio House of Representatives' website, did not detail how the refund would be administered, how a renter's tax burden would be calculated, or what would fund the refunds."
