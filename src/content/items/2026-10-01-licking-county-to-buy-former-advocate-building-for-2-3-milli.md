title: "Licking County to buy former Advocate building for $2.3 million"
summary: "Licking County commissioners voted to authorize a $2.3 million purchase of the 72,000-square-foot former Advocate newspaper building at 22 N. 1st Street in Newark, with closing expected before year's end pending inspections. The purchase follows a request from the county's two common pleas judges, who average 1,420 cases a year each, more than double the statewide average of 673, and have asked the Ohio Supreme Court for a third judgeship."
detail: "Licking County commissioners Tim Bubb and Duane Flowers voted to authorize Bubb to sign a contract purchasing the former Advocate newspaper building and adjacent parking lots at 22 N. 1st Street in Newark for $2.3 million; Commissioner Rick Black was absent. The 72,000-square-foot building, which dates to 1992 and last served as a Gannett newspaper office before USA Today Company sold it off by 2018, includes 22,000 square feet of office space and 50,000 square feet of warehouse space. The county expects to close before the end of the year, pending inspections. The purchase follows a September 25 letter from Judge Thomas Marcelain, the senior common pleas general division judge, asking the Ohio Supreme Court to approve a third general division judgeship; Marcelain said the county's two current judges average 1,420 cases a year each, more than double the statewide average of 673. Licking County's population has more than doubled to about 186,000 since the 1960s, when the county added its second general division judge. If a third judge is approved, county administrator David Edelblute and commissioners have discussed moving the juvenile and probate division into the Advocate building, which currently takes up about half the space in the 150-year-old Licking County Courthouse. Renovating the former newspaper building is expected to cost several million dollars but less than building a new courthouse, Commissioner Bubb said."
