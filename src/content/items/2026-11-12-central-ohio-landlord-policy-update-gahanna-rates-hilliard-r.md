summary: "Gahanna's City Council took up a proposed 2027 utility rate ordinance on Nov. 9 that would raise the water rate about 18%, adding roughly $12 a month for a typical household, according to RL Property Management. Hilliard's council and zoning commission began reviewing a draft rezoning of three corridors: Downtown Hilliard, the Cemetery/Leap Road gateway, and the Heritage Trail. Reynoldsburg's zoning board is raising its ADU size cap from 600 to 700 square feet. Johnstown votes Nov. 17 on a New Community Authority rule, and Canal Winchester's zoning rewrite nears commission review."
detail: "RL Property Management's weekly Central Ohio landlord policy roundup covers five jurisdictions with pending zoning and rate changes. In Gahanna, the City Council's Committee of the Whole took up Ordinance ORD-0045-2026 on Nov. 9, which would raise the water operational rate from $10.69 to $12.61 per 1,000 gallons, about 18%, plus smaller increases to sewer and refuse fees. The city says the combined change comes to about $12 a month for a household using 4,000 gallons, up from a $7.46 increase in 2026. No vote is scheduled yet, but the city asked for an emergency clause to start the new rates Jan. 1, 2027.\n\nIn Hilliard, the City Council and Planning and Zoning Commission began reviewing a draft amendment to Chapter 1116 that would create three new development districts, Downtown Hilliard, a Cemetery Road/Leap Road corridor, and the Heritage Trail, adding architectural standards tied to the city's 2023 community plan. Public open houses are planned for November and December before any vote.\n\nReynoldsburg's Planning and Zoning Board is revising its zoning code to raise the maximum accessory dwelling unit size from 600 to 700 square feet, or half the primary home's size, whichever is smaller, and to limit each lot to one ADU. The update also adds a definition of \"family\" for single-family zoning purposes.\n\nJohnstown's City Council votes Nov. 17 on an ordinance amending its New Community Authority rule, which ties a community development charge to land that annexes or rezones to denser use; unpaid charges become liens that run with the property. Canal Winchester's broader zoning code rewrite is in its final drafting phase, with Planning and Zoning Commission review expected once the first half of the draft is ready."
why_it_matters: "Owners with Gahanna rentals that include water in the lease should model an extra $12 a month per unit before setting 2027 renewal rents, and anyone with a parcel inside Hilliard's new Cemetery Road, Leap Road, or Heritage Trail district boundaries should check the draft maps before the November and December open houses, since a city-initiated rezoning can change what's buildable on the lot."
