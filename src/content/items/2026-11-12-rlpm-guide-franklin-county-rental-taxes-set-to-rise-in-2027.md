title: "RLPM guide: Franklin County rental taxes set to rise in 2027"
summary: "RL Property Management's guide says two changes are landing on the same 2027 property tax bills for Franklin County rental owners: the county's 2026 triennial value update and Ohio House Bill 186, which cuts the rollback credit on one to three family rentals from 10% to 7.5% starting with tax year 2026. Because neither change shows up in a 2026 budget, the guide recommends building next year's budget from scratch, tracked per rental unit rather than across a whole portfolio."
detail: "RL Property Management's guide to 2027 rental budgeting walks Franklin County owners through two changes landing on the same tax bill: the county's 2026 triennial value update, which the Franklin County Auditor's office set at roughly 10% higher countywide for residential property on a tentative basis, and Ohio House Bill 186, which phases down the rollback credit landlords receive on one to three family rental properties from 10% to 7.5% starting with tax year 2026. That credit reduction raises the taxable share of a rental's bill independent of whatever happened to the property's assessed value, and neither change appears in a 2026 budget built from last year's numbers, which is why the guide recommends starting the 2027 budget from scratch. The guide lays out a per-door method rather than a portfolio-wide total: starting from gross scheduled rent for each unit, then subtracting vacancy and credit loss, management and renewal fees, routine maintenance, turnover costs tied to the property's expected renewal rate, a capital reserve funded by replacement cost divided by remaining useful life, insurance, and Franklin County property taxes. It recommends tracking maintenance and turnover as separate lines, since repeated turns, each averaging $3,000 to $5,000 in the Columbus market, can add up to a line item many owners leave out of their budget entirely. For capital reserves, the guide recommends building a schedule by component, such as a roof lasting 15 to 30 years or an HVAC system lasting 10 to 25 years, dividing each item's replacement cost by its remaining useful life rather than setting aside a flat percentage of rent. The guide also flags Columbus's rental registry fee, $15 per unit per year capped at $1,500 per property, with 2027 registration due December 31, 2026, as a line item owners with Columbus doors should not forget. The guide walks through a worked example using 12 single-family rental doors in Franklin County."
why_it_matters: "A Franklin County rental owner who budgets off last year's tax line will likely be short, since the rollback credit drop to 7.5% raises the taxable share of the bill on top of the 2026 triennial update's roughly 10% countywide value increase, and neither change shows up in a 2026 budget built the old way."
