detail: "The Ohio Environmental Council held a public meeting at the Bexley Public Library to brief Central Ohio residents on plans for hyperscale data centers in the region and their effect on electric bills. OEC central Ohio regional director Annalisa Roca said Ohio's regional grid operator, PJM Interconnection, tracks 63 percent of the recent increase in per-megawatt grid costs to data center demand, and that a single hyperscale facility can draw as much power as 75,000 to 100,000 homes.\n\nRoca cited one AEP Ohio customer's bill that rose from $28.92 in summer 2025 to $269.92, then to $329.17 in 2026, as an example of the increases customers are already seeing, and said data centers are on pace to consume 11 percent of Ohio's total electricity by 2030. She linked the demand growth to new natural gas plants being built or planned around the state, including a facility in Pike County requiring 9.2 gigawatts of capacity, and said similar gas plants are being sited in neighborhoods statewide."
