detail: "Licking County posted the largest year-over-year employment increase among the nation's 376 largest counties, growing 3.6% from March 2025 to March 2026, according to a U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics report cited by the Newark Advocate. The county added about 2,700 jobs, bringing total employment to roughly 77,000, while average weekly wages rose 5.7% to $1,287. That compares with 0.1% national employment growth and a 0.2% decline statewide in Ohio over the same period. Licking County ranked second among the state's 15 largest counties for wage growth, trailing only Delaware County's 10.6% increase. County officials tied the gains to trade, transportation, and utilities hiring, along with continued investment: new manufacturing jobs at Kansai Helios in Johnstown and Westerman Inc. in Hebron, ongoing construction hiring at Intel's New Albany plant despite delays, and expansions at Prudential in Heath, Axium Packaging in Jersey Township, and Covestro in Hebron. The county is also home to dozens of data centers, with more companies pitching local governments on new facilities."
