title: "Delaware County transit levy on Nov. ballot as growth strains U.S. 23"
summary: "Delaware County Transit is asking voters in November to approve a levy expanding bus service and routes as the county, now the 22nd-fastest-growing in the U.S., adds housing and jobs faster than its roads can handle. Commissioner Jeff Benton said the county spent $17 million in June to buy 141 acres along Sawmill Parkway for a growth corridor near the airport. ODOT's U.S. 23 Corridor Action Plan is studying dozens of projects, including a proposed U.S. 23/71 connector, to handle traffic that Benton says the highway cannot currently absorb."
detail: "The city of Delaware alone has more than 4,000 homes under construction, and county officials describe the county as the 22nd-fastest-growing in the country. Commissioner Jeff Benton said keeping infrastructure ahead of that growth is expensive, citing road widening and sewer construction as ongoing costs. One focal point is the Sawmill Parkway corridor, where commissioners voted in June to spend $17 million on 141 acres that Benton said ranks among JobsOhio's top five sites for its access to the airport and major roads. Benton said U.S. 23 cannot currently handle the five to ten thousand additional cars a day that continued development would bring, which is why ODOT is running its U.S. 23 Corridor Action Plan, a study of dozens of projects along a 23.5-mile stretch between I-270 and Waldo that would replace some traffic signals with interchanges, add lanes, and restrict left turns. A separate, more preliminary proposal would build a new U.S. 23/71 connector to improve regional traffic flow. Against that backdrop, Delaware County Transit is asking voters in November to approve a levy to expand bus service and routes."
why_it_matters: "A property near the Sawmill Parkway corridor or along the U.S. 23 Corridor Action Plan's priority-tier projects sits in a stretch the county is actively spending on, which is the kind of infrastructure investment that tends to show up later in rent and resale value for Delaware County rentals near those routes."
