The account notes one caveat directly relevant to compliance: software applying a late fee automatically does not make that fee legal on its own, since fee amounts and notice requirements are still governed by each state's own lease and landlord-tenant law, so the settings still need to match what a lease and state law actually allow before they are turned on."
