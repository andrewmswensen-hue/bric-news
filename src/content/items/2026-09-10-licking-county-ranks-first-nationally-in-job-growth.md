detail: "Licking County recorded the largest year-over-year employment increase among the nation's 376 largest counties, with employment up 3.6 percent between March 2025 and March 2026, according to a U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics report cited by the Newark Advocate. The county added roughly 2,700 jobs, bringing total employment to about 77,000, while average weekly wages rose 5.7 percent to $1,287. The largest employment gains came in the trade, transportation and utilities sectors. By comparison, national employment rose just 0.1 percent over the same period, and Ohio employment statewide fell 0.2 percent. Licking County ranked second among the state's 15 largest counties for wage growth, behind Delaware County's 10.6 percent increase. County officials tied the gains to recent manufacturing investment, including new jobs at Kansai Helios in Johnstown and Westerman Inc. in Hebron, continued construction hiring at the Intel plant in New Albany despite delays, and growth at Illuminate USA in Pataskala. The county is also home to numerous data centers, with additional companies continuing to pitch new facilities to local municipalities."
