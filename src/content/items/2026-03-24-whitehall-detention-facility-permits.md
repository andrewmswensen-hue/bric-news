title: "Whitehall introduces ordinance requiring special permits for detention facilities"
summary: "Whitehall City Council held a first reading on March 24 for an ordinance that would require special permits to construct or operate detention facilities within city limits. The measure adds a new conditional use category to the city's zoning code and sets council review as the approval step. Staff cited recent development inquiries as the reason for creating a formal process. A second reading is expected at a subsequent meeting."
