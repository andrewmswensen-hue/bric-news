title: "Gahanna recommends zoning changes including setback rules for accessory structures"
summary: "Gahanna City Council recommended changes to multiple chapters of the city's zoning code on March 11, including revised setback requirements for accessory structures such as sheds, detached garages, and accessory dwelling units. The recommended changes were forwarded for a scheduled public hearing. Council expects further discussion before any final vote. The full list of affected chapters and proposed setback figures is in the meeting packet."
why_it_matters: "Accessory structure setbacks are the practical constraint on where an ADU or larger outbuilding can physically go on a Gahanna lot; the recommended figures matter if you own a corner or otherwise-irregular lot there and have been waiting for looser rules to unlock a second unit."
