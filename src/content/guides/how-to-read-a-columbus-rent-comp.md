## Why rent comps are trickier in 2026

Columbus rent growth has normalized after the pandemic-era run-up, and submarket variation is widening. A single neighborhood median can hide meaningful differences between class A newer inventory and class B/C older stock on the same street.

## What to pull

- **Three to five truly comparable units** within a half-mile, matched on bed/bath count, square footage within 10 percent, and comparable condition.
- **Days on market** for each (not just the asking rent).
- **Concessions** embedded in the listing (one month free, reduced deposit). Net effective rent is what you compare, not headline.
- **Your actual renewal history** on the subject property over the last 24 months.

## What to discount

- Listings sitting more than 45 days at their current ask.
- "Luxury" amenity packages your unit cannot match.
- Comps from buildings where the owner is clearly chasing a pro-forma rather than a market clearing price.

## Where to draw the line

A comp set that gives you a range wider than 15 percent is usually a sign the submarket is in transition and you need more data before locking in a number.
