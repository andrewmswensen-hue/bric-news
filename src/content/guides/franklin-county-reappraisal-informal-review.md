## What informal review is

Franklin County's informal review is the first and cheapest path to push back on a preliminary reappraisal value. It happens before the value is finalized and before you would need to file a formal Board of Revision complaint.

## When to use it

- You bought the property in the last 24 months for meaningfully less than the preliminary value.
- You have condition issues (roof, foundation, ongoing rehab) that are not reflected in the drive-by assessment.
- Comparable sales in your neighborhood support a lower value than the one on your notice.

## What to bring

- The preliminary value notice
- Closing disclosures or purchase contracts from recent comparable sales
- Photos of documented condition issues
- A recent independent appraisal, if you have one

## What does not help

- Telling the auditor "my neighbor's is lower" without showing the comp documentation.
- Arguments about tax burden or levies (those are separate processes).
- Arguments about the auditor's methodology in general; stick to your specific property.

## What happens next

The review office will either agree with your submission and issue a revised preliminary value, or hold firm. If they hold firm and you still disagree, the Board of Revision process is available later in the year.
