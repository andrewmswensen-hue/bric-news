Franklin County covers Columbus and most of its inner suburbs. The Auditor's office runs the three-year reappraisal cycle that drives property tax bills for roughly half of Central Ohio rental inventory. The Municipal Court handles nearly all landlord eviction filings in the metro core. County-level data and offices are where most investor due diligence and ongoing operations interact with public government.
