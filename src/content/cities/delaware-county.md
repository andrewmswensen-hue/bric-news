Delaware County sits directly north of Franklin County and has been one of the fastest-growing counties in Ohio. Growth has been concentrated around Powell, Sunbury, and the Polaris / Orange Township corridor. Rents skew higher and demand profile skews family-oriented, which tends to favor class A single-family rentals and newer multifamily.
