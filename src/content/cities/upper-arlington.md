Upper Arlington is one of the highest-priced submarkets in Franklin County, with a dominant owner-occupier profile and some of the metro's most restrictive zoning. Teardowns and renovation projects are common. Rental opportunities exist but are thin and typically come through estates or life-event sales rather than open-market listings.
