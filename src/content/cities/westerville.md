Westerville sits on the Franklin / Delaware County line and draws strong owner-occupier and family-renter demand thanks to the school district. Zoning has historically been restrictive, especially around density and ADUs, though recent amendments have loosened a few categories. Rent growth has been steady rather than spectacular, which tends to make Westerville a capital-preservation market more than a cash-flow market.
