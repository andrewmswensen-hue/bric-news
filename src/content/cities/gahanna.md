Gahanna, on the east side of Franklin County, has a long-standing mix of post-war single-family neighborhoods and newer suburban development. Proximity to John Glenn Columbus International Airport and the eastside employment corridor supports consistent rental demand. A stable tax environment and active small-business community make Gahanna friendlier than most Columbus-area cities for smaller portfolio operators.
