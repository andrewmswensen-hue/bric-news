Hilliard is one of the fastest-growing suburbs in northwest Franklin County, anchored by a well-regarded school system and a steady flow of new subdivisions. Rental inventory skews newer and oriented toward families. Recent city planning work has focused on Old Hilliard revitalization and corridor development along Cemetery Road.
