Worthington, just north of Columbus, is one of the more established inner-ring suburbs in the metro. Housing stock skews older with mature neighborhoods, strong schools, and stringent zoning and historic-district oversight. Demand is stable and owner-occupier heavy, which keeps single-family rental supply thin and pricing firm.
