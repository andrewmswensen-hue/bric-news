Grove City, in southwestern Franklin County, has been one of the metro's most consistent suburban growth stories over the past decade. Affordability relative to north-side suburbs, improving retail, and proximity to employment corridors south of downtown have supported steady rental demand. Investors often look here for entry-price single-family with room to appreciate.
