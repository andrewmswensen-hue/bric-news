Licking County, east of Franklin County, is home to the Intel semiconductor campus under construction near New Albany. That project and its supplier ecosystem are reshaping rental demand across Pataskala, Johnstown, Newark, and adjoining communities. Investor interest in Licking County has risen sharply and is expected to remain elevated through the Intel buildout and beyond.
