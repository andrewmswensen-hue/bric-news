Columbus is the core of the Central Ohio investor market and the metro's largest rental inventory by a wide margin. Property is spread across dozens of neighborhoods with meaningfully different price, rent, and regulatory dynamics. Policy changes at City Hall affect more investors and more units than any other municipality in the region, which is why Columbus policy coverage runs deepest on this site.
