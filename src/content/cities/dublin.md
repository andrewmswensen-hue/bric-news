Dublin is the largest and most active submarket in northwest Franklin County. Strong class A rental demand, restrictive zoning, and the ongoing Bridge Street district buildout make Dublin a consistently tight market for both single-family rentals and newer multifamily. Watch city council and planning commission closely, as Dublin moves meaningful development projects through formal votes rather than staff-level approvals.
