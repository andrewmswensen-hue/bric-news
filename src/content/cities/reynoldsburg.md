Reynoldsburg straddles the Franklin / Licking County line on the east side and has historically been one of the more affordable entry points for small-investor single-family rentals in the Columbus metro. Proximity to the Intel campus in Licking County has made eastern Franklin submarkets a watchlist area for investors expecting employment-driven demand growth.
