Bexley is a small, dense inner-ring suburb on the east side of Franklin County. Housing stock is largely pre-war, with a strong owner-occupier base and strong schools. Rental inventory is limited but steady, and multi-unit properties in Bexley tend to be historically significant buildings that require careful renovation planning. Zoning is strict and enforcement active.
